### Core mechanism

```text
Stress identified
    ↓
Lenders assess viability and resolution options
    ↓
Resolution plan is agreed under the applicable framework
    ↓
Terms are implemented and monitored
    ↓
Asset classification and provisioning follow the prudential rules
    ↓
Failure may move the exposure toward enforcement or insolvency
```

| Resolution tool | Core purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Restructuring | Modify contractual terms to make viable repayment possible |
| Change in ownership/control | Replace an unsustainable ownership structure where permitted |
| Additional finance | Support an implementable resolution rather than simply capitalise old arrears |
| Inter-creditor coordination | Align multiple lenders on a common plan |
| Exit/enforcement | Used when consensual resolution is not viable |

Historical schemes such as CDR, 5/25 and pandemic-era dispensations are not taught as current parallel regimes. They matter only as historical context for why RBI moved toward a more general resolution framework.

RBI consolidated entity-wise instructions in 2025. The exact current post-consolidation reference and any exposure-specific implementation period not established here remain UNVERIFIED as at 31 August 2026.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   SMA/NPA · Viability assessment
This:       Stressed-asset resolution
Downstream: Upgrade · Compromise · SARFAESI · IBC
```

### Permanent rule

Restructuring changes contractual terms; it does not automatically erase the prudential consequences of borrower stress.

### Never confuse [Resolution] and [Evergreening]

**Resolution** — a governed plan intended to restore sustainable repayment or realise value.

---

**Evergreening** — new or modified credit used mainly to conceal an existing default without genuine resolution.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Single-lender exposures
Resolution can be implemented within the lender's own governance subject to RBI rules.

#### Multi-lender exposures
Inter-creditor coordination and common implementation requirements become central.
