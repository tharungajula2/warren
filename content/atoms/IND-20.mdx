### Core mechanism

```text
Performing exposure
    ↓
Early stress may appear through SMA status
    ↓
Applicable NPA trigger is reached
    ↓
Income recognition restrictions apply
    ↓
Sub-standard, doubtful or loss classification develops
    ↓
Minimum prudential provisioning follows the applicable rulebook
```

| IRACP object | Question answered |
| :--- | :--- |
| SMA | Is the exposure showing pre-NPA repayment stress? |
| NPA recognition | Has the prudential non-performing trigger been met? |
| Asset class | How seasoned or impaired is the NPA for prudential purposes? |
| Income recognition | Can accrued income still be recognised? |
| Provision | What minimum prudential loss buffer is required? |

RBI reorganised entity-wise prudential instructions on 28 November 2025. As at 31 August 2026 commercial banks remain on the current IRACP framework, while RBI has already issued a future ECL-based prudential framework scheduled to begin on 1 April 2027. That future framework is not treated as current law in this pack.

The exact post-consolidation reference for every bank/NBFC/HFC IRACP provision is UNVERIFIED where not separately stated.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Borrower payment performance
This:       RBI prudential asset quality
Downstream: SMA · NPA · Provisioning · Resolution
```

### Never confuse [IRACP] and [Ind AS 109 ECL]

**IRACP** — RBI prudential classification, income-recognition and minimum-provisioning regime.

---

**Ind AS 109 ECL** — accounting impairment methodology owned by IMP.

### Permanent rule

IRACP status does not disappear because an accounting ECL model produces a different loss estimate.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Commercial banks
Current 2025 entity-wise IRACP Directions apply as at 31 August 2026, with prudential ECL transition scheduled for 1 April 2027.

#### NBFCs and HFCs
Apply their current entity-wise prudential Directions; exact post-consolidation references are UNVERIFIED here.
