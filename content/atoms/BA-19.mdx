### Core mechanism

Separate delivery cadence from governance completeness.

| Delivery practice | Safe when | Failure mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Incremental build | Each increment traces to approved scope | Stories become the only source of requirement truth |
| Evolving detail | Detail clarifies within authorised boundaries | Scope changes enter as backlog refinement |
| Demonstrations | Feedback is captured and routed to decisions | Demo comments are mistaken for formal approval |
| Backlog reprioritisation | Mandatory coverage remains protected | Low-visibility obligations continually slip |
| Sprint acceptance | Increment meets its agreed conditions | Local acceptance is mistaken for final business acceptance |
| Hybrid gates | Gates protect material decisions and evidence | Ceremony exists without a real decision right |

```text
Mandate -&gt; Approved scope -&gt; Delivery slices -&gt; Integrated evidence -&gt; Business acceptance
```

A user story may be useful to a delivery team, but it is not automatically the full regulatory or business obligation. The BA maintains the relationship between slices and the approved baseline so that the programme can prove completeness at the end.

"Agile" never means material decisions can remain oral. If a decision changes scope, control or acceptance, record and approve it through the same authority model used elsewhere.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Approved baseline · Traceability · Change control
This:       Iterative delivery with control invariants
Downstream: Test governance · Acceptance · Release readiness
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Delivery teams produce increments; BA maintains business completeness and decision continuity |
| Approves | Requirement and scope owners approve material changes |
| Consumes | Product, project, technology, testing and governance teams use the shared baseline |
| Independently challenges | Control functions challenge gaps in mandatory coverage or evidence |

### Permanent rule

Methodology can change the rhythm of delivery, not the existence of accountability. Every mandatory obligation still needs an owner, implementation, evidence and acceptance.

### Never confuse [Sprint acceptance] and [Business acceptance]

**Sprint acceptance** - confirms that an increment meets its local agreed conditions.

---

**Business acceptance** - authorises the delivered change for business use against the complete approved obligation and disclosed limitations.

### Permanent trap

Allowing backlog tools to become a substitute for governed scope. A backlog can organise work brilliantly while still losing the link to why the bank had to change.

No delta.
