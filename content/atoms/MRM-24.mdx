### Core mechanism

Retirement trigger → dependency/use assessment → replacement/fallback → approval → production access removed → inventory updated → evidence archived

| Question | Required answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Why retire? | Obsolescence, replacement, poor performance, business exit or regulatory change |
| Dependencies? | Models, reports, decisions, interfaces and users |
| Replacement? | New model, manual process, vendor service or none |
| Reproducibility? | Historical outputs, decisions and regulatory evidence |
| Residual access? | Read-only archive versus production execution |
| Open issues? | Transfer to replacement or close with use |
| Closure owner? | Model owner plus technology/governance functions |

Exit is incomplete while hidden spreadsheets, APIs or downstream processes still call the model; that leaves an ownerless orphan model.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Monitoring · Change decision · Replacement readiness
This:       Controlled model retirement
Downstream: Archive · Historical lineage · Replacement model · Inventory closure
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Model owner | Initiates and documents decommissioning |
| Business / technology | Removes use and manages dependencies |
| MRM function | Challenges retirement completeness |
| Records / governance | Preserves required evidence |

### Permanent rule

Retired means no longer used for live decisions; it does not mean its evidence can immediately disappear.

### Never confuse [Retirement] and [Deletion]

**Retirement** - governed removal from active use while preserving required lineage, records and historical reproducibility.

---

**Deletion** - destruction of an artefact. It may be prohibited by retention requirements and is not itself a lifecycle process.

### Permanent trap

Decommissioning an upstream model without tracing downstream consumers. A successful technical shutdown can still break reports, challenger processes or other models that depended on its output.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Exit expectation | Retention / status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US | Principles-based decommissioning. | No universal retention period; legal, recordkeeping and risk needs govern. |
| UK | Inventory/lifecycle governance through exit. | No Indian-style ten-year rule. |
| EU | Internal retirement plus CRR/ECB processes where prudential models are replaced. | Permissions/replacement arrangements may apply. |
| IN | Draft keeps decommissioned models in inventory at least ten years. | Proposed only; not in force as at August 2026. |
