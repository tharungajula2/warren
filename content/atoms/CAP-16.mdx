### Core mechanism

```text
Eligible exposure class
    ↓
Supervisory IRB permission
    ↓
Internal rating system
    ↓
Permitted PD / LGD / EAD / M estimates
    ↓
Regulatory constraints and floors
    ↓
Basel risk-weight function
    ↓
IRB RWA
```

Foundation IRB lets the bank estimate fewer inputs; Advanced IRB permits more internal parameter estimation where the exposure class and local implementation allow it. Basel finalisation restricts advanced modelling for specified portfolios to reduce unwarranted RWA variability.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Credit-risk parameters · standardised approach
This:       IRB permission and model scope
Downstream: IRB risk function · parameter floors · output floor
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Model development | Estimate permitted risk parameters |
| Credit / rating owners | Operate ratings within governed processes |
| Validation | Independently test model performance and limitations |
| Capital function | Translate approved parameters into regulatory RWA |
| Supervisor | Grant, condition, amend or withdraw permission |

### Permanent rule

IRB means permission to estimate specified inputs inside a prescribed capital framework; it does not mean permission to set capital internally.

### Never confuse [Internal model] and [IRB permission]

**Internal model** — bank-developed risk measurement tool.

---

**IRB permission** — supervisory authorisation to use specified internal estimates for regulatory capital.

### Permanent trap

Calling any PD scorecard an "IRB model" ignores the use test, governance, data, validation and supervisory approval required for regulatory use.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
**As at August 2026:** the final 2026 credit-risk Directions choose the Standardised Approach from 1 April 2027; IRB is not the new domestic commercial-bank credit-capital method.

#### UK
**As at August 2026:** Basel 3.1 IRB rules are final and commence 1 January 2027, with revised model-scope restrictions.

#### EU
CRR3 retains permission-based IRB subject to Basel-finalisation constraints and the output floor.

#### US
**As at August 2026:** the 19 March 2026 large-bank capital NPR is not finalised; its proposed expanded risk-based architecture is not a rule in force.
