### Core mechanism

| Object | What happens | What does not happen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gross carrying amount | Contractual asset remains recorded | Borrower debt is not automatically forgiven |
| Loss allowance | Expected credit loss is recognised | It is not a pool of capital |
| Net carrying amount | Asset is shown net of allowance where the framework requires | Contract is not extinguished |
| Impairment gain/loss | Change in allowance affects earnings where required | It is not the same as a cash loss |
| Write-off | Gross asset and allowance are reduced when recovery is no longer reasonably expected | Enforcement rights need not always disappear |

Performing exposure → expected loss can still exist → allowance recognised

Default → expected loss usually rises → allowance rises

No reasonable expectation of recovery → write-off

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Loan asset · Contractual cash flows · Credit risk
This:       Impairment · Loss allowance · Write-off
Downstream: Net carrying amount · Earnings · Regulatory capital interaction
```

### Permanent rule

Impairment measures expected recoverability. It does not by itself cancel the contractual claim.

### Never confuse [Loss allowance] and [Capital]

**Loss allowance** — a measured reduction in the carrying amount, or a provision for an off-balance-sheet exposure, reflecting expected credit loss.

---

**Capital** — loss-absorbing financial resource supporting the institution against losses, including unexpected loss.

They are different objects and are not substitutes.

### Never confuse [Impairment] and [Default]

**Impairment** — accounting or prudential recognition of expected credit loss.

---

**Default** — a governed credit-risk state of an obligor or facility. Impairment can exist before default.

### Permanent trap

“Performing” does not mean “zero allowance”. Expected-loss frameworks recognise credit loss before a borrower necessarily becomes delinquent or defaults.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Commercial banks currently use RBI’s IRACP provisioning regime; the separate RBI ECL framework starts on 1 April 2027. Ind AS-implementing NBFCs and ARCs apply Ind AS impairment subject to RBI prudential guidance.

#### UK (UKEB)
No accounting-mechanics delta: UK-adopted IFRS 9 applies the expected-credit-loss model.

#### EU (European Commission)
No accounting-mechanics delta: EU-adopted IFRS 9 applies, with EBA supervisory guidance for credit institutions.

#### US (FASB)
ASC 326 uses an allowance for credit losses but generally measures lifetime expected losses for in-scope amortised-cost assets from initial recognition.
