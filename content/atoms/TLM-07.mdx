A **repricing bucket** is a time interval such as overnight, one month, three months, one year or longer. A rate-sensitive asset is placed into the bucket containing its next effective repricing date; a rate-sensitive liability is treated the same way. The simple bucket gap is:

`Gap = rate-sensitive assets − rate-sensitive liabilities`

A positive gap means more assets than liabilities reprice in that bucket. Under a simplified parallel-rate assumption, rising rates can increase income faster than expense. A negative gap suggests the reverse. A **cumulative gap** sums bucket gaps up to a selected horizon.

The method is valuable because it makes concentration visible. Two banks can have the same total assets and liabilities while one has a large one-month liability reset against five-year fixed assets and the other is closely matched across buckets.

Yet the sign of a bucket gap is not an earnings forecast. Assets and liabilities may reprice by different amounts; deposit rates may respond only partially or with delay; yield-curve points may move differently; options may alter expected cash flows; new business can replace maturities. A bucket also loses information because every cash flow inside the interval is treated as though its timing were equivalent.

Gap analysis is therefore best treated as a diagnostic layer. It identifies where timing mismatches reside and supports limit setting and scenario design. More sophisticated NII and EVE models then apply scenario-specific curves, behavioural assumptions, cash-flow timing and basis relationships. RBI’s forthcoming IRRBB directions explicitly recognise gap, basis and option risks and require both internally selected and prescribed stress scenarios.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Repricing gaps locate timing mismatches; they do not by themselves capture basis, curve or option risk.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Multiplying each bucket gap by one assumed rate shock and calling the result a complete IRRBB measure.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Repricing gap" b="Liquidity gap">
**Repricing gap** — difference between assets and liabilities scheduled to reset in a time bucket.
---
**Liquidity gap** — difference between cash inflows and cash outflows over time; an item can reprice without producing cash.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Bucket definitions and regulatory templates may differ, but the analytical mechanism is universal.
</Rule>

<Recall
