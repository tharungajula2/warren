### Core mechanism

Need → due diligence → method/data/limits understood → access/change rights → independent validation → approval → monitoring/change control → continuity/exit

| Control | Core question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Provider | Can it support/govern the product? |
| Transparency | Can the bank challenge suitability? |
| Data | Relevant to bank population/use? |
| Validation access | Enough evidence for independent challenge? |
| Change notice | Will method/data/version changes be known? |
| Outcomes | Can bank experience monitor performance? |
| Concentration | Critical reliance on vendor/platform? |
| Continuity/exit | Can the bank operate, replace or migrate? |

Vendor certification or external assurance is evidence, not a substitute for the bank's own fitness-for-use judgement.

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business / model owner | Remains accountable for use and outcomes |
| Procurement / third-party risk | Supports due diligence and contracts |
| Vendor | Supplies agreed documentation and change information |
| MRM / validation | Independently challenges institution-specific use |

### Permanent rule

Outsourcing model development does not outsource model risk ownership.

### Never confuse [Vendor due diligence] and [Model validation]

**Vendor due diligence** - assesses provider capability, resilience, governance and contractual suitability.

---

**Model validation** - assesses whether the model is reliable and appropriate for the institution's intended use.

### Permanent trap

Accepting "proprietary" as a complete answer to validation questions. Where full internals cannot be disclosed, alternative evidence and stronger controls must still support a defensible use decision.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Accountability | Control / status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US | Dedicated vendor/third-party section; bank retains oversight. | May use vendor evidence, but must understand product and assess own-use performance. |
| UK | In-scope vendor/external models remain under SS1/23. | Outsourcing does not transfer MRM responsibility. |
| EU | Third-party components do not transfer prudential-model responsibility. | Institution remains responsible under CRR/ECB expectations. |
| IN | Draft expressly covers third-party models; regulated entity remains accountable. | Proposed due diligence, validation documentation/access, continuity and exit; draft as at August 2026. |
