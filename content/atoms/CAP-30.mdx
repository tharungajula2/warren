### Core mechanism

Binding position:

```text
Existing US risk-based capital rules
        +
Existing leverage requirements
        +
Existing stress capital buffer for covered large banks
        +
Existing GSIB surcharge where applicable
        ↓
Binding capital position as at August 2026
```

| NPR | Federal Reserve docket | Status as at August 2026 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Category I/II firms, significant trading activity, optional adoption | R-1887 | Proposed; comments closed 18 Jun 2026; not finalised |
| Regulatory capital and standardised RWA | R-1888 | Proposed; comments closed 18 Jun 2026; not finalised |
| GSIB surcharge and FR Y-15 | R-1889 | Proposed; comments closed 18 Jun 2026; not finalised |

The three NPRs address large-bank and market-risk capital, broader standardised capital treatment, and the GSIB surcharge respectively. None replaces current rules before finalisation and an effective or compliance date.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Basel-to-law · leverage · stress capital
This:       Current US rules versus 2026 NPRs
Downstream: Final-rule monitoring · US capital planning
```

### Permanent rule

A notice of proposed rulemaking is not a binding capital rule, even after its comment period closes.

### Never confuse [Comment period closed] and [Rule finalised]

**Comment period closed** — the scheduled public-comment window has ended.

---

**Rule finalised** — agencies have issued a final rule, normally with an effective or compliance date.

### Permanent trap

Teaching R-1887, R-1888 or R-1889 as "the US Basel rule" as at August 2026 converts unsettled proposals into law.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### US
**As at August 2026:** all three NPRs were issued 19 March 2026; comments closed 18 June 2026; none is finalised. Current binding requirements therefore remain the existing US capital, leverage, stress-capital-buffer and applicable GSIB-surcharge framework until changed by final rule.
