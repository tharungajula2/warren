### Core mechanism

#### Non-maturity deposits
NMD balance
    ↓ segment
Volatile component + stable component
    ↓                         ↓
short-tenor FTP         behavioural maturity ladder
                              ↓
                         blended FTP credit

The stable balance model says how long funding remains. The beta/lag model says how quickly the customer rate moves. A long-lived balance with a high beta supplies stable funding but less interest-rate benefit than a similarly stable deposit whose administered rate is sticky.

#### Prepayable assets
Contractual amortisation
    ↓
Scenario-dependent prepayment
    ↓
Effective cash-flow tenor
    ↓
Matched FTP charge

#### Undrawn commitments
Committed amount
    ↓
Stress drawdown / collateral / liquidity need
    ↓
Standby HQLA or funding requirement
    ↓
Contingent-liquidity FTP charge

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   NMD behaviour · commitments · matched-maturity FTP
This:       FTP where effective tenor or funding need is behavioural
Downstream: Product pricing · model reconciliation · ALCO
```

### Formula

```text
NMD FTP credit = sum of (behavioural weight_j × FTP rate_j)

Behavioural weight_j — proportion replicated to tenor j.

FTP rate_j — transfer rate for tenor j.

Longer stable weights can raise the credit, but only if the same behavioural position is genuinely transferred to treasury and governed consistently.
```

### Permanent rule

FTP, IRRBB and liquidity may use purpose-specific overlays, but their underlying behavioural story must reconcile; otherwise the bank can manufacture profit from contradictory assumptions.

### Never confuse [Stable balance] and [Sticky rate]

**Stable balance** — funding amount persists.

---

**Sticky rate** — administered price responds slowly or partially to market rates. One does not imply the other.

### Permanent trap

Giving the deposit franchise a five-year transfer credit while treasury’s risk model assumes the same balances disappear in months. The inconsistency creates internal profit without economic value.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Supervisory constraints on behavioural models can affect allowable assumptions, but the FTP logic is internal and universal.
