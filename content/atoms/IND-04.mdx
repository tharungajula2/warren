### Core mechanism

```text
NBFC registered with RBI
    ↓
Activity and balance-sheet characteristics assessed
    ↓
Base Layer or Middle Layer
    ↓
Systemic assessment may place selected NBFCs in Upper Layer
    ↓
Top Layer reserved for exceptional supervisory concern
```

| Layer | Regulatory idea | Lending consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Base | Lowest-intensity layer for eligible smaller or specified NBFCs | Lighter prudential requirements than higher layers |
| Middle | Larger, deposit-taking or specified important categories | Stronger governance and prudential requirements |
| Upper | RBI-selected systemically significant NBFCs | Enhanced bank-like prudential and supervisory expectations |
| Top | Exceptional supervisory layer | Additional restrictions can be imposed if systemic risk warrants |

The architecture matters because the same NBFC activity can face different governance, exposure, provisioning or supervisory requirements after layer classification.

RBI reorganised entity-wise NBFC instructions on 28 November 2025. The exact current post-consolidation instrument title and reference for every SBR provision is UNVERIFIED in this pack as at 31 August 2026; no unstated number is inferred from the superseded legacy text.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   NBFC perimeter · Activity type · Scale
This:       SBR layer
Downstream: Prudential intensity · Governance · Entity-specific lending rules
```

### Permanent rule

Layer is a regulatory attribute of the NBFC, not of an individual loan.

### Permanent trap

Treating Base, Middle and Upper Layer as credit ratings. They classify regulatory intensity and systemic importance, not the probability that a particular borrower defaults.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Base Layer
Lower-intensity requirements apply where the entity is eligible for Base Layer treatment.

#### Middle and Upper Layer
Additional prudential and governance obligations apply; Upper Layer placement reflects RBI's supervisory assessment.

  a="It determines how intensively RBI regulates an NBFC based on size, activity and systemic significance."
