Prior to 2019, HFCs were regulated by the National Housing Bank (NHB) under the NHB Act. The Finance (No.2) Act 2019 amended the NHB Act to shift HFC regulation to RBI.  From Aug 9, 2019, HFCs are treated as a category of NBFCs for regulatory purposes. RBI announced that it would “carry out a review” of HFC regulations and issue a revised framework in due course. Until that new RBI framework is in place, HFCs continue to comply with NHB-issued directions and returns.

Under the new SBR regime (post-2022), all HFCs are to be classified either in the Middle Layer or Upper Layer (never Base Layer). In practice, this reflects that HFCs are treated as systemically important NBFCs.  They now follow RBI’s prudential norms (e.g. capital adequacy, ALM) as per their NBFC layer, and ultimately will migrate fully to RBI’s directions (for example, RBI’s NBFC regulations including IRACP, provisioning, etc.).  The NHB itself ceased primary supervision, though it continues as the housing finance policy body.

```
HFC Regulation (2019):
      +------------------------------+
      | NHB supervises HFCs (till Aug 9, 2019) |
      +------------------------------+
                    |
         [ Finance Act 2019 ]
                    |
      +------------------------------+
      | RBI regulates HFCs (from Aug 9, 2019) |
      +------------------------------+
```

### Permanent rule

HFCs are now regulated by RBI as NBFCs.  Any housing finance company must register with RBI and follow RBI’s NBFC prudential norms once the transition is complete.

### Permanent trap

Ignoring the shift: continue treating HFC regulations as per NHB.  Post-2019, NHB guidelines apply only until RBI substitutes them.  Compliance advisors must confirm whether RBI has issued a master direction for HFCs.

### Never confuse

**Housing Finance Company (HFC)** — a financial institution primarily extending home loans. After 2019, HFCs are NBFCs regulated by RBI.

---

**Mortgage Refinance (NHB)** — NHB continues as a refinancing institution and policy authority, but **no longer supervises HFC prudentially**.

### Jurisdiction variants

**IN** — HFCs = NBFCs under RBI since 2019. There is no analogous transfer in other jurisdictions covered.
