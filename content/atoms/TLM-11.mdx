IRRBB can be hedged by changing the balance sheet or adding derivatives. Treasury may raise longer-term fixed funding against fixed-rate assets, issue floating funding where asset income floats, alter securities holdings or use interest-rate swaps and other derivatives. The objective is to change the cash-flow or value sensitivity of the consolidated banking book.

Suppose a bank holds long fixed-rate assets and is exposed to rising rates. In a stylised swap hedge, it may receive floating and pay fixed. As market rates rise, the floating receipts increase and offset part of the asset/funding mismatch. The useful hedge quantity is therefore not derivative notional alone but the sensitivity offset: tenor, curve point, index, optionality and cash-flow profile must line up sufficiently with the exposure.

**Hedge effectiveness** is economic: does the hedge offset the intended risk under relevant scenarios? **Hedge accounting** is an accounting mechanism governing when and how gains and losses from a qualifying hedge relationship are recognised. It exists partly because the accounting timing or measurement of the hedging instrument and hedged item can otherwise differ.

ALM must therefore run two conversations simultaneously. Risk management asks whether EVE, NII, basis and option exposures are reduced. Finance asks whether designation, documentation and accounting eligibility are met. Optimising the second while worsening the first is not sound hedging.

There is a critical Indian accounting constraint. RBI deferred Ind AS implementation for scheduled commercial banks until further notice in March 2019. Therefore, Indian scheduled commercial banks must not be described as applying Ind AS 109 merely because IRRBB or hedge accounting is being discussed. RBI has separately issued Ind AS implementation guidance relevant to applicable NBFCs.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Economic hedge effectiveness and hedge-accounting qualification are separate tests.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Rejecting a sound economic hedge because its accounting presentation is inconvenient, or accepting a poor risk hedge because it produces desirable accounting treatment.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Economic hedge" b="Hedge accounting">
**Economic hedge** — a position designed to offset financial risk.
---
**Hedge accounting** — accounting treatment for a qualifying designated hedge relationship; it does not itself reduce risk.
</NeverConfuse>
