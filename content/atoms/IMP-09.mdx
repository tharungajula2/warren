### Core mechanism

| Evidence of credit impairment can include | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| Significant borrower financial difficulty | Capacity to pay has weakened materially |
| Breach such as default or serious delinquency | Contractual performance has failed |
| Concession granted because of financial difficulty | Original cash flows are no longer expected |
| Probable bankruptcy or financial reorganisation | Future collection is impaired |
| Other detrimental event affecting expected cash flows | Substance governs, not one indicator |

CR-03 → default is a governed credit event; it is not identical to Stage 3.

### Flow

```text
Stage 2 or Stage 1
    ↓
Credit-impairment event
    ↓
Stage 3
    ↓
Lifetime ECL
    ↓
Interest on amortised cost for qualifying non-POCI assets

Improvement
    ↓
No longer credit-impaired?
    ↓
Then reassess whether SICR still remains
    ↓
Stage 2 or Stage 1
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Default · Delinquency · Restructuring · Financial difficulty
This:       Credit impairment · Stage 3 · Cure
Downstream: Lifetime ECL · Interest recognition · Recovery
```

### Permanent rule

Leaving Stage 3 does not automatically mean Stage 1. Credit impairment may have cured while SICR relative to origination still remains.

### Never confuse [Stage 3] and [NPA]

**Stage 3** — an IFRS 9 or similar ECL credit-impaired state.

---

**NPA** — a prudential asset-classification status defined by the applicable regulator.

They can be operationally related without being the same legal object.

### Permanent trap

Treating 90 DPD as the universal definition of Stage 3 misses credit impairment that becomes evident earlier and confuses an operational default backstop with the accounting concept.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP uses NPA, substandard, doubtful and loss classifications rather than IFRS Stage 3. RBI’s ECL framework from 1 April 2027 introduces Stage 3 while explicitly retaining NPA classification.

#### UK (UKEB)
No accounting delta from IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission)
No accounting delta from IFRS 9; prudential default remains a separate regulatory concept.

#### US (FASB)
CECL has no Stage 3 allowance category. US nonaccrual and regulatory classifications remain separate from Topic 326 measurement.

  a="No — Stage 3 requires credit-impairment evidence, not merely elevated default probability."
