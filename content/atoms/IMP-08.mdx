### Core mechanism

| Evidence | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Internal rating / PD migration | Quantifies deterioration |
| Watchlist or covenant deterioration | Borrower-specific early warning |
| Forbearance / restructuring | May indicate financial difficulty |
| Sector or macro stress | Forward-looking deterioration |
| More than 30 DPD | Rebuttable SICR presumption under IFRS 9 |
| Shared risk characteristics | Supports collective SICR assessment |
| Low credit risk at reporting date | IFRS 9 practical expedient where its conditions are met |

### Flow

```text
Forward-looking and borrower information
    ↓
Individual SICR identifiable?
    ├─ Yes → assess Stage 2
    └─ No
         ↓
Collective deterioration identifiable?
    ├─ Yes → assess collectively
    └─ No
         ↓
Past-due backstop
         ↓
More than 30 DPD → SICR presumed unless supported rebuttal
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Risk grades · DPD · Watchlists · Macroeconomic information
This:       SICR evidence · Backstops · Collective assessment
Downstream: Stage 2 population · Lifetime ECL
```

### Permanent rule

A backstop catches deterioration that earlier information failed to identify. It does not replace earlier information.

### Never confuse [30-DPD presumption] and [Stage 2 definition]

**30-DPD presumption** — a rebuttable backstop.

---

**Stage 2 definition** — significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition.

### Permanent trap

Waiting until day 31 before considering Stage 2 turns an early-recognition framework into a delinquency framework.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
For Ind AS-implementing NBFCs, RBI reinforces the more-than-30-DPD rebuttable presumption and requires that recognition of SICR not be deferred beyond 60 days past due. RBI’s commercial-bank ECL framework from 1 April 2027 also contains SICR backstops.

#### UK (UKEB)
No accounting delta from IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
No accounting delta; EBA guidance reinforces timely, forward-looking and collective assessment.

#### US (FASB)
No SICR or 30-DPD staging presumption exists under CECL, although delinquency remains relevant credit information.

  q="Is more than 30 DPD the definition of SICR?"
  a="No — it is a rebuttable backstop, while SICR is significant deterioration since initial recognition."

  a="Portfolio deterioration may be visible before borrower-specific evidence emerges."
