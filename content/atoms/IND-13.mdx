### Core mechanism

DLG cap = 5% of amount disbursed out of the fixed DLG set

DLG set = the identifiable portfolio specified upfront for the guarantee arrangement.

Amount disbursed = the amount actually disbursed from that fixed portfolio at the relevant point under the Direction.

```text
Fixed DLG set specified upfront
    ↓
Loans are disbursed from that set
    ↓
Maximum DLG amount is determined from the prescribed 5% basis
    ↓
Loss event may trigger invocation under the contract
    ↓
RE still applies normal asset classification and provisioning rules
```

| DLG does | DLG does not |
| :--- | :--- |
| Provides contractual first-loss-style credit support | Replace underwriting |
| Reduces the lender's realised loss when validly invoked | Change the borrower into a non-defaulting borrower |
| Operates against a fixed identifiable portfolio | Apply a separate Rs 5 crore ceiling |
| Is subject to eligibility, form and disclosure conditions | Make NPA recognition disappear |

The legacy expression "FLDG / First Loss Default Guarantee" is not used here because RBI's current Directions use DLG.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Digital lending partnership · Underwriting
This:       DLG credit support
Downstream: Loss allocation · Provisioning · Partner risk
```

### Permanent rule

DLG support and borrower asset classification are separate. A guaranteed default can still be an NPA.

### Never confuse [DLG] and [Securitisation]

**DLG** — contractual loss support on a fixed loan portfolio.

---

**Securitisation** — transfer of credit exposures into a structured asset-transfer framework governed by separate RBI rules.

### Jurisdiction variants

No within-India delta in the core DLG cap basis under the Digital Lending Directions; eligibility and prudential effects still depend on the participating regulated entity.
