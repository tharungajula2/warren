### Core mechanism

| Lever | Changes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Change asset mix | asset repricing and duration |
| Change funding tenor/rate basis | liability repricing and duration |
| Interest-rate swap | fixed/floating sensitivity without replacing principal funding |
| Options / optional hedges | non-linear sensitivity where justified |
| Securities repositioning | EVE, NII and liquidity simultaneously |

Stylised swap logic for a bank exposed to rising funding rates against fixed-rate assets:

Receive floating / pay fixed swap
    ↓
Floating receipts rise when reference rates rise
    ↓
Offsets part of rising floating funding cost

But the hedge can leave basis risk if the funding index differs, and it can become imperfect if NMD beta or loan prepayment changes.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   IRRBB scenarios · duration · behavioural options
This:       Transfer or reshape structural rate sensitivity
Downstream: Structural FX · liquidity/funding side effects · ALCO
```

### Ownership

| Role | Owns |
| :--- | :--- |
| ALM / treasury | hedge design and execution proposal |
| Markets execution | transaction execution where separately organised |
| ALCO / delegated authority | position and hedge decisions |
| Independent risk | limit and effectiveness challenge |
| Finance | accounting treatment and P&amp;L/OCI consequences |

### Permanent rule

Hedge the sensitivity you own, not the notional you can see.

### Never confuse [Economic hedge] and [Hedge accounting]

**Economic hedge** — changes the bank’s financial risk under relevant scenarios.

---

**Hedge accounting** — governs accounting recognition for a qualifying relationship; it does not itself reduce risk.

### Permanent trap

Optimising the accounting presentation first and calling the resulting transaction a good hedge. The risk question is whether ΔNII/EVE and residual basis/option exposure improve.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in economic mechanism. Accounting eligibility and prudential recognition differ by jurisdiction and reporting framework, so this atom does not re-teach those accounting rules.
