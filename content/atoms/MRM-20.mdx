### Core mechanism

```text
Validation opinion + business need + residual risk
    ↓
Approval authority
    ↓
Approve · Approve with conditions · Reject
    ↓
Documented scope and conditions
    ↓
Controlled deployment
```

| Approval record | Required content |
| :--- | :--- |
| Model / version | Exact object authorised |
| Intended use | Permitted decision and population |
| Validation status | Findings and limitations considered |
| Conditions | Limits, overlays, monitoring or remediation |
| Exceptions | Rationale and compensating controls |
| Authority | Person or committee with delegated power |
| Duration | Expiry or review point where conditional |
| Escalation | What triggers restriction or withdrawal |

Urgent need may justify controlled exceptional use in some frameworks, but the logic remains the same: uncertainty is made explicit and compensated by stronger controls rather than silently ignored.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Validation opinion · Business case · Residual model risk
This:       Model approval and conditions
Downstream: Deployment · Use · Monitoring · Remediation
```

### Permanent rule

Approval authorises a specific model version for a specific use under stated conditions. It is not a permanent certificate of general correctness.

### Never confuse [Conditional approval] and [Validation waiver]

**Conditional approval** - governance accepts limited residual risk subject to explicit restrictions, remediation or enhanced monitoring.

---

**Validation waiver** - absence of required independent challenge. These are not equivalent; conditional use should not erase the need for validation where the framework requires it.

### Permanent trap

Treating executive urgency as evidence that model risk is acceptable. Urgency affects the risk decision and controls; it does not improve model reliability.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Approval / use | Exception / status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US | Validation generally before first use. | Urgent early use may proceed with limits, stakeholder awareness and closer monitoring. |
| UK | Proportionate governance; clear approval/escalation. | Validation and senior accountability remain distinct. |
| EU | Internal approval plus CRR controls. | Use/material changes may need supervisory permission/notification. |
| IN | Draft: formal approval, documented exceptions, stronger high-risk approval including RMCB. | Draft as at August 2026; not binding. |
