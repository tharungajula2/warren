### Core mechanism

Use three distinct objects.

| Object | Meaning | Minimum content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Assumption | A proposition temporarily treated as true | Statement · impact if false · owner · resolution date |
| Open question | Information or decision still required | Question · decision authority · due date · dependency |
| Decision | An authorised choice that constrains future work | Decision · authority · date · rationale · consequences |

```text
Unknown -&gt; Assumption or open question -&gt; Owner -&gt; Decision -&gt; Requirement or scope updated
```

A decision log is not meeting minutes. Minutes record discussion; the log records the outcome that delivery must rely on. Capture enough rationale to distinguish a conscious trade-off from an accidental omission.

When a decision changes, do not overwrite history. Record the superseding decision and identify what earlier requirement, design, test or plan is affected. This makes traceability survive scope change.

Time matters. An unanswered question needed before design freeze is different from one needed before go-live. The due date should be the latest safe decision point, not the next convenient committee meeting.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Elicitation · Governance · Requirements · Constraints
This:       Assumptions · Open questions · Decisions
Downstream: Sign-off · Traceability · Design · Change control
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA records assumptions, open questions and durable decision entries |
| Approves | Named decision authority approves each material decision |
| Consumes | Delivery, testing, governance and operations rely on the current decision state |
| Independently challenges | Control functions challenge assumptions or decisions within their mandate |

### Permanent rule

Material uncertainty must have a visible state: unresolved, assumed, decided or superseded. It must never be allowed to look accidentally final.

### Never confuse [Decision log] and [Meeting minutes]

**Decision log** - the durable record of authorised choices that constrain delivery.

---

**Meeting minutes** - a record of discussion, actions and events in a meeting.

### Permanent trap

Marking an issue "closed" because it was discussed. Closure requires either an authoritative answer or an accepted assumption with explicit consequences.
