### Core mechanism

| Situation | Credit interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Healthy commercial amendment | Terms change for ordinary business reasons; distress not implied |
| Concession because of financial difficulty | Forbearance / distressed modification analysis required |
| Material forgiveness or postponement | Test whether diminished financial obligation indicates UTP/default |
| New contract after old arrears are capitalised | Preserve pre-modification history; do not reset the borrower to “new” |
| Repeated restructuring | Escalating evidence that apparent cure may be unstable |

The key question is **why terms changed and what risk the concession reveals**. Extending maturity may restore short-term affordability; capitalising arrears can clean DPD without improving cash generation; principal forgiveness changes expected recovery.

### Flow

```text
Original terms → Financial difficulty → Concession / restructuring
    ↓
UTP / default test
    ↓
Revised cash flows → Performance monitoring
    ↓
Cure / probation OR re-default
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Deterioration · Missed/expected missed payments · Negotiation
This:       Modify terms → Was concession driven by financial difficulty? → Default/forbearance test
Downstream: Revised cash flows · Default status · Cure/probation · LGD and recovery history
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Workout / relationship team documents distress, proposal and economics |
| Approves | Restructuring authority / credit committee approves within policy |
| Consumes | Operations, finance, default management, modelling and reporting |
| Challenges | Independent credit risk and audit test classification and economic substance |

### Permanent rule

A new contractual schedule must not erase the borrower’s prior distress, default date or economic concession.

### Never confuse [Restructuring] and [Forbearance]

**Restructuring** — a modification of contractual credit terms.

---

**Forbearance** — a concession linked to the debtor’s financial difficulty; not every restructuring is forbearance.

### Permanent trap

Evergreening converts unpaid amounts into a formally current contract while leaving repayment capacity unchanged; operational current status is not proof of economic cure.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Commercial banks apply the IRACP Directions together with the RBI *Commercial Banks – Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions, 2025* where relevant; restructuring classification and provisioning must follow those rules rather than a generic Basel label.

#### UK (PRA)
Distressed restructuring can be an UTP indicator under the definition-of-default framework; forbearance and regulatory default remain distinct classifications.

#### EU
CRR/EBA rules distinguish forbearance, non-performing exposure and default. The May 2026 EBA amendment retains the 1% NPV-loss threshold in the relevant distressed-restructuring guidance and becomes applicable on 19 October 2026.

#### US
Accounting modification, troubled-credit treatment and regulatory default are separate determinations; Advanced Approaches default remains governed by 12 CFR Part 217.
