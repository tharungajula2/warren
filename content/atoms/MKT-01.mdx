## Parametric VaR Definition

<Formula math="VaR_{\alpha} = -\mu + \sigma \cdot Z_{\alpha}" />

<NeverConfuse a="Value at Risk (VaR)" b="Expected Shortfall (ES)">
Calculates loss threshold at confidence level alpha. Ignores severity of losses beyond alpha threshold.

---

Calculates expected loss conditional on exceeding VaR threshold. Subadditive coherent risk measure.
