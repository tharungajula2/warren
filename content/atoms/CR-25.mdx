### Core mechanism

| Layer | Question | Main drivers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Expected loss | What loss is anticipated on average? | PD, LGD, EAD and their aligned conditioning |
| Loss volatility | How much can realised loss vary? | Granularity, parameter variation, migration |
| Tail / unexpected loss | How severe can adverse loss become above the mean? | Concentration, correlation, systematic stress |
| Capital | What loss-absorbing resources protect solvency? | Prudential/economic confidence standard and capital rules |

Two portfolios can have identical EL but very different tail risk. One may contain thousands of diversified exposures; another may depend on a few correlated names. Their average loss can match while their severe-loss quantiles do not.

### Flow

```text
Exposure losses
    ↓
Expected loss = mean
    ↓
Concentration + correlation + systematic stress
    ↓
Loss distribution widens
    ↓
Tail above EL = unexpected loss
    ↓
Capital
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Exposure-level EL · Concentration · Correlation · Stress
This:       Build portfolio loss distribution → identify adverse tail above EL
Downstream: Regulatory/economic capital · Risk appetite · Pricing return-on-capital · Limits
```

### Formula

```text
Conceptual decomposition:

`Unexpected loss ≈ high loss quantile − expected loss`

This is a mental model, not a universal regulatory capital formula. Basel IRB risk-weight functions incorporate prescribed dependence, confidence and other parameters rather than applying this subtraction mechanically.
```

### Permanent rule

Expected loss and capital answer different questions: average credit cost versus protection against adverse loss beyond that average.

### Never confuse [Expected loss] and [Capital]

**Expected loss** — probability-weighted average credit loss for the defined measurement.

---

**Capital** — loss-absorbing resources held under a regulatory or internal solvency framework; it is not simply `PD × LGD × EAD`.

### Permanent trap

Calling exposure-level EL “capital” ignores diversification, correlation, concentration and the shape of the portfolio loss distribution.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta in the conceptual split.** Regulatory capital formulas, buffers, Pillar 2 practices and accounting/provision regimes vary materially across jurisdictions.
