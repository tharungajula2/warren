### Core mechanism

| Statement / component | Owns | Time basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Balance sheet / statement of financial position | Assets · Liabilities · Equity | At a date |
| Profit and loss / income statement | Recognised income · Expenses | Over a period |
| Cash-flow statement | Cash receipts · Cash payments | Over a period |
| Changes in equity | Movements among equity components | Over a period |

“Three statements” is practitioner shorthand, not a claim that a complete statutory set contains only three documents.

### Flow

```text
Opening balance sheet
    ↓
Transactions and economic events
    ↓
Profit / loss + Other recognised movements + Cash flows
    ↓
Asset / liability / equity changes
    ↓
Closing balance sheet
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   FND-01 Balance sheet · FND-04 Earnings
This:       Position · Performance · Cash · Equity linkage
Downstream: Accruals · Provisions · OCI · Balance-sheet analysis
```

### Permanent rule

Profit is not cash, and a cash movement is not automatically income or expense. Recognition and cash timing are separate questions.

### Never confuse [Profit] and [Cash flow]

**Profit** — recognised income minus recognised expenses for a period.

---

**Cash flow** — cash received or paid; accruals, non-cash items, financing, and investing transactions make it differ from profit.

### Permanent trap

Forcing the change in cash to equal profit. Borrowing can increase cash and liabilities without income; impairment can reduce profit without an equal current-period cash payment.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Reporting boundary |
| :--- | :--- |
| IN | Commercial banks follow the Banking Regulation Act framework and RBI financial-statement directions; Ind AS should not be assumed for scheduled commercial banks. |
| UK | Entities within scope use the applicable UK-adopted accounting framework, including UK-adopted international accounting standards where relevant. |
| EU | Accounts within the IAS Regulation's scope use EU-adopted international accounting standards. |
| US | Entities subject to US GAAP use the FASB Accounting Standards Codification under their applicable reporting requirements. |

<Recall
  q="Which statement is a snapshot?"
  a="The balance sheet, because it reports recognised position at a specified date."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can borrowing increase cash without increasing profit?"
  a="Borrowing increases an asset and a liability rather than creating income."
/>

<Recall
  q="Does “three statements” mean a complete statutory set literally contains only three statements?"
  a="No. Complete reporting sets can also include changes in equity, notes, and other required information."
