### Core mechanism

Testing needs governed handoffs, not only execution.

| Handoff | BA question | Owner outside BA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Requirement to test design | Is the current requirement unambiguous and approved? | Test owner designs coverage |
| Business rule to expected result | Who can state the correct business outcome? | SME or requirement owner supplies truth |
| Data need to test data | What business scenario must be represented? | Data/test team prepares controlled data |
| Test result to requirement | Does the evidence address the current approved requirement? | Test owner records result |
| Evidence gap to governance | What acceptance decision is blocked by missing evidence? | Decision authority resolves or accepts consequence |

DAT owns what a test strategy, test pack and UAT evidence set must contain for data-control purposes. BA carries a one-line reminder of those objects and focuses on readiness, ownership, traceability and decision consequence.

A requirement is not test-ready because it has been signed off if key rules remain assumed. Conversely, testing can reveal a requirement ambiguity rather than a software defect. Classify that outcome correctly before raising a defect or changing scope.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Approved requirements · Traceability · Delivery increments
This:       Test entry · Business truth · Evidence handoffs · Escalation
Downstream: Defect decisions · Business acceptance · Readiness
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Test owners produce test artefacts; BA produces requirement clarification and evidence linkage |
| Approves | Test governance approves test readiness and completion; requirement owners approve business meaning |
| Consumes | Business acceptance and release governance consume the evidence |
| Independently challenges | Assurance or control functions challenge evidence sufficiency where mandated |

### Permanent rule

Test evidence is useful only against the current approved requirement. Passing obsolete requirements is not evidence that the bank is ready to accept the change.

### Never confuse [Test evidence] and [Acceptance decision]

**Test evidence** - demonstrates observed results against defined conditions.

---

**Acceptance decision** - an authorised business judgement that the delivered change is suitable for use given the evidence and known limitations.

### Permanent trap

Letting testers decide ambiguous business rules because execution needs an answer. That converts a test assumption into an undocumented requirement decision.
