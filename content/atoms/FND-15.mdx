### Core mechanism



### Formula

```text
E[X] = sum of (p_i × x_i) across all outcomes i
E[X] = expected value of X
p_i = probability assigned to outcome i
x_i = numerical value of outcome i

0 &lt;= p_i &lt;= 1
p_i = a valid probability

sum of p_i across all mutually exclusive exhaustive outcomes = 1
1 = total probability mass

Expected value is a probability-weighted mean, not a promise that the realised outcome will equal the mean.
```

### Flow

```text
Define possible outcomes
    ↓
Assign probabilities
    ↓
Multiply each outcome by its probability
    ↓
Sum weighted outcomes
    ↓
Expected value
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   None
This:       Probability · Probability-weighted average
Downstream: Variance · Quantiles · Expected loss · Scenario weighting
```

### Permanent rule

An expected value summarises a distribution. It does not identify which outcome will occur next.

### Never confuse [Probability] and [Expected amount]

**Probability** — likelihood assigned to an event.

---

**Expected amount** — probability-weighted numerical outcome.

### Permanent trap

Reading a 1% chance of losing 100 as “the loss is 1%”. Likelihood and loss amount have different units and answer different questions.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Probability and expected-value mathematics are universal.
