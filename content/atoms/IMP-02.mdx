### Core mechanism

| Framework | Initial performing exposure | Deteriorated exposure | Credit-impaired/defaulted exposure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| IFRS 9 | Stage 1: 12-month ECL | Stage 2: lifetime ECL after SICR | Stage 3: lifetime ECL |
| US CECL | Lifetime expected loss | Re-estimated lifetime loss | Re-estimated lifetime loss |
| RBI commercial banks: current | IRACP classification and prescribed provisioning | IRACP rules | NPA classification and prescribed provisioning |
| RBI commercial banks: from 1 Apr 2027 | Stage 1: 12-month ECL, subject to prudential floors | Stage 2: lifetime ECL | Stage 3: lifetime ECL, with NPA rules retained |
| Ind AS-implementing NBFCs / ARCs | Ind AS 109 ECL | Ind AS 109 ECL | Ind AS 109 ECL, plus RBI prudential safeguards |

### Flow

```text
Economic credit risk
    ↓
Applicable accounting / prudential framework
    ↓
Recognition horizon
    ↓
Measurement method
    ↓
Loss allowance / provision
    ↓
Earnings and capital interaction
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Accounting framework · Prudential framework · Entity type
This:       IFRS 9 · CECL · RBI IRACP / RBI ECL
Downstream: Staging · Measurement horizon · Provision · Capital effects
```

### Permanent rule

Similar terminology does not make two frameworks equivalent. Recognition horizon, staging, scope and prudential floors can create materially different allowances on the same economics.

### Never confuse [Accounting standard] and [Prudential provisioning regime]

**Accounting standard** — determines financial-statement recognition and measurement.

---

**Prudential provisioning regime** — regulatory requirements designed for safety and soundness, which may prescribe floors or classifications beyond accounting measurement.

### Permanent trap

Calling CECL “IFRS 9 without stage labels” misses the central difference: CECL generally recognises lifetime expected loss from initial recognition instead of waiting for SICR.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Three tracks coexist: current bank IRACP, Ind AS for applicable NBFCs/ARCs, and the final RBI bank ECL framework effective 1 April 2027.

#### UK (UKEB)
UK-adopted IFRS 9 supplies the accounting architecture.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
EU-adopted IFRS 9 supplies accounting measurement; EBA guidance overlays supervisory expectations.

#### US (FASB)
ASC 326 CECL uses lifetime expected-loss recognition for in-scope amortised-cost assets without an IFRS 9 SICR gateway.
