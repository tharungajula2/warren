### Core mechanism

Conceptual transfer rate:

FTP tenor rate ≈ reference term rate + bank funding/liquidity premium

Then add a distinct contingent-liquidity charge where the product can generate stressed cash needs.

| Product | FTP mapping |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bullet five-year loan | five-year effective funding point |
| Amortising loan | weighted cash-flow strip / blended tenor |
| Short trade asset | short point consistent with effective life |
| Term deposit | credit for effective stable funding supplied |
| NMD | behavioural replication, not contractual overnight rate |

Matched-maturity does **not** mean treasury externally issues a bond for each loan. It is an internal pricing representation while actual funding is optimised at portfolio level.

### Flow

```text
Product cash flows
    ↓
Effective funding tenor
    ↓
Marginal FTP curve point / strip
    ↓
Transfer charge or credit
    ↓
Business margin
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   FTP purpose and incentives · funding strategy
This:       Tenor-sensitive marginal internal funding price
Downstream: Behavioural/contingent FTP · product pricing · ALCO
```

### Formula

```text
Business asset spread = customer yield − matched-maturity FTP charge

Business deposit spread = matched-maturity FTP credit − customer deposit rate

Effective tenor ↑ → FTP generally reflects the economics of committing funding for longer, rather than automatically staying at the overnight rate.
```

### Permanent rule

Funding cost follows effective cash-flow tenor, not the accounting period in which product profit is reported.

### Never confuse [Matched-maturity FTP] and [Matched external funding]

**Matched-maturity FTP** — internal pricing against comparable tenor economics.

---

**Matched external funding** — actually borrowing externally with cash flows mirroring an asset; this is not required transaction by transaction.

### Permanent trap

Using average historical funding cost because it reconciles neatly to the general ledger. Average cost can make today’s incremental asset decision look profitable even when replacing the funding would be much more expensive.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Curve construction is institution-specific; the economic principle is universal.
