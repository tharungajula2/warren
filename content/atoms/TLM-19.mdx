### Core mechanism

LCR = stock of HQLA / total net cash outflows over 30 days

Basel minimum in ordinary conditions = 100%. The stock is a buffer: Basel permits its use in stress, including a ratio below 100%, subject to supervisory assessment.

Liabilities / commitments
    ↓ runoff and drawdown factors
Stressed outflows

Receivables
    ↓ inflow factors and caps
Recognised inflows

Stressed outflows − recognised inflows
    ↓
Net cash outflows

HQLA after haircuts/caps
    ↓
LCR

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   HQLA inventory · liquidity ladder · commitments
This:       Standardised 30-day regulatory liquidity coverage
Downstream: NSFR · internal stress · jurisdiction calibration
```

### Formula

```text
LCR = HQLA / 30-day net cash outflows

HQLA ↑ → LCR ↑.

Runoff/drawdown factors ↑ → net outflows ↑ → LCR ↓.
```

### Permanent rule

LCR is a standardised 30-day stress ratio; internal liquidity risk remains broader in horizon, currency, entity and scenario.

### Never confuse [LCR] and [Survival horizon]

**LCR** — regulatory HQLA divided by standardised 30-day net outflows.

---

**Survival horizon** — first point an institution-specific stress exhausts usable resources.

### Permanent trap

Reading LCR 120% as “36 days of survival”. The numerator and denominator are regulatory constructs, not a linear clock.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Final amendments dated 21 April 2025 became effective **1 April 2026**: IMB-enabled stable retail deposits receive 7.5% runoff and IMB-enabled less-stable retail deposits 12.5%, with revised treatment for specified deposits and government-security HQLA valuation.

#### UK (PRA)
UK LCR sits within PRA liquidity and ILAAP/Pillar 2 supervision; current SS24/15 has applied since **20 January 2026**.

#### EU
EU LCR is binding under CRR and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/61, as amended.

#### US
As at August 2026, 2019 tailoring remains the scope architecture: Categories I–II full LCR; Category III full or 85% depending on weighted short-term wholesale funding; some Category IV firms 70%, others no LCR. The agencies rescinded public FAQs on **10 February 2026** but said the rule itself was unchanged.
