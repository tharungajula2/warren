The **Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process (ILAAP)** is a structured assessment of whether an institution has adequate liquidity and funding resources relative to its risks. A credible assessment integrates risk appetite, cash-flow mismatch analysis, stress tests, liquid-asset capacity, funding concentrations, intraday risk, contingency planning and governance. It is broader than proving compliance with LCR and NSFR.

The key practitioner point is linguistic as much as technical. In UK prudential supervision, ILAAP is an explicit formal term. The PRA’s current SS24/15 states that its liquidity and funding supervisory approach covers the Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process and the Liquidity Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process. The version confirmed final in January 2026 took effect on 20 January 2026.

The term is also embedded in EU supervisory vocabulary; EBA has issued guidance specifically concerning ICAAP and ILAAP information.

That does **not** mean every jurisdiction calls its internal liquidity assessment an ILAAP. Indian banks are subject to RBI liquidity standards, ALM, LCR, NSFR and supervisory risk-management requirements, but practitioners should not import a UK/EU label into an RBI rule unless the RBI text itself uses it. Likewise, the US uses formal liquidity-risk-management, stress-testing, liquidity-buffer and contingency-funding constructs. Regulation YY, for example, prescribes liquidity-risk management and CFP requirements without framing them as an EU-style ILAAP.

For cross-border roles, the right translation is functional rather than lexical: ask which local process performs internal liquidity adequacy assessment, not whether every regulator uses the same acronym.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
ILAAP describes a formal UK/EU supervisory construct; internal liquidity adequacy is a universal problem, but the acronym is not a universal regulatory label.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Writing “the bank submits its ILAAP” in an India or US procedure merely because that is what the equivalent process is called in a UK or EU entity.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="ILAAP" b="Liquidity-risk framework">
**ILAAP** — the formal Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process terminology used in UK/EU prudential supervision.
---
**Liquidity-risk framework** — the broader universal set of risk governance, stress, buffers and contingency processes, whatever the local regulator calls it.
</NeverConfuse>
