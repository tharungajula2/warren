### Core mechanism

| Layer | Owns the question | Core objects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Credit object | Who owes what? | Obligor · Facility · Account · Exposure · Connected group |
| Credit decision | Should this risk be taken and on what terms? | Policy · Repayment capacity · Approval · Mitigants |
| Credit state | Has contractual credit performance failed under the governed rule? | DPD · UTP · Default · Restructuring · Cure · Write-off |
| Risk parameters | How likely, how severe, how much exposed? | PD · LGD · EAD |
| Expected loss | What is the average loss cost? | PD × LGD × EAD under aligned definitions |
| Risk measurement system | How is risk ranked and observed through time? | Retail pools · Scores · Bureau data · Ratings · Migration · Vintages |
| Portfolio tail | What can fail together? | Concentration · Correlation · Systematic risk |
| Solvency protection | What absorbs severe loss above the mean? | Unexpected loss · Capital |

### Flow

```text
Obligor + Facility + Exposure
    ↓
Underwriting: repayment capacity first
    ↓
Collateral / guarantees as secondary mitigants
    ↓
Performing → Deterioration → Default → Cure / Re-default / Recovery
    ↓
PD + LGD + EAD
    ↓
Expected loss
    ↓
Concentration + Correlation + Systematic stress
    ↓
Unexpected loss
    ↓
Capital
```

### Permanent rule

Keep each layer’s question separate, then connect the outputs deliberately. A precise answer at the wrong layer is still the wrong risk measure.

### Never confuse [Expected loss capacity] and [Capital]

**Expected loss capacity** — pricing, provisions/allowances or other mechanisms that recognise and absorb anticipated credit cost under the applicable framework.

---
**Capital** — resources protecting solvency against adverse outcomes beyond ordinary expected loss, under the applicable capital regime.

### Permanent trap

A credit pack becomes incoherent when the same label is used for several layers—for example “risk” meaning score, PD, EL, provision and capital depending on the page. Always name the object, state and measure.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The architecture is universal. Jurisdictions differ in prudential definitions, accounting treatment, regulatory formulas, thresholds and legal enforceability, but those variants sit inside the same object → state → parameter → portfolio-loss chain.

  q="What comes immediately before collateral in the architecture?"
  a="Underwriting of sustainable primary repayment capacity."

<Recall
