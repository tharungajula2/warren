### Core mechanism

| Role | Produces | Approves / decides | Consumes | Challenges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Data owner | Business decisions on the data domain | Definition, required quality, priorities and risk acceptance | Quality and issue information | Producer and steward performance |
| Data steward | Metadata, monitoring and remediation coordination | Operational changes within delegated authority | DQ results and issue evidence | Day-to-day adherence to standards |
| Producer | Source or transformed data | Operational production controls | Requirements and mappings | Upstream defects and unclear requirements |
| Consumer | Use-specific outputs and feedback | Acceptance for intended use where assigned | Data, reports and extracts | Fitness for purpose and unexplained changes |
| Independent challenge | Review evidence and findings | Independent assessment, not business ownership | Policies, lineage, tests and issue records | Design and operating effectiveness |

```text
Owner sets accountable meaning and quality
    ↓
Producer supplies and steward governs operation
    ↓
Consumer tests usefulness in real use
    ↓
Challenge independently tests the control framework
```

The same person may wear more than one role in a small organisation, but decision rights must remain explicit and incompatible duties should not become self-approval.

### Never confuse [Data owner] and [Data steward]

**Data owner** - accountable business authority for the governed data domain, including definition, required quality and prioritisation.

---

**Data steward** - operational custodian of standards, metadata, monitoring and remediation coordination; stewardship does not remove the owner's accountability.

### Permanent trap

Assigning "IT" as owner merely because IT hosts the system. System operation does not confer business authority to decide what exposure, customer, product or risk classification means.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Organisational titles vary, but the separation of accountability, operation, production, use and independent challenge is a general governance need.

<Recall
  q="Who decides whether the required quality threshold for a CDE is acceptable?"
  a="The accountable owner, informed by consumers, stewards and risk context. The steward can operate monitoring but should not silently redefine acceptable risk."

<Recall

  a="Data can pass technical checks yet fail its intended use; the consumer is positioned to detect missing granularity, stale timing or semantic problems that production controls may not reveal."
