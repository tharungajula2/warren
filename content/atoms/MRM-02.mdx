### Core mechanism

```text
Underlying reality
    ↓
Model represents reality imperfectly
    ↓
Decision relies on model output
    ↓
Error, limitation or misuse affects decision
    ↓
Financial · reporting · operational · compliance consequences
```

| Object | What can go wrong |
| :--- | :--- |
| Underlying credit exposure | Borrower fails to pay |
| Market position | Prices or rates move adversely |
| Liquidity position | Cash cannot be raised when needed |
| Model | The institution measures, predicts or acts on those risks incorrectly |

A model can be technically sound yet still generate model risk if it is used outside its intended population, horizon or decision context.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Model classification · Business decision
This:       Model risk
Downstream: Risk sources · Tiering · Governance · Validation
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Model owner | Identifies and manages model-specific risk |
| Business user | Understands limitations before consuming output |
| MRM / validation | Independently challenges reliability and use |
| Senior management | Oversees material model risk within appetite |

### Permanent rule

Model risk belongs to the use of the representation, not to the underlying exposure itself.

### Never confuse [Model risk] and [Underlying financial risk]

**Model risk** - risk that reliance on a model contributes to an adverse decision or outcome.

---

**Underlying financial risk** - risk inherent in the exposure or activity being modelled, such as borrower default, market movement or funding stress.

### Permanent trap

Explaining an unexpected loss entirely as "credit risk" or "market risk" without asking whether the model mismeasured the risk, was used outside scope or produced misleading outputs.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. The cited frameworks differ in perimeter and prescription, but all treat model risk as a risk arising from model use rather than as another name for the risk the model measures.

<Recall
  q="Can model risk exist when the model's underlying portfolio has not changed?"
  a="Yes. A coding defect, invalid assumption or misuse can change the reliability of model output even when the economic exposure itself is unchanged."
