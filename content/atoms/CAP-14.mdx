### Core mechanism

```text
Exposure
    ↓
Protection exists?
    ↓
Eligible type?
    ↓
Legally enforceable?
    ↓
Operational conditions met?
    ↓
Valuation / maturity / currency rules met?
    ↓
Regulatory CRM relief
```

| CRM form | Prudential effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Financial collateral | Can reduce recognised exposure after prescribed adjustments |
| Guarantee / eligible credit derivative | Can substitute protection-provider treatment for protected amount |
| Enforceable netting | Can recognise qualifying offsets instead of gross exposure |
| Ineligible security | May reduce economic risk but gives no regulatory relief |

The legal-enforceability test matters because capital relief assumes the bank can actually realise or call the protection when the obligor fails.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Standardised exposure · collateral as mitigant
This:       CRM eligibility gate
Downstream: Substitution · netting · haircuts · post-CRM RWA
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Front line / credit | Capture collateral and guarantee terms accurately |
| Legal | Confirm enforceability and documentation |
| Risk / capital | Apply eligibility and prudential treatment |
| Independent assurance | Challenge data, legal opinions and calculation controls |

### Permanent rule

Protection reduces regulatory capital only to the extent the capital framework recognises it.

### Never confuse [Economic mitigation] and [Regulatory CRM]

**Economic mitigation** — protection that can reduce actual loss.

---

**Regulatory CRM** — protection that also satisfies the prudential recognition conditions for capital relief.

### Permanent trap

Subtracting every pledged asset's market value from exposure ignores eligibility, legal enforceability, haircuts and mismatch rules.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in the principle. Eligible collateral, guarantors and detailed operational tests vary by jurisdiction.

  a="Yes; economic protection can fail prudential eligibility or enforceability conditions."
