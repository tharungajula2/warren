### Core mechanism

| Input | Measurement role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Contractual cash flows | Establish what is due |
| Expected borrower payments | Reduce the shortfall |
| Eligible recoveries | Reduce the shortfall when expected to be realised |
| Recovery timing and costs | Change present value of collections |
| Effective interest rate | Discounts shortfalls to the reporting date |

### Flow

```text
Contractual cash flows due
    ↓
Cash flows expected to be received
    ↓
Shortfall in amount and / or timing
    ↓
Discount to reporting date
    ↓
Credit loss
```

### Formula

```text
Credit loss = PV of contractual cash flows due - PV of cash flows expected to be received

PV of contractual cash flows due = present value of payments owed under the contract.

PV of cash flows expected to be received = present value of expected borrower payments plus eligible expected recoveries.

Discount rate = the effective interest rate required by the applicable measurement rules.

Credit loss ↑ when expected collections fall, expected recovery is delayed, recovery costs rise or discounting reduces distant recoveries more heavily.
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Contractual cash flows · Expected recoveries · EIR
This:       Discounted cash shortfall
Downstream: ECL engine · LGD · Allowance
```

### Permanent rule

Timing is part of credit loss. Receiving the same nominal cash much later can still create a loss.

### Never confuse [Credit loss] and [Fair-value loss]

**Credit loss** — expected contractual cash shortfall attributable to credit risk.

---

**Fair-value loss** — market-value movement that can contain interest-rate, liquidity, credit and other effects.

### Permanent trap

Treating PD × LGD × EAD as the accounting definition can make the implementation drive the objective. The model must reproduce the required loss concept, not redefine it.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP is rule-based rather than an Ind AS 109 discounted-cash-shortfall model. RBI’s bank framework from 1 April 2027 introduces ECL measurement and EIR mechanics.

#### UK (UKEB)
No delta from IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission)
No delta from IFRS 9.

#### US (FASB)
CECL aims to reflect the net amount expected to be collected but permits several estimation techniques; discounted cash flow is not mandatory for every asset.
