### Core mechanism

| Entity class | Lending role | Key perimeter point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Scheduled commercial banks | Full-service lending across retail, MSME and corporate segments | Bank prudential and conduct rules apply |
| Small Finance Banks | Full-service banks with inclusion-oriented business constraints | Bank rules plus SFB-specific requirements |
| Payments Banks | Payments and deposit services | They are not a general lending channel |
| Regional Rural Banks | Rural and inclusion-focused banking | Separate statutory and RBI/NABARD-linked framework |
| Cooperative banks | Member/community-oriented banking | Entity-specific cooperative-bank prudential treatment |
| NBFCs | Non-bank lending and financial activity | RBI registration and NBFC Directions; SBR layer matters |
| HFCs | Housing-finance-focused NBFC category | RBI-regulated and integrated into the NBFC prudential estate |
| Account Aggregators | Consent-based financial-information intermediaries | They do not lend or hold the customer's financial information as their own asset |

"Scheduled" is not a synonym for "commercial lender", and "NBFC" is not a small bank. The labels determine legal powers, funding model and applicable directions.

### Never confuse [Payments Bank] and [Small Finance Bank]

**Payments Bank** — a restricted bank focused on deposits and payments and not a general credit originator.

---

**Small Finance Bank** — a bank that can lend and take deposits but operates with an inclusion-oriented mandate and specific business requirements.

### Permanent rule

Before applying a lending rule, identify the regulated entity and the product. Similar customer journeys can sit on different legal and prudential estates.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Commercial banks, SFBs, RRBs and cooperative banks
They are banks, but entity-specific prudential and PSL treatment differs.

#### NBFCs and HFCs
They are non-bank lenders; NBFC layer and activity type can change the governing requirements.
