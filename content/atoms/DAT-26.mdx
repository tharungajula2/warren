### Core mechanism

```text
Submission-ready return
    ↓
Final review and authorised approval
    ↓
Submit through prescribed channel
    ↓
Retain receipt, version and submission evidence
    ↓
Post-submission error found?
    ↓
Assess materiality and affected periods
    ↓
Notify / correct / resubmit as required
    ↓
Root-cause remediation and restatement record
```

| Control point | Minimum evidence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Approval | Who approved, what version, when |
| Transmission | Submitted file or form and technical identifier |
| Receipt | Portal acknowledgement or equivalent evidence |
| Correction | Original vs corrected values and reason |
| Impact | Affected returns, periods and linked decisions |
| Remediation | Root cause, issue owner and permanent fix |

Attestation is not assumed to have one universal signatory. The required certification, maker-checker structure and correction mechanics depend on the specific return and jurisdiction.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Regulatory production · Final validation · Exception disposition
This:       Filing accountability and post-filing correction
Downstream: Regulator receipt · Restatement · Issue remediation · Governance reporting
```

### Never confuse [Attestation] and [Independent assurance]

**Attestation** - accountable management certification or approval required by the applicable filing process.

---

**Independent assurance** - separate review or audit that tests evidence. Management sign-off does not become independent because it is formal.

### Permanent rule

A correction does not erase the original filing history; the bank must preserve what was submitted, what changed, why and who approved the correction.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### EU (EBA / competent authority)
Submission and correction mechanics follow the applicable supervisory reporting framework, national competent authority channel and taxonomy validations; there is no single universal EBA executive attestor for every return.

#### UK (PRA)
PRA data items and submission processes apply through the UK reporting estate; approval responsibilities are determined by the relevant return and firm's governance.

#### US
Call Reports and Federal Reserve reports have form-specific signature, certification and amendment instructions. These requirements must be read from the applicable form family rather than generalised across all US filings.

#### IN (RBI)
RBI supervisory returns are submitted through designated online portals under applicable directions and return instructions; maker-checker access and correction controls are governed by the relevant portal and return framework.
