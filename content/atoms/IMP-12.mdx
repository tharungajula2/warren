### Formula

```text
Marginal PD_t = Cumulative PD_t - Cumulative PD_(t-1)

Survival to start of t = 1 - Cumulative PD_(t-1)

Conditional PD_t = Marginal PD_t / Survival to start of t

Cumulative PD_t = probability of default from the observation date through period t.

Marginal PD_t = unconditional probability allocated specifically to period t.

Conditional PD_t = probability of default during period t conditional on having survived to its start.
```

### Core mechanism

| Horizon | Cumulative PD | Marginal PD |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Year 1 | 2% | 2% |
| Year 2 | 5% | 3% |
| Year 3 | 9% | 4% |

The year-3 cumulative 9% already contains the earlier 2% and 3%. Adding 2% + 5% + 9% would repeatedly count the same defaults.


### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   PD calibration · Expected life · Forward-looking information

```

### Permanent rule

A borrower can newly default only once. Period default probabilities must therefore be mutually consistent.

### Never confuse [Cumulative PD] and [Marginal PD]

**Cumulative PD** — probability of default by a horizon.

---

**Marginal PD** — probability of default specifically within one future interval.

### Permanent trap

Adding cumulative PDs across years double-counts defaults already included in earlier horizons.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current IRACP does not require PD term structures. RBI’s bank ECL framework from 1 April 2027 requires 12-month and lifetime default-risk measurement appropriate to stage.

#### UK (UKEB)
No prescribed IFRS 9 term-structure methodology; the measurement objective governs.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
No accounting delta from IFRS 9.

#### US (FASB)
CECL permits PD-based methods but does not require them.
