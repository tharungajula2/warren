### Formula

```text
LGD_t = discounted loss if default occurs in period t / EAD_t

Discounted loss = EAD_t - present value of eligible expected recoveries

Expected recoveries = borrower collections + eligible collateral recoveries + eligible guarantee recoveries - workout costs

LGD ↑ when recoveries fall, recovery timing lengthens or workout costs rise.

LGD ↓ when expected recoveries rise or arrive sooner.
```

### Core mechanism

Default
    ↓
Exposure to recover
    ↓
Borrower payments + collateral + guarantees
    ↓
Recovery timing and costs
    ↓
Discounted shortfall
    ↓
Accounting LGD

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Default · Collateral · Guarantees · Workout strategy
This:       Recovery cash flows · LGD
Downstream: Period ECL · Allowance
```

### Permanent rule

Collateral changes loss severity, not the borrower’s underlying obligation to repay.

### Never confuse [Accounting LGD] and [Regulatory LGD]

**Accounting LGD** — calibrated to the expected-loss measurement objective at the reporting date.

---

**Regulatory LGD** — calibrated for prudential capital purposes and may incorporate downturn requirements, floors or other conservatism.

A regulatory parameter can be a starting point. It is not automatically an accounting parameter.

### Permanent trap

Copying a downturn regulatory LGD unchanged into accounting ECL can import prudential conservatism into a measurement that is supposed to be an unbiased expected outcome.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP is not an accounting-LGD model. From 1 April 2027 banks will estimate ECL under RBI’s framework while also applying RBI prudential floors; the modelled loss and the regulatory minimum therefore remain distinct.

#### UK (UKEB / PRA)
IFRS 9 accounting LGD and prudential capital LGD have different objectives.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
Same accounting distinction; prudential IRB parameters remain governed separately from IFRS 9 ECL.

#### US (FASB)
CECL permits LGD-based approaches but does not prescribe them.
