### Core mechanism

| Dimension | EU | UK |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Prudential reporting owner | EU legal framework with EBA technical standards | PRA Rulebook and PRA policy |
| Core reporting families | COREP for prudential data; FINREP for supervisory financial reporting where applicable | PRA data items, including UK implementations and retained / restated reporting requirements |
| Technical model | EBA DPM, validation rules and XBRL taxonomy packages | Bank of England Banking XBRL taxonomy and PRA instructions |
| Change control | EBA reporting framework releases and EU legal adoption | PRA policy statements plus Bank of England taxonomy releases |

As at August 2026, **EBA/CP/2026/07**, dated 10 April 2026, is a closed consultation, not a final rule: the general consultation closed 10 July 2026, with an earlier 10 May 2026 deadline for specified IFRS 18-related FINREP items. EBA Reporting Framework 4.4 applies by module with future first-reference dates; its July 2026 technical package was still draft ahead of final publication planned for September 2026.

For the UK, **PS1/26**, published 20 January 2026, is final policy, not a consultation. Its Basel 3.1 reporting requirements take effect from 1 January 2027. Bank of England Banking XBRL taxonomy **v4.1.1**, published 27 August 2026, is a corrective release implementing policy intended to take effect from 1 January 2027; it is future-effective as at August 2026.

### Permanent rule

Use the framework and taxonomy applicable to the reporting reference date; current publication date and effective reporting date are not the same thing.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### EU (EBA)
COREP and FINREP sit in the EBA supervisory reporting architecture. EBA/CP/2026/07 remains a closed consultation as at August 2026; draft or future framework content must not be taught as already effective.

#### UK (PRA)
The UK has its own PRA reporting and Bank of England taxonomy estate. PS1/26 is final, while its relevant Basel 3.1 reporting changes and taxonomy v4.1.1 are future-effective from 1 January 2027.
