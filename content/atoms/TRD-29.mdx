OTC derivatives are documented through a stack rather than one self-contained trade ticket. The master agreement establishes relationship-wide legal terms such as payment mechanics, events of default, termination events, representations and close-out. A negotiated schedule amends or supplements the standard master terms for the two parties.

Credit-support documentation addresses collateral. Under ISDA relationships, a Credit Support Annex or other credit-support document defines eligible collateral, thresholds, minimum transfer amounts, valuation, haircuts and transfer mechanics. These collateral terms affect exposure, liquidity and valuation.

Individual transactions are then recorded in confirmations that specify product economics: notional, dates, fixed rates, floating-rate options, strikes, settlement and other terms. Confirmations frequently incorporate published definitional booklets rather than restating every calculation rule. For interest-rate derivatives, the 2021 ISDA Interest Rate Derivatives Definitions are a major modern reference framework.

The “single agreement” concept under ISDA connects transactions legally under the master framework. That architecture is crucial for close-out netting because a default can lead to termination and calculation of a relationship-level net amount under the applicable agreement.

Product-specific financing markets use other master agreements. Repo is commonly documented under the GMRA; securities lending under the GMSLA. Those should not be replaced casually by an ISDA label because legal form and product mechanics differ.

For data design, contract identity is therefore separate from trade identity. A trade should map to the correct master agreement, schedule, collateral agreement and definitional version. Amendments must be effective-dated. If a relationship is repapered, historical trades may remain under old terms while new trades use new terms.

The practical question is: which document controls this field? Economic data may come from the confirmation; close-out rights from the master; collateral thresholds from the CSA; calculation mechanics from definitions.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Map every contractual data element to the document that legally controls it.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Treating the confirmation as the entire legal contract or assuming every OTC product uses the same master agreement.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Master agreement" b="Confirmation">
**Master agreement** — relationship-level legal framework governing multiple transactions.

---

**Confirmation** — transaction-level document recording specific economic terms.
</NeverConfuse>

**No delta.**
</Rule>
