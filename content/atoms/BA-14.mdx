### Core mechanism

The minimum business chain is bidirectional.

```text
Mandate -&gt; Scope item -&gt; Requirement -&gt; Decision or change -&gt; Delivery evidence -&gt; Acceptance
Acceptance -&gt; Evidence -&gt; Requirement -&gt; Scope item -&gt; Mandate
```

| Trace question | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| Why does this requirement exist? | Prevents orphan scope |
| What changed its meaning? | Preserves decision history |
| Which delivery item implements it? | Exposes missing implementation |
| Which evidence supports acceptance? | Exposes untested or unevidenced obligations |
| What happened if it was deferred? | Prevents silent loss |
| Which requirement does this build item serve? | Exposes gold-plating and accidental scope |

A data mapping specification must carry the data-control content needed for lineage; a test pack must carry the evidence mechanics needed for testing. BA does not duplicate those structures. BA keeps the business link into and out of them.

Traceability must support versioning. If requirement R4 becomes R4a and R4b after a policy decision, preserve the relationship rather than deleting R4 and pretending the children always existed.

Coverage is not just "every requirement has a link". A link to the wrong version or an obsolete test is false comfort. Status must reflect the current baseline.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Signed requirements · Decision history · Scope
This:       Business trace chain · Version continuity
Downstream: Design · Test governance · Acceptance · Closure
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA maintains business-level traceability and change relationships |
| Approves | Requirement owners confirm material changes to meaning |
| Consumes | Delivery, data, testing, governance and assurance teams use the chain |
| Independently challenges | Assurance or control functions challenge gaps in required evidence or ownership |

### Permanent rule

Traceability is about meaning, not hyperlinks. A complete-looking matrix is useless if it connects obsolete versions or artefacts that no longer prove the approved requirement.

### Never confuse [Traceability] and [Data lineage]

**Traceability** - connects mandate, requirement, decision, delivery and acceptance through change.

---

**Data lineage** - connects data origin, transformation and use so the movement and meaning of data can be followed.

### Permanent trap

Rebuilding traceability just before acceptance. By then, scope decisions and version history have been lost, and the matrix becomes a reconstruction rather than a control.
