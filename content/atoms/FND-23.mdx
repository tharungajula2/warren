### Core mechanism

| Function | Question answered |
| :--- | :--- |
| Statutory mandate | What powers and objectives exist? |
| Regulation / rulemaking | What requirements apply? |
| Authorisation | Who may conduct regulated activity? |
| Reporting | What information must firms provide? |
| Supervision / examination | What is actually happening inside the firm? |
| Remediation / enforcement | What must change, and what consequences are available? |

A minimum ratio is only one supervisory input. Supervisors can also assess governance, controls, risk concentrations, data, financial condition, and management capability.

### Flow

```text
Statutory mandate
    ↓
Rules / permissions
    ↓
Authorisation
    ↓
Reporting + Ongoing supervision
    ↓
Assessment / examination
    ↓
Remediation → Enforcement where legally available
```

### Permanent rule

A regulator's title does not reveal its full mandate. Identify the entity supervised, legal power, and function being exercised.

### Never confuse [Regulation] and [Supervision]

**Regulation** — establishes applicable requirements under the relevant mandate.

---

**Supervision** — assesses firms and practices against requirements and supervisory objectives.

### Permanent trap

Treating a reported ratio above minimum as proof that supervisory concern is impossible. Point-in-time compliance does not settle governance, controls, concentrations, or resilience.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Supervisory architecture |
| :--- | :--- |
| IN | RBI exercises banking regulation and supervision within its statutory remit, including directions, reporting, inspection, and enforcement. |
| UK | PRA prudentially regulates and supervises firms within remit; FCA has major conduct responsibilities and prudential responsibilities for firms within its remit. |
| EU | EBA develops harmonised rules and convergence; ECB/SSM and national competent authorities supervise institutions according to remit. |
| US | Supervision is multi-agency; Federal Reserve, OCC, FDIC, and state authorities cover institutions according to charter and statute. |
