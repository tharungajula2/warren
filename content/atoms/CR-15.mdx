### Core mechanism

| Dimension | Point-in-time tendency | Through-the-cycle tendency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Current borrower information | High sensitivity | Greater emphasis on persistent characteristics |
| Current macro conditions | More direct response | Temporary cyclical effects damped |
| Rating migration | Typically more frequent through the cycle | Typically more stable through ordinary cycles |
| Near-term risk monitoring | Naturally responsive | Can lag rapid cyclical change if used alone |
| Long-run calibration | May still require long-run anchoring depending on use | Does not automatically satisfy any regulatory calibration rule |

A one-year PD can be PIT, TTC-like or somewhere between. Likewise, a lifetime forecast can use changing point-in-time macroeconomic paths. **Horizon and cyclical conditioning are separate axes.**

<Flow>
Current borrower / macro conditions deteriorate
    ↓
PIT risk signal ↑ → PIT PD / migration ↑

Cycle-neutral design ↑
    ↓
Cyclical responsiveness ↓ → Rating stability ↑
</Flow>

<Flow>
Upstream:   Rating drivers · Macroeconomic information · Calibration philosophy
This:       Choose degree of current-condition sensitivity
Downstream: PD movement · Rating migration · Pricing · Provisioning · Stress analysis
</Flow>

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
PIT/TTC describes sensitivity to current and cyclical conditions; it does not by itself specify the PD horizon.
</Rule>

<NeverConfuse a="Point-in-time" b="Short horizon">
**Point-in-time** — strong conditioning on information and conditions at the observation date.

---
**Short horizon** — a forecast interval; it says nothing by itself about how cyclical information enters the model.
</NeverConfuse>

<NeverConfuse a="Through-the-cycle" b="Long-run average PD">
**Through-the-cycle** — a rating philosophy designed to damp temporary cyclical movements.

---
**Long-run average PD** — a calibration concept; averaging observed defaults is not by itself a TTC rating system.
</NeverConfuse>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Calling a multi-year average default rate “TTC” without examining rating assignment, macro sensitivity and calibration confuses statistical smoothing with rating philosophy.
</Trap>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Supervisors and accounting standards may require particular calibration or forward-looking treatment for specific uses, but PIT/TTC remains the same conceptual distinction.
</Rule>

<Recall
  q="Does TTC mean lifetime PD?"
  a="No. TTC concerns cyclical sensitivity, not forecast horizon."
/>

<Recall
  q="Which view usually moves more when current economic conditions deteriorate?"
  a="Point-in-time."
/>

<Recall
  q="Can a stable grade have a changing PD?"
  a="Yes. Grade assignment and grade-to-PD calibration can respond differently to conditions."
/>
