### Core mechanism

CR-10 → PD = likelihood of default over a defined horizon.

| Step | Question |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 | What was default risk at initial recognition? |
| 2 | What is default risk at the reporting date over the relevant remaining life? |
| 3 | Has the increase become significant? |
| 4 | Do qualitative, forward-looking or collective indicators change the conclusion? |
| 5 | If yes: Stage 2 and lifetime ECL; if no: Stage 1, subject to other rules |

### Flow

```text
Origination risk
    ↓ compare with
Current lifetime default risk
    ↓
No significant increase → Stage 1
Significant increase    → Stage 2
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Origination benchmark · Current PD · Credit indicators
This:       SICR assessment
Downstream: Stage 1 / Stage 2 · ECL horizon
```

### Permanent rule

SICR measures deterioration from the risk accepted at initial recognition.

### Never confuse [Relative deterioration] and [Absolute high risk]

**Relative deterioration** — how far risk has moved from the starting point.

---

**Absolute high risk** — how risky the exposure is today.

A low-risk origination can experience SICR before becoming absolutely high risk.

### Permanent trap

One current-PD threshold for every exposure can miss severe deterioration in loans that began with very low risk and over-stage loans that were already risky at origination.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP does not use IFRS 9 SICR. RBI’s bank framework from 1 April 2027 requires assessment of significant deterioration for Stage 2. Ind AS-implementing NBFCs apply Ind AS ECL subject to RBI guidance.

#### UK (UKEB)
No accounting delta from UK-adopted IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
No accounting delta from EU-adopted IFRS 9; EBA guidance expects timely identification of deterioration.

#### US (FASB)
CECL has no SICR gateway determining whether lifetime loss begins.

<Recall
  q="What is the reference point for SICR?"
  a="Credit risk at initial recognition."
/>

<Recall
  q="Can a borrower with moderate current PD have SICR?"
  a="Yes — a moderate current PD can represent a significant increase from a low origination benchmark."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why is SICR absent from CECL?"
  a="Lifetime expected loss is already recognised from initial recognition."
