### Core mechanism

The BA preserves meaning as work crosses organisational boundaries; documenting the change is only one part of that job.

```text
Mandate -&gt; Need -&gt; Requirement -&gt; Decision -&gt; Build -&gt; Evidence -&gt; Acceptance -&gt; Production ownership
```

| Change point | BA contribution | Accountable owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Problem | Frame and test the need | Sponsor or business owner |
| Requirement | Elicit, structure, trace | Requirement or process owner |
| Design | Challenge against approved need | Architect or technology owner |
| Evidence | Connect to requirement; expose gaps | Test, control or acceptance owner |
| Go-live | Expose readiness and unresolved issues | Go/no-go authority |
| Live outcome | Force explicit handover | BAU process or service owner |
| Disputed decision | Frame options and consequences | Authorised decision-maker |
| Missing owner | Surface and escalate the gap | Role holding the mandate |

Facilitation never transfers accountability. Resolve an unowned decision before it becomes an implied BA choice, informal approval or production risk.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Change mandate · Sponsor · Business objective
This:       Requirement and decision discipline
Downstream: Design challenge · Acceptance · Go-live · BAU ownership
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA produces structured requirements, decision records, traceability and impact analysis |
| Approves | Business or control owners approve what falls within their mandate |
| Consumes | Delivery, testing, operations, risk, finance and control teams consume the agreed meaning |
| Independently challenges | Risk, compliance, model validation, internal audit or other independent functions where their mandate applies |

### Permanent rule

Facilitation does not transfer accountability. Every material requirement, acceptance decision and residual risk needs a named owner with authority to own it.

### Never confuse [Facilitation] and [Accountability]

**Facilitation** - frames the question, brings the right people together, records the decision and keeps the chain coherent.

---

**Accountability** - carries the authority and consequence for the decision or outcome.

### Permanent trap

The competent BA becomes the unofficial owner of everything because everyone relies on them. That feels efficient during delivery and fails at sign-off, go-live or audit because the person who prepared the decision was never authorised to make it.
