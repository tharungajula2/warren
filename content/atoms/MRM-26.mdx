### Core mechanism

| AI/ML issue | Control question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Complexity | What decision benefit justifies harder challenge? |
| Explainability | Can users and validators understand important drivers and limitations? |
| Training data | Is provenance, quality and representativeness traceable? |
| Bias / fairness | Are material outcome differences identified and governed? |
| Automation | Can users recognise when output should not be accepted automatically? |
| Human oversight | Who can review, override, suspend or escalate? |
| Dynamic updating | Can behaviour change without an ordinary release event? |
| Fallback | What happens if the model is unavailable or unsafe? |

```text
AI output
    ↓
Human or automated decision
    ↓
Outcome
    ↓
Monitoring and challenge
    ↓
Override · Restrict · Disable
```

Explainability is purpose-dependent: developers, decision users and validators need different kinds of explanation.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Model definition · Method complexity · Production use
This:       AI/ML governance and human control
Downstream: AI/ML validation · Monitoring · Consumer / operational controls
```

### Permanent rule

Higher predictive performance does not cancel governability. If a material model cannot be understood, challenged, controlled or stopped, performance alone is insufficient.

### Never confuse [Explainability] and [Accuracy]

**Explainability** - ability to communicate relevant factors, behaviour or reasons at the level needed by the use and audience.

---

**Accuracy** - degree to which outputs correspond to targets or realised outcomes. A model can be accurate yet insufficiently explainable.

### Permanent trap

Adding a post-hoc explanation tool and treating the model as fully understood. Explanation methods are analytical tools with their own faithfulness and limitations.

<Recall
  q="Why is explainability not one universal metric?"
  a="Different users need different explanations: validators need model-behaviour evidence, business users need decision drivers, and governance may require reasons suitable for affected customers."
/>

<Recall
  q="What if an AI model cannot be safely controlled during failure?"
  a="Stronger intervention, fallback or disable mechanisms are needed before material reliance; inability to stop the model is itself a governance weakness."
