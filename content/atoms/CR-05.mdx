### Core mechanism

| Decision layer | Question | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Credit policy | What business and risk will the institution accept? | Target markets, criteria, limits, prohibited/exception cases |
| Repayment capacity | From what recurring cash flow or resources will contractual payments be made? | Base-case debt service assessment |
| Downside resilience | What happens if revenue, rates, costs or asset values move adversely? | Stressed repayment capacity and weaknesses |
| Structure | Can tenor, amortisation, covenants, limit or pricing make the risk acceptable? | Proposed terms and controls |
| Mitigants | What secondary recovery exists if primary repayment fails? | Collateral / guarantee assessment |
| Approval authority | Who may accept this size, risk and exception profile? | Approve / decline / approve with conditions / escalate |

A sound decision is not “good borrower versus bad borrower”. It is **borrower + purpose + amount + structure + repayment source + downside + mitigants + price**, all within policy and delegated authority.

### Flow

```text
Purpose + amount + obligor
    ↓
Policy eligibility
    ↓
Primary repayment source → Repayment capacity → Downside resilience
    ↓
Structure → Secondary mitigants → Approval authority
    ↓
Approve / conditions / escalate / decline
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Obligor · Facility request · Financial/behavioural data · External credit data · Policy
This:       Repayment capacity → Downside → Structure → Mitigants → Approval authority
Downstream: Sanction terms · Documentation · Disbursement · Monitoring · Rating
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Front office / credit analyst assembles facts, cash-flow analysis and recommendation |
| Approves | Delegated credit officer, committee, senior management or board according to authority |
| Consumes | Documentation, operations, monitoring, portfolio management and finance |
| Challenges | Independent credit risk, second-line policy governance and internal audit |

### Permanent rule

Approve credit primarily on sustainable repayment capacity. Treat collateral and guarantees as secondary protection unless the product is explicitly designed to repay from asset sale or liquidation.

### Never confuse [Credit policy] and [Approval authority]

**Credit policy** — sets what risks may be taken and under what criteria.

---

**Approval authority** — determines who may accept a particular exposure, size, risk or exception.

### Permanent trap

A high collateral value can make an uneconomic loan look safe. If the borrower cannot service the debt, the bank has underwritten a workout, not a performing credit.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta in the core rule.** Local consumer-protection, responsible-lending, related-party and governance requirements vary, but sound underwriting universally requires defined criteria, repayment-capacity analysis and controlled approval authority.
