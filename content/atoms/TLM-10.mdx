A robust test begins with a base cash-flow and behavioural model, then applies multiple shocks. A **parallel up** or **parallel down** scenario shifts the curve broadly in one direction. **Steepener** and **flattener** scenarios change the relationship between short and long rates. **Short-rate shocks** isolate the front of the curve. Basis and option stresses should be layered where material.

Each scenario is translated into ΔEVE and, where relevant, ΔNII. Behaviour should not automatically remain at the base case: deposit stability, deposit beta, loan prepayments and term-deposit redemption may change under stress. Reverse stress testing asks a different question: what combination of rate and behavioural assumptions would breach a critical risk or capital limit?

Basel’s supervisory outlier test uses six prescribed interest-rate shock scenarios for EVE and identifies banks with a maximum EVE decline exceeding a supervisory threshold calibrated at 15% of Tier 1 capital. The threshold is a supervisory trigger for attention rather than a statement that exposure immediately below 15% is acceptable.

RBI’s November 2025 forthcoming framework adopts a six-shock EVE structure and identifies a bank as an outlier if ΔEVE decline exceeds 15% of Tier 1 capital in any prescribed scenario. It places IRRBB capital assessment in ICAAP under Pillar 2 and permits supervisory responses including additional capital, hedging, exposure reduction, parameter constraints or risk-management improvements. Its implementation date, however, remains to be communicated; the Directions explicitly describe interim preparedness and reporting.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
A supervisory outlier threshold is a screening threshold, not a target risk appetite.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Managing ΔEVE to sit just below the regulatory outlier threshold while ignoring NII, basis risk, optionality and internal stress scenarios.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Stress test" b="Supervisory outlier test">
**Stress test** — a broad internal or supervisory assessment under severe scenarios and assumptions.
---
**Supervisory outlier test** — a prescribed screen used to identify unusually high exposure under standardised shocks.
</NeverConfuse>
