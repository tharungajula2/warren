### Core mechanism

| Question | 12-month ECL | Lifetime ECL |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Which default events enter? | Defaults possible in next 12 months, or shorter remaining life | Defaults possible over relevant remaining life |
| How much loss follows an included default? | Full discounted loss consequence | Full discounted loss consequence |
| Can post-month-12 recoveries matter? | Yes | Yes |
| Typical IFRS 9 use | Stage 1 | Stage 2 and Stage 3 |

### Flow

```text
Reporting date
    ↓
Choose default-event horizon
    ↓
Identify defaults possible within that horizon
    ↓
Measure full cash-shortfall consequence of those defaults
    ↓
Probability-weight and discount
    ↓
ECL
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Stage assignment · Expected life · Default timing
This:       12-month ECL · Lifetime ECL
Downstream: PD term structure · ECL amount
```

### Permanent rule

“Twelve months” limits the default-event window, not the period over which resulting cash shortfalls and recoveries are measured.

### Never confuse [12-month ECL] and [Twelve months of losses]

**12-month ECL** — lifetime consequences of defaults possible within the next twelve months.

---

**Twelve months of losses** — only shortfalls occurring during the next year. That is not the IFRS 9 definition.

### Permanent trap

Cutting off post-default recoveries or losses at month twelve understates or distorts Stage 1 ECL whenever workout continues beyond one year.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP has no 12-month-ECL concept. RBI’s bank ECL framework from 1 April 2027 adopts 12-month ECL for Stage 1 and lifetime ECL for Stage 2 and Stage 3.

#### UK (UKEB)
No delta from IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission)
No delta from IFRS 9.

#### US (FASB)
CECL does not use a Stage 1 12-month allowance for amortised-cost assets; lifetime expected credit loss is recognised from initial recognition.

<Recall
  q="What does “12-month” restrict?"
  a="The set of default events included."
/>

<Recall
  q="Can a default in month nine generate losses in year three that enter 12-month ECL?"
  a="Yes — the default occurs inside the 12-month window, so its full discounted loss consequence is included."
/>

<Recall
  q="Which IFRS 9 stages use lifetime ECL?"
  a="Stage 2 and Stage 3."
