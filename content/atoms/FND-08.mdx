### Core mechanism

| Event | Reporting effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Credit exposure exists | Contractual claim is recognised |
| Loss estimate changes | Governing framework measures the change |
| Allowance / provision changes | Carrying amount and/or liability changes as required |
| Charge / release recognised | Profit or loss changes under the applicable rules |

The allowance is not cash placed in a separate reserve. Under US CECL, the allowance for applicable amortised-cost assets is a valuation account; under IFRS 9, impairment distinguishes 12-month and lifetime expected credit losses. Indian scheduled commercial banks sit under a different RBI provisioning framework.

### Permanent rule

An allowance or provision is a recognised measurement or prudential amount, not a segregated pot of cash.

### Never confuse [Accounting allowance] and [Prudential provision]

**Accounting allowance** — credit-loss recognition under the applicable financial-reporting framework.

---

**Prudential provision** — an amount required or recognised under supervisory rules; interaction does not make the two calculations identical.

### Permanent trap

Assuming that “expected loss”, “provision”, “stage”, or “allowance” carries identical mechanics across jurisdictions.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Real delta |
| :--- | :--- |
| IN | RBI deferred Ind AS for scheduled commercial banks until further notice. Bank asset classification and provisioning follow RBI directions, including the 2025 IRACP Directions. |
| UK | Entities applying UK-adopted IFRS 9 use the IFRS 9 expected-credit-loss model. |
| EU | Accounts within EU-adopted IFRS scope use adopted IFRS 9 impairment requirements. |
| US | ASC Topic 326 uses the CECL objective for applicable assets; no single estimation technique is universally mandated. |
