### Core mechanism

| UAT control | Question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Requirement coverage | Has every material requirement been exercised or explicitly dispositioned? |
| Scenario realism | Do cases reflect real products, populations and reporting conditions? |
| Expected outcome | Is the pass result known before execution? |
| Evidence | Can another reviewer reproduce what was tested and observed? |
| Defect linkage | Are failures tied to requirements, mappings and impacted reports? |
| Acceptance | Are residual limitations understood and approved by the right authority? |

```text
Approved requirements
    ↓
Business scenarios and coverage matrix
    ↓
Execute end-to-end output
    ↓
Compare with expected business result
    ↓
Defect → fix and retest
Pass → documented acceptance
```

UAT should include consumers who understand the reporting or risk use. A technically correct field can still fail because the wrong definition was agreed, required history is absent or a downstream user cannot distinguish a legitimate null from missing data.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Requirements · Test packs · Integrated build
This:       Business acceptance and evidence
Downstream: Downstream validation · Release decision · Defect closure
```

### Never confuse [UAT] and [System testing]

**UAT** - asks whether the solution satisfies the approved business need and can be relied on in use.

---

**System testing** - asks whether the technical solution behaves according to specifications. Both are necessary when business meaning and technical implementation can diverge.

### Permanent rule

UAT sign-off is evidence of acceptance against defined criteria, not a ceremonial approval after a deadline has already forced go-live.

### Permanent trap

Using only production-like totals as UAT evidence. Aggregate agreement can hide wrong classifications, duplicated records or incorrect edge-case treatment.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** UAT practice is universal; the scenarios inherit jurisdiction-specific reporting requirements where relevant.

<Recall
  q="Why is a UAT coverage matrix useful?"
  a="It links material requirements to executed scenarios and results, exposing requirements that were never tested instead of assuming broad testing covered them."
/>

<Recall
  q="Can UAT pass with known limitations?"
  a="Only where the limitations are explicitly assessed, controlled and accepted by the proper authority; silence or schedule pressure is not risk acceptance."
/>
