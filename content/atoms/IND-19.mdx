  standardized lending processes. It provides open protocols so any bank or NBFC
  can offer standardized lending products (LOAN service) to any borrower via
  their preferred interface (called Loan App Provider). The **Unified Lending

  let a borrower initiate a loan request once, then multiple lenders can compete

OCEN’s idea: separate *procurement of borrowers* (handled by digital aggregators or apps, called LAPs) from *funding* (banks/NBFCs). Using open APIs (like rupee-instant-pay, AA), a Loan App can share borrower’s data with many lenders using OCEN. Each lender then provides loan quotes. Final loan issuance happens through UPI’s LTI (loan transfer initiation) or direct credit.

The OCEN framework is still evolving. It is mandated for certain government loan schemes (like for MSMEs). Under OCEN, lenders must publish their loan “schema” (min criteria) and pricing APIs. Borrowers see an offer comparison screen. Ongoing pilots involve fintech lending apps and major banks. The concept addresses fragmentation – instead of one bank-per-relationship, small borrowers can access many lenders digitally.

Regulatory stance: RBI encourages this open network but underscores customer protection (same KFS, consent rules apply). OCEN/ULI bypass middlemen fees and aim for credit deepening. However, RBI has not mandated full roll-out; it’s voluntary with incentives. By 2026 RBI reports many pilots, but wide adoption is pending. Key point: OCEN/ULI are *not* separate entities, just infrastructure standards.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Forthcoming RBI guideline: digital lenders can use the ULI (under OCEN) to interconnect, but all standard rules (KFS, KYC) still apply. For now, any participation is voluntary but encouraged for efficiency.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Thinking OCEN/ULI replaces banks. In fact, banks/NBFCs remain the credit providers. OCEN just standardizes the way loan requests flow to them. Another mistake: assuming an app can lend via OCEN without partnering a regulated lender – regulatory approval is still needed.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse>
**OCEN** — a framework of open APIs and data standards for credit enabling (no single “company”).
**ULI** — proposed RBI/IBA interface for unified loan origination/decision (still under development).
</NeverConfuse>
