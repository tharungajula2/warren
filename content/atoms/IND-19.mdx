### Core mechanism

```text
Credit account created or updated
    ↓
Credit institution submits prescribed data to CIC
    ↓
CIC updates borrower record
    ↓
Borrower accesses CIR or raises dispute
    ↓
Lender and CIC investigate source data
    ↓
Correction or reasoned closure is recorded
```

| Control point | Failure it prevents |
| :--- | :--- |
| Regular reporting | Missing or stale credit history |
| Standard data fields | Inconsistent borrower/account interpretation |
| Data-quality checks | Wrong balances, status or identifiers |
| Dispute workflow | Persistent customer harm from erroneous reporting |
| Closure/update process | Accounts remaining incorrectly shown as active or delinquent |

RBI strengthened credit-information reporting and customer compensation/correction expectations through recent directions. Further amendments took effect during the 2025-2026 period. The exact post-consolidation amendment references are UNVERIFIED in this pack as at 31 August 2026, so no unsupported reporting-frequency number is stated here.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Loan servicing · Payment status
This:       CIC reporting and correction
Downstream: CIR · Underwriting · Customer grievance
```

### Permanent rule

A lender's obligation is not satisfied by submitting data once; reported credit information must remain accurate as the account changes.

### Permanent trap

Treating a bureau error as solely the CIC's problem. The originating credit institution is responsible for the accuracy of the data it supplies and must participate in correction.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Banks and NBFCs that are credit institutions
Reporting obligations apply through the CIC framework.

#### CICs
They have separate duties for record handling, dispute management and customer access.

<Recall
  q="Why is credit-information reporting a servicing process rather than only an origination process?"
  a="Because balances, delinquency, closure and restructuring statuses change over time and must be reflected accurately in the borrower's record."
/>

<Recall
  q="Who must help correct wrong lender-supplied data?"
  a="The reporting credit institution must investigate and correct its source data; the CIC cannot independently invent the correct loan facts."
/>
