### Core mechanism

To summarize August balances by product, join the account to its facility and aggregate at product grain:

```sql
SELECT
    f.product_type,
    COUNT(*) AS account_rows,
    SUM(s.outstanding_balance) AS outstanding_balance
FROM loan_snapshots s
JOIN loan_accounts a
  ON a.account_id = s.account_id
JOIN facilities f
  ON f.facility_id = a.facility_id
WHERE s.reporting_date = '2026-08-31'
GROUP BY f.product_type
ORDER BY f.product_type;
```

`COUNT(*)` counts result rows. `COUNT(column)` counts only non-NULL values in that column. `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)` answers a different question again: how many unique customers appear.

```sql
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS account_rows,
    COUNT(outstanding_balance) AS rows_with_balance,
    COUNT(DISTINCT f.customer_id) AS customers
FROM loan_snapshots s
JOIN loan_accounts a
  ON a.account_id = s.account_id
JOIN facilities f
  ON f.facility_id = a.facility_id
WHERE s.reporting_date = '2026-08-31';
```

`WHERE` filters rows before aggregation. `HAVING` filters groups after aggregates exist:

```sql
SELECT f.product_type, SUM(s.outstanding_balance) AS balance
FROM loan_snapshots s
JOIN loan_accounts a ON a.account_id = s.account_id
JOIN facilities f ON f.facility_id = a.facility_id
WHERE s.reporting_date = '2026-08-31'
GROUP BY f.product_type
HAVING SUM(s.outstanding_balance) &gt; 10000000;
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Correct row population · Preserved grain
This:       GROUP BY · SUM · COUNT · DISTINCT · HAVING
Downstream: Reconciliation totals · Portfolio views · Cohort summaries
```

### Never confuse [COUNT(*)] and [COUNT(column)]

**COUNT(*)** - counts every result row in the group.

---

**COUNT(column)** - counts only rows where that column is not NULL.

### Never confuse [WHERE] and [HAVING]

**WHERE** - removes source rows before grouping.

---

**HAVING** - removes groups after aggregate values have been calculated.

### Permanent trap

Counting rows when the business question asks for customers or facilities. The SQL can be correct while the denominator is wrong because the table grain differs from the metric grain.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Aggregate semantics do not vary by regulator.

<Recall
  q="What changes when you add `GROUP BY product_type` to account-level data?"
  a="The result grain becomes one row per product type, and measures must be interpreted at that new grain."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can `COUNT(outstanding_balance)` be lower than `COUNT(*)`?"
  a="`COUNT(outstanding_balance)` ignores rows where the balance is NULL, while `COUNT(*)` counts every row."
/>

<Recall
  q="When is `HAVING` required?"
  a="Use it when the filtering condition depends on an aggregate such as `SUM`, `COUNT`, or `AVG` computed for each group."
/>
