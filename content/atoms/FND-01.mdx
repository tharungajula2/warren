### Core mechanism

| Element | Meaning on the bank's balance sheet | Typical bank items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Assets | Economic resources or claims controlled by the bank | Cash, central-bank balances, securities, loans |
| Liabilities | Present obligations of the bank | Customer deposits, wholesale borrowings, payables |
| Equity | Residual after liabilities are deducted from assets | Share capital, retained earnings, reserves |

A customer's loan is normally a **bank asset** because the customer owes the bank. A customer's deposit is normally a **bank liability** because the bank owes the customer. The label follows the reporting entity's perspective, not everyday language.

### Formula

```text
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
Assets = recognised economic resources controlled by the bank
Liabilities = present obligations of the bank
Equity = residual interest after liabilities

Equity = Assets - Liabilities
Equity = residual amount after recognised liabilities are deducted from recognised assets

If assets fall by 4 while liabilities are unchanged, equity falls by 4. Equity is not cash; it is the residual claim.
```

### Flow

```text
Economic event
    ↓
Recognition and measurement
    ↓
Asset / Liability amount
    ↓
Residual effect on Equity
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   None
This:       Balance-sheet identity · Asset / liability perspective
Downstream: Deposits · Loans · Earnings · Capital · Financial statements
```

### Permanent rule

Read every item from the reporting bank's perspective. A customer's asset can be the bank's liability, and a customer's liability can be the bank's asset.

### Never confuse [Equity] and [Cash]

**Equity** — the residual interest after liabilities are deducted from assets.

---

**Cash** — an asset available for payment; a bank can have substantial equity without holding the same amount as cash.

### Permanent trap

Treating a customer's deposit as a bank asset because the money is “at the bank”. The deposit is a claim the customer has against the bank, so it is normally the bank's liability.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The balance-sheet identity and reporting-entity perspective do not change by banking regulator or jurisdiction.
