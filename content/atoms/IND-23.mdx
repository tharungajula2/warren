For instance, every home loan borrower must be told the benchmark (now MCLR or an external rate) and margin. Any change in the base rate must be communicated one month in advance. Prepayment or foreclosure should be allowed per the FPC (sometimes with max 2% penalty). No hidden charges are allowed. If a bank refuses to lend, it must give a reason in writing (borrower’s right to information under FPC).

The 2019 “external benchmark” circular mandated that by Oct 1, 2019 all new floating-rate personal, retail, and MSME loans link to external benchmarks, and from April 1, 2020 medium enterprise loans too. This revolutionized rate transparency: borrowers now see explicit linkage to, say, RBI’s repo. Banks cannot arbitrarily change rates without a rule.

In essence, fair practices and rate guidelines aim to protect retail borrowers from predatory pricing and information asymmetry. While bankers often resent caps on lending rates, RBI views transparency (e.g. bench-marking) as non-negotiable. All retail credit product literature (e.g. home loans, auto, personal loans) must clearly detail these terms.

### Permanent rule

Lenders must disclose all loan terms in writing (sanction letter or KFS) per RBI’s FPC. All new floating-rate loans must use an external benchmark (e.g. policy rate) for interest calculations.

### Permanent trap

Failing to update borrowers on rate changes. RBI requires communication if the external benchmark shifts. Some banks adjust rates silently (not allowed). Also, bundling hidden fees into loan rate rather than showing them separately violates FPC.

### Never confuse

**Fair Practices Code (FPC)** — RBI’s consumer protection rules for lending (disclosure, no coercion, grievance mechanism).
**External Benchmarking** — the requirement that floating rates link to public indices. This is part of FPC transparency.

### Jurisdiction variants

**IN** — RBI’s mandatory external benchmark linkage (repo or gilt) is specific to India’s circulars. Elsewhere, countries may require APR disclosure but not compulsory benchmark linking.
