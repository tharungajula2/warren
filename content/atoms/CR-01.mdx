### Core mechanism

| Object | What it means | Why credit risk cares |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Obligor | Person contractually responsible for an obligation | Default status and wholesale borrower risk normally attach here |
| Borrower | Person receiving or using borrowed funds | Often the obligor in loans; not a safe synonym in every structure |
| Counterparty | Other contractual party to a transaction | Central for derivatives, securities financing and settlement exposures |
| Commercial customer | Relationship-management label | May contain several legal obligors and therefore several credit risks |

One corporate group can contain several obligors. One obligor can have many facilities. A derivative counterparty can create a credit exposure whose amount changes with market value even though no conventional loan exists.

### Flow

```text
Upstream:   Legal identity · Contract · Transaction role
This:       Identify the person that owes performance
Downstream: Facility mapping · Default status · Limits · PD · Exposure aggregation
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Front office / onboarding establishes legal identity and contractual roles |
| Approves | Credit / legal functions confirm the risk-bearing entity and structure |
| Consumes | Underwriting, limits, modelling, collections, capital and reporting |
| Challenges | Independent risk, legal, compliance and audit test entity mapping and aggregation |

### Permanent rule

Credit risk must be mapped to the legal or natural person responsible for the obligation before ratings, defaults, limits or exposures are aggregated.

### Never confuse [Obligor] and [Counterparty]

**Obligor** — the person responsible for a credit obligation.

---
**Counterparty** — the other party to a transaction; the term describes the transactional relationship and may or may not coincide with a loan borrower.

### Permanent trap

Using “customer”, “borrower”, “obligor” and “counterparty” as database synonyms can propagate one entity’s rating, default or limit status to legally separate persons.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Legal definitions and regulatory wording vary, but identifying the person responsible for an obligation is universal.
