### Core mechanism

| Parameter | Role in loss | Alignment test |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PD | Chance default occurs | Same default event and horizon as the loss measurement |
| LGD | Severity if default occurs | Conditional on that same default definition and exposure basis |
| EAD | Amount exposed at default | Measured for the same default event and population |

For a low-PD loan, the most likely realised loss may be zero while expected loss is positive. EL is an average across possible outcomes, not a prediction that every individual exposure will lose a fractional amount.

Dependence can hide inside the inputs. Revolving utilisation and recovery severity can both worsen around default or in recessions. Multiplying three independently estimated averages without considering their conditional meaning can therefore misstate the true expectation.

### Flow

```text
Align PD event / horizon + LGD conditional basis + EAD default basis
    ↓
PD × LGD × EAD
    ↓
Expected loss
    ↓
Aggregate across exposures → Portfolio EL
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   PD · LGD · EAD with aligned definitions
This:       Probability × Severity × Exposure
Downstream: Pricing · Portfolio EL · Provision/capital comparison · Profitability
```

### Formula

```text
`EL = PD × LGD × EAD`

Example: `2% × 40% × 100 = 0.8` monetary units.

PD ↑, LGD ↑ or EAD ↑ → EL ↑, holding the other inputs constant.
```

### Permanent rule

Reconcile event, horizon, population, observation point and valuation basis before multiplying PD, LGD and EAD.

### Never confuse [Expected loss] and [Most likely loss]

**Expected loss** — probability-weighted average loss over the defined horizon.

---

**Most likely loss** — the modal outcome; for many low-PD exposures it is zero.

### Permanent trap

Combining a regulatory one-year PD, a lifetime accounting LGD and today’s balance creates a numeric answer whose components do not describe one coherent loss experiment.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
For covered commercial banks, RBI’s 2026 Asset Classification, Provisioning and Income Recognition Directions mandate an expected-credit-loss provisioning framework from **1 April 2027**.

**No delta in the probability decomposition.** Regulatory capital, accounting ECL and internal economics prescribe different parameter horizons, conditions and treatments; the same formula label does not make those calculations interchangeable.
