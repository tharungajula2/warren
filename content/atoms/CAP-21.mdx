A common capital framework requires standardisation. Credit RWA, market risk, operational risk and CVA can be measured through prescribed approaches that allow comparison across institutions. That is the strength of **Pillar 1**.

Its weakness is equally important: no finite rulebook can anticipate every concentration, business-model vulnerability, governance weakness or emerging risk. Two banks with the same Pillar 1 ratio can have very different sensitivity to interest rates, concentration, pension risk, strategic risk or deficiencies in risk controls.

**Pillar 2** addresses that gap through the supervisory review process. Basel requires banks to maintain a process for assessing overall capital adequacy in relation to risk profile and strategy, and supervisors must review that process, evaluate capital adequacy and take action where necessary.

Pillar 2 can therefore affect capital even when a Pillar 1 calculation is perfectly correct. That does not mean Pillar 1 was “wrong”; it means its standardised scope was not intended to be exhaustive.

The distinction also prevents double counting. A supervisor assessing Pillar 2 should understand which risks are already captured by Pillar 1 before adding capital for residual risk. Conversely, management should not exclude a material risk from internal capital assessment merely because no dedicated Pillar 1 formula exists.

The conceptual sequence is formula first, residual-risk analysis second, supervisory judgement third.

### Permanent rule

Pillar 2 begins where standardised minimum requirements stop being a complete description of the bank’s material risk.

### Permanent trap

Adding capital under Pillar 2 without identifying what Pillar 1 already captures can create hidden double counting.

### Never confuse

**Pillar 1 requirement** — capital generated by prescribed minimum-risk methodologies.

---
**Pillar 2 requirement or supervisory assessment** — capital and supervisory response arising from risks or weaknesses not adequately captured by Pillar 1, according to the local framework.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Legal labels and calibration differ, but the conceptual function of Pillar 2 as a complement to Pillar 1 is common.
