### Core mechanism

| Layer | Question | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pillar 1 | What do prescribed capital formulas require? | Minimum risk-based capital |
| Pillar 2 | What risks or weaknesses remain insufficiently captured? | Internal assessment, supervisory review and possible additional capital |
| Buffers | How much usable CET1 should sit above requirements for conservation, cycle or systemic risk? | Additional CET1 cushion |
| Pillar 3 | What must market participants be able to observe? | Regulatory disclosures |

```text
Pillar 3 disclosure
        observes
           ↓
Buffers / usable CET1
           ↓
Pillar 2 requirement or supervisory capital
           ↓
Pillar 1 minimum
           ↓
Capital quality minima within the stack
```

The exact legal order and labels differ locally, but the conceptual error to avoid is treating Pillar 1, Pillar 2 and buffers as three interchangeable topics. Pillar 1 is the baseline calculation; Pillar 2 is the completeness check; buffers are deliberately usable layers above requirements.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   RWA engines · capital ratios
This:       Whole prudential capital architecture
Downstream: Pillar 1 · Pillar 2 · buffers · distribution constraints
```

### Permanent rule

Pillar 3 discloses the capital architecture; it does not itself add a capital layer.

### Never confuse [Pillar 2] and [Capital buffer]

**Pillar 2** — institution-specific assessment and supervisory response to residual risks or weaknesses.

---

**Capital buffer** — CET1 cushion maintained above applicable capital requirements for a defined macroprudential or conservation purpose.

### Permanent trap

Saying "Pillar 1 + Pillar 2 + Pillar 3 = total capital requirement" wrongly turns disclosure into capital and ignores buffers.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in the Basel conceptual architecture. Domestic law determines the exact Pillar 2 instruments, buffer order and disclosure implementation.
