### Core mechanism

| Role | Core contribution |
| :--- | :--- |
| Governing body | Oversight, direction, accountability to stakeholders |
| First-line roles | Deliver products/services and manage the risks arising from activity |
| Second-line roles | Expertise, support, monitoring, and challenge on risk-related matters |
| Third-line / internal audit | Independent and objective assurance and advice |
| External assurance providers | Additional assurance under their own mandates |

First- and second-line roles are both management roles. Internal audit is qualitatively different because its independence from management responsibilities supports objective assurance.

The IIA refreshed its Statement of Position on the Three Lines Model in July 2026. The current model continues to emphasise clear accountability, internal-audit independence, and useful coordination among assurance providers.

### Flow

```text
Governing body
    ↓ delegation / oversight
Management
    ├─→ First-line roles: act + manage risk
    └─→ Second-line roles: support + monitor + challenge
    ↓ information / coordination
Internal audit
    ↓
Independent assurance to governing body
```

### Ownership

| Question | Primary answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Who owns business risk? | Management / first-line roles |
| Who supports and challenges management? | Second-line roles |
| Who independently assures governance and control? | Internal audit / third line |
| Who oversees the whole structure? | Governing body |

### Permanent rule

Management retains responsibility for managing risk. Internal audit must preserve the independence and objectivity required to assure that management system.

### Never confuse [Second line] and [Third line]

**Second line** — management roles providing expertise, support, monitoring, and challenge.

---

**Third line** — internal audit providing independent and objective assurance and advice.

### Permanent trap

Calling a control failure “Risk's problem” merely because the second line monitors it. Monitoring does not transfer first-line ownership of the underlying activity and control.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The Three Lines Model is a governance model, not a jurisdiction-specific banking rule. Local laws and supervisory rules can add role-specific requirements.
