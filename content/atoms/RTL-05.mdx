### Core logic

Monthly hazard:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle h_t=P(Default_t \mid survived\ to\ t)" />

Survival:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle S_t=\prod_{j=1}^{t}(1-h_j)" />

Cumulative PD:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle PD_{0,t}=1-S_t" />
