Under current RBI rules, domestic banks’ overall PSL target is 40% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (with RRBs at 75%). Within that, there are sub-targets: at least 18% to agriculture (including 8% to small farmers), 7.5% to micro-enterprises, etc.  There is also a category of lending to weaker sections (10% of ANBC) and a target to small-scale industries (7.5%). Loans to export sectors, large industries, or corporates do NOT count.

PSL has grown to cover new areas (education, housing, renewable energy) as per RBI prescriptions.  Starting in 2016, RBI allowed banks that exceed PSL targets to sell Priority Sector Lending Certificates (PSLCs) to others who fall short. This “bank-on-bank trading” ensures goals are met system-wide. PSLCs come in types – e.g. Agriculture, Micro, General PSL – reflecting the underlying loan category.

Priority sector rules profoundly shape Indian bank books: banks fund many low-yield rural loans simply to comply with the 40% mandate. This constrains their liquidity and profitability (since many PSL segments demand concessional rates or higher risk). Unlike any developed-country system, India’s mandatory directed-credit framework ties national policy to banking regulation.

```
Adjust Net Bank Credit (ANBC):         = Total loans - (loans to foreign banks, etc.)
Priority Sector target = 40% of ANBC
    - Agriculture (including small farmers): 18%
    - Micro enterprise: 7.5%
    - Weaker sections: 10% of ANBC
    - ... (sub-targets within)
PSLC Market: surplus lender sells PSL credits to deficit lender
```

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
RBI-prescribed lending quotas (e.g. 40% to PSL) are mandatory. Banks must ensure each year’s lending meets or exceeds these sub-targets.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Ignoring sub-categories and PSL certificates. A bank might focus only on agriculture without meeting MSME or weaker section targets, risking non-compliance. Also, some assume PSL is optional – it is not.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse>
**PSL quotas** — mandatory lending targets (40% of bank credit to certain sectors).
**PSLC (certificates)** — tradable certificates representing excess PSL achievement; used to buy compliance.
</NeverConfuse>

<Delta j="IN">
— Mandatory priority-sector quotas are unique to India’s financial system. (No similar directed-credit rules exist under PRA/Fed/FASB.)
</Delta>
