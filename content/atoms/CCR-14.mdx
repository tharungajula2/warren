  SA-CCR is the Basel III capital approach for measuring CCR exposure. It
  computes Exposure at Default (EAD) as **EAD = α × (Replacement Cost +
  Potential Future Exposure)**, with α=1.4. Replacement Cost (RC) is the
  positive current value net of haircut collateral. Potential Future Exposure
  (PFE) is derived from standardized add-on factors by asset class, aggregated
  by hedging sets and offset by correlation, then net of initial margin (subject
  to a small 5% floor). This approach accounts for margining, netting, asset
  classes, and uses a higher multiplier for safety.

SA-CCR’s formula: **EAD = 1.4 × (RC + PFE)**. For example, RC = max(MTM, 0) – collateral. PFE add-ons use supervisory notional * factors (similar to old CEM add-ons but more refined). Trades are grouped by asset class and hedging sets to allow some offset. Initial margin reduces PFE but only up to 95% (the multiplier means at least 5% of IM benefit is ignored, ensuring some capital).

SA-CCR replaced the older CEM and SM and was calibrated to be risk-sensitive (accounting for netting, hedges, margin). It avoids banks’ using bespoke models (except IMM). SA-CCR became effective 2017 globally (though actual regulatory implementation varied by jurisdiction).

```text
    +-------------------------+
    | EAD = 1.4 × (RC + PFE)  |
    +-------------------------+
         /             \
     +--------+     +--------+
     |  RC    |     |  PFE   |
     +--------+     +--------+
     | Current|     | Add-on |
     | exposure (MTM)|   (std. potential exposure) |
```

### Permanent rule

EAD under SA-CCR always uses α=1.4 as a conservatism buffer. Replacement Cost cannot be negative (floor at zero).

### Permanent trap

Ignoring netting sets or collateral: SA-CCR explicitly nets trades and collaterals first. Also, forgetting to use correct supervisory factors for add-ons by product type and maturity.

### Never confuse

**Replacement Cost (RC)** — Current exposure (MTM) minus collateral.
---
**Potential Future Exposure (PFE)** — The supervisory-derived add-on component for future risk. RC is “today’s” risk, PFE is “future” risk.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Basel SA-CCR formula is standard. Jurisdictions implement it verbatim (e.g. EU CRR, UK PRA rules, Fed final rule).
