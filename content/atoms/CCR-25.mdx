Thus, while Basel global rules exist, Indian banks (scheduled commercial banks) have not implemented SA-CCR for now. They continue to compute PFE using CEM add-ons (recently revised to align with BCBS). RBI’s master circular (April 2025) discusses the future potential of SA-CCR in context of investment funds, but general bank norms will likely shift later. CCIL (Clearing Corp of India) provides CCP services for interest rate and FX derivatives, facilitating clearing, but netting benefits and new global standards are gradually being incorporated.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
For Indian banks, follow RBI’s Basel III master directions. As of now, CCR capital uses CEM (not SA-CCR) for banks. Check RBI circulars for updates on implementation timelines.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Assuming India already uses SA-CCR for banks. It does *not*. RBI is aligning CEM parameters and planning SA-CCR in the future, but until then use CEM as per RBI’s instructions.
</Trap>
