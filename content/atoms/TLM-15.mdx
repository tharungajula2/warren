### Core mechanism

| Issue | Mechanism | Failure mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Asset/liability currency mismatch | assets and liabilities denominated differently | FX value sensitivity |
| Cross-currency funding | borrow currency A, swap into B | swap rollover/basis dependence |
| Foreign investment capital | foreign net assets translate into home currency | capital-ratio volatility |
| Currency liquidity mismatch | liquid resources sit in wrong currency | payment failure despite aggregate surplus |

USD asset
    ↓ funded by
INR borrowing + INR/USD FX swap
    ↓
Swap matures before asset
    ↓
Need swap rollover or USD funding
    ↓
Swap market closes / basis widens
    ↓
USD liquidity stress

Basel also permits national supervisors, subject to conditions, to exclude qualifying **structural** FX positions from net open FX capital calculations where the position genuinely hedges exchange-rate sensitivity of the capital ratio. That prudential treatment does not remove the economic position.

### Permanent rule

Liquidity is currency-specific at payment: surplus currency A covers currency B only if conversion or transfer remains executable in time and in stress.

### Never confuse [FX position hedge] and [Currency funding hedge]

**FX position hedge** — reduces sensitivity of value to exchange-rate moves.

---

**Currency funding hedge** — secures the required currency over the required horizon; an FX swap can reduce spot exposure while creating rollover dependence.

### Permanent trap

Netting all currencies into one liquidity number. Convertibility, swap depth, legal-entity restrictions and settlement timing can prevent an apparent surplus from covering the short currency.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI liquidity guidance requires monitoring across currencies and foreign-currency structural-liquidity reporting; local open-position/capital rules apply separately.

#### UK (PRA)
No delta.

#### EU
No delta.

#### US
Regulation YY requires covered firms to reflect currency exposure in cash-flow projections and monitor significant-currency liquidity where appropriate.

<Recall
  q="How can a bank be liquid in aggregate but fail in USD?"
  a="Its usable liquidity may be in another currency and the conversion, swap or transfer channel may fail before USD settlement."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can FX-swap funding remain risky after spot FX is hedged?"
  a="The swap may mature before the asset, forcing rollover in a market whose basis and availability can deteriorate."
/>

<Recall
  q="What does a structural capital hedge protect?"
  a="The home-currency capital ratio from exchange-rate changes in foreign operations or net assets."
/>
