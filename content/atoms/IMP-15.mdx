### Flow

```text
Historical experience
    ↓
Current conditions
    ↓
Reasonable and supportable forecasts
    ↓
Scenario-specific PD / EAD / LGD / staging
    ↓
Scenario-specific ECL
    ↓
Probability weighting
    ↓
Final forward-looking ECL
```

### Formula

```text
Probability-weighted ECL = sum over s [Probability_s × ECL_s]

Probability_s = probability assigned to economic scenario s.

ECL_s = ECL calculated under internally consistent assumptions for scenario s.

Scenario probabilities across the modelled set = 100%.
```

### Core mechanism

| Design question | Required discipline |
| :--- | :--- |
| Which macro variables? | Use variables with defensible connection to portfolio credit outcomes |
| How many scenarios? | Enough to capture material alternative outcomes and non-linearity |
| How assign probabilities? | Governed economic judgement, not reverse-engineering a desired allowance |
| What beyond supportable forecast horizon? | Use a disciplined extrapolation or reversion method |
| Where does FLI enter? | PD, LGD, EAD, staging or combinations, without double-counting |

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Historical data · Current conditions · Macroeconomic forecasts
This:       FLI · Scenarios · Probability weights
Downstream: Scenario ECL · Overlays · Allowance
```

### Permanent rule

Forward-looking information belongs in ECL through a demonstrable connection to expected credit outcomes.

### Never confuse [Forecast horizon] and [ECL horizon]

**Forecast horizon** — period over which specific economic forecasts remain reasonable and supportable.

---

**ECL horizon** — period over which the framework requires expected losses to be measured.

The ECL horizon can be longer.

### Permanent trap

Calculating ECL once using probability-weighted average macro inputs can miss non-linearity. Weighted inputs and weighted losses are not generally the same calculation.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP is not a scenario-weighted ECL regime. RBI’s framework from 1 April 2027 requires forward-looking macroeconomic information and multiple scenarios.

#### UK (UKEB)
No accounting delta from IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
No accounting delta; EBA guidance reinforces robust forward-looking estimation.

#### US (FASB)
CECL incorporates historical experience, current conditions and reasonable and supportable forecasts, with reversion to historical information beyond the supportable forecast period.
