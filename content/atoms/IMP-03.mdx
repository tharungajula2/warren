### Core mechanism

| IFRS 9 exposure | ECL treatment | Presentation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Debt asset at amortised cost | In scope | Loss allowance reduces carrying amount |
| Debt asset at FVOCI | In scope | Allowance recognised in OCI; fair-value carrying amount is not reduced |
| Loan commitment in scope | In scope | Provision rather than asset deduction |
| Financial guarantee contract in scope | In scope | Provision rather than asset deduction |
| Trade receivable / contract asset | In scope | Simplified lifetime-ECL approach may apply |
| Lease receivable | In scope | Simplified lifetime-ECL policy may apply |
| Instrument at FVTPL | Generally outside ECL impairment model | Credit effects are reflected through fair-value measurement |

### Flow

```text
Instrument
    ↓
Classification and measurement basis
    ↓
Inside impairment scope?
    ↓
Yes → determine ECL horizon → recognise allowance / provision
No  → apply governing measurement basis
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Instrument type · Accounting classification · Measurement basis
This:       ECL scope · Balance-sheet presentation
Downstream: Staging · Measurement · Disclosure
```

### Permanent rule

Do not calculate an ECL allowance until the instrument’s accounting classification and impairment scope are known.

### Never confuse [FVOCI allowance] and [Asset carrying amount reduction]

**FVOCI allowance** — credit loss is recognised through the impairment mechanism and OCI.

---

**Asset carrying amount** — remains at fair value; the allowance does not reduce that reported fair-value amount.

### Permanent trap

Applying ECL to every item labelled “credit exposure” ignores instruments whose credit risk is already captured through another measurement basis.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank provisioning scope is governed by IRACP rather than IFRS 9 classification. From 1 April 2027, RBI’s bank ECL framework covers loans, investments, undrawn commitments and other specified credit exposures, with prudential floors.

#### UK (UKEB)
No delta from UK-adopted IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission)
No accounting delta from EU-adopted IFRS 9.

#### US (FASB)
ASC 326 applies CECL to financial assets measured at amortised cost and has separate credit-loss requirements for available-for-sale debt securities. Off-balance-sheet credit exposures are also addressed where the entity is exposed to credit risk.

<Recall
  q="Does an IFRS 9 FVOCI loss allowance reduce the asset’s fair-value carrying amount?"
  a="No — the asset remains at fair value while the allowance is recognised through OCI."

<Recall

  a="No — credit effects are already reflected through fair-value measurement."
