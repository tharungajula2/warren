### Core mechanism

Escalation is not failure. It is the mechanism for moving a decision to the correct level of authority.

| Decision | Keep local when | Escalate when |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Requirement wording | Owner and SMEs agree within approved scope | Meaning changes obligation or creates cross-function conflict |
| Design choice | Solution owner can satisfy agreed constraints | Choice changes business outcome, control or accepted risk |
| Scope trade-off | Within delegated tolerance | Mandatory outcome, deadline or major dependency is affected |
| Defect disposition | Existing tolerance clearly covers it | Release consequence or residual risk requires new acceptance |
| Go-live readiness | Criteria are met | Criteria are waived, evidence is incomplete or material risk remains |

```text
Question -&gt; Named owner -&gt; Decision within mandate
                    |
                    -&gt; Outside mandate -&gt; Escalation -&gt; Authorised forum -&gt; Recorded decision
```

The BA prepares escalation so governance receives a decision, not a data dump: issue, options, consequences, recommendation if appropriate, decision required, owner and latest decision date. The decision date matters because an answer delivered after build or test has already depended on it is not governance; it is retrospective documentation.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Stakeholder mandates · Open issues · Delivery constraints
This:       Governance routing · Escalation · Decision authority
Downstream: Decision log · Requirements baseline · Scope control · Release decisions
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA frames decision papers and records outcomes |
| Approves | Forum chair or authorised decision-maker approves within delegated mandate |
| Consumes | Workstreams consume the decision and resulting constraints |
| Independently challenges | Relevant control functions challenge decisions affecting their risk or control mandate |

### Permanent rule

Every escalation must ask for a specific decision from a forum authorised to make it. Escalating information without a decision request creates visibility, not resolution.

### Never confuse [Escalation] and [Delegation]

**Escalation** - moves a decision upward or across because the current owner lacks mandate, agreement or tolerance.

---

**Delegation** - grants decision authority downward within defined boundaries.

### Permanent trap

Using senior governance to settle every detailed question. That slows delivery and weakens accountability because people stop making decisions within their own mandate.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta.
