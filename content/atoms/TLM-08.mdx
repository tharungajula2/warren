### Core mechanism

For each time bucket:

Gap = rate-sensitive assets − rate-sensitive liabilities

| Gap sign | Simplified interpretation | What can overturn it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Positive | more assets reprice than liabilities | beta, basis, floors, curve shape, new business |
| Negative | more liabilities reprice than assets | same |
| Near zero | timing appears matched | basis and options can still dominate |

A cumulative gap sums bucket gaps to a chosen horizon. The representation is useful because concentrations become visible: a bank can be near zero in aggregate while carrying a large short-term negative gap and a later positive gap.

But a bucket discards detail. Cash flows at the start and end of the same interval are treated alike, and a gap does not say by how many basis points each rate will move. Deposit beta and lag can make an overnight contractual liability behave much more slowly in NII than the legal term suggests.

### Flow

```text
Positions
    ↓
Next effective repricing date
    ↓
Time buckets
    ↓
RSA − RSL by bucket
    ↓
Concentration diagnosis
    ↓
Scenario model for ΔNII / ΔEVE
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   NII/EVE dual view · behavioural assumptions
This:       First-order map of repricing timing
Downstream: Duration/EVE sensitivity · NMDs · stress scenarios
```

### Formula

```text
Gap_i = RSA_i − RSL_i

RSA_i — rate-sensitive assets next repricing in bucket i.

RSL_i — rate-sensitive liabilities next repricing in bucket i.

Cumulative gap_n = sum of Gap_i from first bucket through n.
```

### Permanent rule

Repricing gaps locate timing mismatch; they do not capture the full size or sign of IRRBB under realistic scenarios.

### Never confuse [Repricing gap] and [Liquidity gap]

**Repricing gap** — difference in positions resetting interest rates in a time bucket.

---

**Liquidity gap** — difference in cash inflows and cash outflows over time; repricing can occur without cash movement.

### Permanent trap

Multiplying every gap by one parallel shock and calling the result “IRRBB”. That ignores basis, curve shape, options and behavioural repricing.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Bucket definitions and reporting templates vary; the analytical mechanism does not.

<Recall
  q="What does a positive repricing gap tell you?"
  a="More assets than liabilities reset in that bucket; it does not by itself predict the earnings outcome."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can a near-zero gap still be risky?"
  a="The two sides may reference different indices or contain options and behavioural responses."
