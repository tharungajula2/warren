### Core mechanism

| Model | Enterprise centre | Domain or business units | Main strength | Main failure mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Centralised | Makes most data decisions and operates standards | Execute centrally set decisions | Consistency | Bottleneck and weak business ownership |
| Federated | Sets minimum policy and coordination | Make substantial domain decisions | Domain expertise and speed | Conflicting definitions and duplicated controls |
| Hybrid | Owns enterprise standards, architecture and escalation | Own definitions, quality and delivery within standards | Balance of consistency and accountability | Ambiguous decision boundary |

A workable model assigns each decision before a dispute occurs:

```text
Enterprise policy and architecture
    ↓
Domain ownership and stewardship
    ↓
Operational controls and delivery
    ↓
Issue escalation or cross-domain conflict
    ↓
Enterprise decision where local resolution fails
```

Committees are mechanisms, not ownership. A data council can resolve cross-domain conflicts, but the committee should not become a place where accountability is diffused among attendees.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Owner · Steward · Producer · Consumer · Challenge
This:       Decision rights and governance forums
Downstream: CDE standards · Data quality · Issue escalation · Change governance
```

### Ownership

| Layer | Typical decision |
| :--- | :--- |
| Board / senior management | Framework, risk appetite, material remediation priorities |
| Enterprise data governance | Common policy, taxonomy, architecture principles, cross-domain conflicts |
| Domain owner | Definitions, quality requirements, local priorities and exceptions |
| Stewardship | Metadata, monitoring, issue coordination and evidence |
| Independent challenge | Whether design and operation are effective |

### Permanent rule

A governance model is complete only when a person can determine who decides, who executes, who must be consulted and where unresolved conflict escalates.

### Permanent trap

Measuring governance maturity by committee count. More forums can increase delay while leaving the actual decision right undefined.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Regulators expect effective governance but do not impose one universal organisational design.

<Recall
  q="What distinguishes federated governance from uncontrolled local ownership?"
  a="Federation preserves enterprise policies, minimum standards and escalation while delegating defined decisions to domains; uncontrolled local ownership lacks that binding framework."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why should a data council not be the owner of every CDE?"
  a="Collective forums are poor substitutes for named accountability. The council can set standards and resolve conflicts while a specific owner remains accountable for each governed domain."
