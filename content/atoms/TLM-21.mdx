Assume treasury can raise three-month funds at one internal cost and five-year funds at a higher cost. A flat FTP rate would charge both a three-month asset and a five-year asset the same amount, despite the longer asset creating substantially more refinancing and liquidity exposure.

Under **matched-maturity FTP**, the internal rate is selected from the FTP curve at the product’s effective funding tenor. A five-year bullet loan is therefore charged an internal five-year transfer rate; a shorter exposure receives a shorter rate. Amortising assets can be decomposed into cash-flow strips or assigned a blended rate reflecting their repayment profile.

On the liability side, a stable source of funding receives a credit related to the tenor of funding value it provides. A long-term issued liability can therefore earn a higher transfer credit than unstable overnight funding if the term structure and liquidity economics warrant it.

The critical word is **effective**. Contractual maturity is appropriate only when contractual cash flows reasonably represent economic behaviour. Prepayable loans require expected prepayment assumptions. Non-maturity deposits require behavioural tenor. Revolving commitments require contingent rather than simply funded treatment.

The FSI’s liquidity transfer-pricing work identifies matched-maturity marginal funding costs as better practice and notes that amortising and non-maturing products require blended marginal rates. It specifically criticises one-average-rate approaches because they fail to penalise longer-term funding commitments or reward longer-term funding benefits.

Matched-maturity FTP does not mean treasury actually issues an external bond matching every customer transaction. The transfer is a pricing construct: it allocates the economic cost of the tenor while treasury centrally optimises the actual funding portfolio.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Funding cost should follow the effective tenor of the exposure, not the accounting period in which profitability is reported.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Charging every asset the current overnight funding rate because treasury happens to be funding the bank overnight today.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Matched-maturity FTP" b="Matched external funding">
**Matched-maturity FTP** — internal pricing against funding economics of comparable effective tenor.
---
**Matched external funding** — actually issuing external funding that mirrors an individual asset’s cash flows.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Matched-maturity pricing is an economic allocation principle rather than a jurisdiction-specific formula.
</Rule>
