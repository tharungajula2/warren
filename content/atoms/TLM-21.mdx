### Core mechanism

| Stress channel | Recalculate under the scenario |
| :--- | :--- |
| Funding behaviour | deposit runoff · wholesale rollover |
| Contingent outflows | commitment draws · derivative/collateral calls |
| Asset monetisation | sale/repo capacity after stressed haircuts and market depth |
| Transferability | currency · legal entity · encumbrance · settlement timing |
| Management actions | only actions executable inside the horizon |

Run idiosyncratic, market-wide and combined scenarios; combined = institution pressure + market dysfunction.

### Flow

```text
Stress narrative
    ↓
Behavioural + market assumptions worsen
    ↓
Stressed cash inflows/outflows
    ↓
Usable counterbalancing capacity
    ↓
Cumulative liquidity position
    ↓
First uncovered obligation = survival-horizon breach
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Liquidity ladder · LCR · NSFR · behavioural model
This:       Institution-specific stressed cash path
Downstream: Intraday stress · collateral · CFP · funding strategy
```

### Formula

```text
Stressed liquidity position_t = opening liquidity + stressed inflows_t − stressed outflows_t + executable counterbalancing actions_t

Survival horizon = first t where stressed liquidity position_t &lt; 0

Outflows ↑ or haircuts ↑ → survival horizon shortens.

Credible available liquidity ↑ → survival horizon extends.
```

### Permanent rule

There is no meaningful survival-horizon number without the scenario, currencies, assumptions and permitted management actions that generate it.

### Permanent trap

Counting the same collateral in a repo, asset sale and central-bank facility at once, or assuming a management action can be executed instantly because it appears in the contingency plan.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI liquidity guidance requires regular stress testing across bank-specific and market-wide stresses, use of results in liquidity strategy and contingency planning, and allows risk tolerance to be expressed through minimum survival horizons.

#### UK (PRA)
PRA supervision combines firm ILAAP assessment with Pillar 2 liquidity. The PRA explicitly assesses liquidity risks beyond LCR, including cash-flow mismatch and intraday risk.

#### EU
No mechanism delta. CRD/CRR and supervisory review require internal liquidity adequacy and stress assessment beyond minimum ratios.

#### US
Regulation YY requires covered firms to conduct liquidity stress tests and maintain a liquidity buffer based on those tests; assumptions and methodologies are subject to governance and independent review.

<Recall
  q="What creates a survival-horizon breach?"
  a="The first point at which stressed obligations exceed credible usable resources under the specified scenario."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why should behavioural assumptions worsen in liquidity stress?"
  a="Depositor, borrower and market behaviour changes under stress; holding base-case behaviour constant understates the cash problem."
/>
