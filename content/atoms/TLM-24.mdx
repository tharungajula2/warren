### Core mechanism

| CFP layer | What must be executable |
| :--- | :--- |
| Detect + invoke | warnings · triggers · activation authority |
| Act | prioritised funding/buffer/asset/business actions |
| Quantify | amount · currency · haircut · tenor · settlement |
| Operate | collateral · documentation · systems · counterparties |
| Govern | communications · escalation · drills/testing |

### Flow

```text
Early-warning signal
    ↓
Trigger / management judgement
    ↓
Crisis governance activated
    ↓
Prioritised funding and liquidity actions
    ↓
Execution + communication
    ↓
Recalculate survival horizon
    ↓
Escalate / de-escalate
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Liquidity stress · intraday · collateral capacity
This:       Operational response to liquidity crisis
Downstream: Funding strategy lessons · ALCO governance
```

### Ownership

| Role | Owns |
| :--- | :--- |
| Treasury | liquidity execution and market actions |
| Crisis/ALCO governance | invocation and prioritisation |
| Operations | settlement and collateral movement |
| Communications / senior management | stakeholder messaging |
| Independent risk | challenge of assumptions and triggers |
| Board / risk committee | plan oversight and approval as applicable |

### Permanent rule

A contingency funding source counts only to the extent it can be accessed in the stress, currency, entity and time horizon in which it is claimed.

### Never confuse [Liquidity stress test] and [Contingency funding plan]

**Liquidity stress test** — quantifies the stressed cash problem.

---

**Contingency funding plan** — specifies who does what, when and with which executable resources to address it.

### Permanent trap

Adding every theoretical funding source together without recognising that several actions may rely on the same collateral, market or decision window.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI requires a formal CFP with responsibilities, escalation procedures and regular testing within its liquidity-risk framework.

#### UK (PRA)
CFP capability forms part of the broader PRA liquidity/ILAAP supervisory assessment.

#### EU
No mechanism delta. Contingency planning is part of internal liquidity adequacy and supervisory expectations.

#### US
Regulation YY expressly requires covered bank holding companies to maintain and update a CFP, define stress events and invocation circumstances, identify funding sources and test operational components.

<Recall
  q="What turns a list of funding sources into a CFP?"
  a="Triggers, decision rights, quantified executable capacity, operations, communications and testing."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why must the survival horizon be recalculated after a CFP action?"
  a="The action changes cash resources, encumbrance or future obligations and may create new constraints."
