### Core mechanism

| Question | Current treatment |
| :--- | :--- |
| Who prudentially regulates HFCs? | RBI |
| Are HFCs banks? | No; they are a distinct NBFC category |
| Does SBR matter? | Yes; HFCs sit within the SBR architecture rather than outside it |
| Do housing-specific conditions disappear? | No; HFC status carries activity-specific requirements |
| What remains of NHB? | Policy, development and refinance functions continue, but HFC prudential supervision sits with RBI |

The Finance Act changes made in 2019 transferred HFC regulatory authority to RBI. Subsequent RBI harmonisation brought HFC prudential treatment closer to other NBFCs while preserving housing-finance-specific eligibility and business rules.

RBI reorganised entity-wise instructions on 28 November 2025 and amended parts of the HFC/NBFC estate in 2026. The exact current post-consolidation HFC instrument reference is UNVERIFIED here as at 31 August 2026. The pack therefore states the regulatory position without importing any legacy threshold or ratio that has not been established.

### Permanent rule

An HFC is not outside NBFC regulation merely because its business is specialised housing finance.

### Never confuse [Housing Finance Company] and [National Housing Bank]

**Housing Finance Company** — an RBI-regulated lender whose business is predominantly housing finance under the applicable definition.

---

**National Housing Bank** — a housing-finance development and refinance institution; it is not the prudential supervisor replacing RBI for HFCs.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### HFCs
Housing-specific business tests and applicable prudential requirements apply.

#### Other NBFCs
General NBFC rules apply unless another activity-specific category modifies them.
