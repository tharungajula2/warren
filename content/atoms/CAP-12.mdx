### Core mechanism

```text
Credit risk RWA ─────────┐
Market risk RWA ─────────┤
Operational risk RWA ────┼→ Total risk-based RWA
CVA risk requirement ────┘   under the applicable framework
```

| Engine | Risk captured | Typical object |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Credit risk | Obligor, issuer or counterparty failure to perform | Banking-book loans, securities, counterparty exposures |
| Market risk | Adverse moves in market risk factors | Trading-book positions |
| Operational risk | Failed processes, people, systems or external events | Bank-wide operational activity |
| CVA risk | Change in credit valuation adjustment | Covered derivative counterparty positions |

CVA risk is not merely default risk under another name. Counterparty default capital addresses failure to perform; CVA capital addresses valuation changes caused by counterparty credit spread and related risk before or without default.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Risk weights · exposure measures
This:       Multiple RWA engines
Downstream: Standardised credit risk · IRB · market/operational/CVA aggregation
```

### Permanent rule

Each risk engine must be measured under its own prudential methodology before aggregation.

### Never confuse [Counterparty default risk] and [CVA risk]

**Counterparty default risk** — loss because the counterparty fails to perform.

---

**CVA risk** — loss from changes in the market value of the credit valuation adjustment.

### Permanent trap

Applying a loan-style risk weight to operational or market risk ignores that those risk types have separate capital methodologies.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in the architecture. Implementation dates and detailed approaches can differ; those differences belong to local rulebooks.

  a="Different risk types require different prudential measurement engines before aggregation."
