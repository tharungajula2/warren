### Core mechanism

Secured funding capacity for an asset is approximately:

Funding capacity = eligible collateral value × (1 − haircut)

Then reduce for assets already encumbered or operationally unavailable.

| Driver | Effect on capacity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Market value ↓ | capacity ↓ |
| Haircut ↑ | capacity ↓ |
| Asset becomes ineligible | capacity → zero for that channel |
| Encumbrance ↑ | unpledged capacity ↓ |
| Pre-positioning completed | executable capacity ↑ |
| Currency mismatch | may require FX conversion/swap before use |

Collateral calls create a second-order stress: market moves reduce asset values or derivative positions, more collateral must be posted, encumbrance rises, and the remaining pool available for emergency borrowing shrinks.

### Flow

```text
Asset inventory
    ↓
Eligibility by funding channel
    ↓
Market value × haircut
    ↓
Subtract encumbered / unavailable assets
    ↓
Operationally executable borrowing capacity
    ↓
Stress haircuts + margin calls
    ↓
Revised capacity
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Liquidity inventory · intraday · structural FX
This:       Translation from assets to secured cash capacity
Downstream: CFP · funding strategy · stress management
```

### Formula

```text
Secured funding capacity = eligible collateral value × (1 − haircut)

Haircut ↑ → cash advanced per unit of collateral ↓.

Encumbrance ↑ → collateral optionality ↓.
```

### Permanent rule

Collateral value is not liquidity until a specific funding channel can turn it into cash within the required time.

### Never confuse [Encumbered asset] and [Unencumbered HQLA]

**Encumbered asset** — already pledged, secured or otherwise constrained so it cannot freely support another liquidity action.

---

**Unencumbered HQLA** — qualifying liquid asset available to the bank subject to the applicable operational requirements.

### Permanent trap

Reporting a large “collateral pool” without showing which legal entity owns it, which currency it supports, how long mobilisation takes and which assets are already pledged.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in mechanism. Eligibility schedules, central-bank collateral frameworks and supervisory reporting differ.

<Recall
  q="Why does a higher repo haircut reduce liquidity?"
  a="The lender advances less cash against the same collateral market value."

<Recall
