### Core mechanism

```text
Detect defect or control breach
    ↓
Assess materiality, affected data and consumers
    ↓
Contain immediate reporting or decision risk
    ↓
Assign owner and perform root-cause analysis
    ↓
Design and implement permanent remediation
    ↓
Retest affected chain and regression scope
    ↓
Validate evidence → close or escalate
```

| Issue record must answer | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| What data and reports are affected? | Defines impact and consumers |
| Since when? | Determines historical exposure and possible restatement |
| What is the root cause? | Prevents cosmetic fixes |
| What containment exists? | Controls current risk while remediation is open |
| Who is accountable and by when? | Creates enforceable ownership |
| What proves closure? | Stops tickets closing on assertion alone |

Root causes can sit at source capture, master/reference data, definition, interface, transformation, manual adjustment or governance. The issue should be assigned to the party able to remove the cause, while the data owner remains accountable for the risk to the governed domain.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   DQ breach · Reconciliation break · Consumer challenge · Production defect
This:       Issue containment and root-cause remediation
Downstream: Change requirements · Retest · Governance escalation · Restatement assessment
```

### Never confuse [Workaround] and [Remediation]

**Workaround** - temporary control or manual action that contains immediate impact while the defect remains.

---

**Remediation** - change that removes or durably controls the root cause and is validated before closure.

### Permanent rule

Closure evidence must demonstrate the root cause is resolved or that any residual risk is explicitly accepted through the proper governance route.

### Permanent trap

Measuring success by ticket closure rate. Fast closure can conceal recurring issues if fixes address symptoms rather than sources.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Supervisory escalation mechanisms vary, but sound issue lifecycle mechanics do not.

  a="It can be valid containment to protect an immediate decision or filing, but it is not permanent remediation unless it also removes the underlying cause."
