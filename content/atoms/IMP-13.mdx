### Formula

```text
EAD_t = expected drawn balance_t + expected future draw_t

Where a CCF architecture is used:

Expected future draw_t = CCF_t × undrawn amount_t

EAD_t = exposure expected if default occurs in period t.

CCF_t = expected proportion of an undrawn amount drawn before default.

Undrawn amount_t = available but not yet drawn credit.

EAD ↑ when expected utilisation before default rises.
```

### Core mechanism

| Facility | Main EAD question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Amortising term loan | How much principal remains at each date? |
| Bullet loan | Does exposure remain high until maturity? |
| Undrawn commitment | How much will be drawn before default? |
| Revolving facility | How do drawdown, repayment, limit management and cancellation behave? |

### Flow

```text
Current balance and limit
    ↓
Amortisation / prepayment
    ↓
Expected new drawings
    ↓
Future exposure profile
    ↓
Possible default date
    ↓
EAD at that date
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Facility terms · Limits · Repayment · Prepayment · Drawdown behaviour
This:       EAD path · Behavioural exposure period
Downstream: Period ECL · Capital / provisioning exposure measures
```

### Permanent rule

EAD must correspond to the exposure expected at the same future default date represented by PD.

### Never confuse [Contractual notice period] and [Behavioural exposure period]

**Contractual notice period** — legal period over which cancellation or repayment rights operate.

---

**Behavioural exposure period** — period credit exposure is expected to persist given actual behaviour and risk-management actions.

### Permanent trap

Using today’s balance for every future period ignores amortisation and stress-driven drawdown. Using a nominal cancellation right as the horizon without considering actual revolving behaviour can also understate risk.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI’s bank ECL framework from 1 April 2027 addresses drawn and undrawn exposures and applies prudential floors to undrawn commitments after relevant conversion.

#### UK (UKEB)
IFRS 9 contains a specific exposure-period exception for qualifying revolving instruments whose credit exposure is not effectively limited by the contractual notice period.

#### EU (European Commission)
No accounting delta from IFRS 9.

#### US (FASB)
CECL considers the contractual exposure period for off-balance-sheet commitments; no allowance is recorded for commitments the institution can unconditionally cancel.
