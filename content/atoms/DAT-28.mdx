### Core mechanism

| Form | Who files it | What it covers | Cadence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| FFIEC 031 | Banks with domestic and foreign offices | Call Report: condition, income and supporting schedules | Quarterly |
| FFIEC 041 | Banks with domestic offices only that do not use FFIEC 051 | Call Report: condition, income and supporting schedules | Quarterly |
| FFIEC 051 | Eligible banks with domestic offices only and total assets below the form's threshold; current FFIEC description states less than USD 5 billion | Streamlined Call Report: condition, income and supporting schedules | Quarterly |
| FR Y-9C | BHCs, SLHCs, IHCs and SHCs meeting the respondent criteria; current page states generally USD 3 billion or more, subject to specified exceptions | Consolidated financial statements for holding companies | Quarterly |
| FR Y-14A | Covered large firms in the FR Y-14 panel | Capital-assessment and stress-testing projections | Annual |
| FR Y-14Q | Covered large firms in the FR Y-14 panel | Detailed asset, capital and PPNR data, with schedule-specific exceptions | Quarterly |
| FR Y-14M | Applicable firms in the FR Y-14 panel with relevant portfolios | Detailed retail loan portfolio data | Monthly |

As at August 2026, FFIEC 031/041/051 current materials include June 2026 forms or instructions.

### Never confuse [US form family] and [Taxonomy-driven reporting framework]

**US form family** - a named collection with defined respondent panel, schedules and instructions; applicability is determined form by form.

---

**Taxonomy-driven reporting framework** - a broader technical reporting model where templates and data points are released through a common taxonomy architecture. The EU example is closer to this model.

### Permanent rule

Never infer US filing obligations from a bank label alone; use the current respondent panel and instructions for the specific form and reference date.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### US (FFIEC / Federal Reserve)
The real delta is form selection, respondent panel and cadence. FFIEC Call Reports, FR Y-9C and FR Y-14 serve different entities and supervisory purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable versions of one return.

<Recall
  q="What determines whether FFIEC 031, 041 or 051 applies?"
  a="Institution profile and the current Call Report eligibility instructions, including office structure and the FFIEC 051 asset criterion; the bank must use the form applicable to its reporting status."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why is FR Y-14 not simply another Call Report?"
  a="FR Y-14 is a Federal Reserve capital-assessment and stress-testing information collection for the covered large-firm panel, with annual, quarterly and monthly components; Call Reports are depository-institution condition and income reports."
