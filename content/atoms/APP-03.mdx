## Stationarity — The Key Statistical Decision

Raw series:

<Flow>
ADF p = 1.00
→ non-stationary
</Flow>

First difference:

<Flow>
ADF p = 0.1167
→ still non-stationary
</Flow>

Regular + seasonal difference:

<Flow>
ADF p ≈ 0
→ stationary
</Flow>

Therefore:

```text
d = 1
D = 1
m = 12
```

Model family:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle SARIMA(p,1,q)(P,1,Q)_{12}" />
