### Core mechanism

Separate disagreements by type before negotiating them.

| Conflict type | What is really disputed | BA response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fact | What happens today or what evidence shows | Establish evidence |
| Interpretation | What a rule or policy means | Obtain authoritative interpretation |
| Objective | Which outcome matters more | Escalate to business authority |
| Constraint | What is feasible by date or platform | Validate constraint and options |
| Risk appetite | Which exposure may be accepted | Route to authorised risk owner |
| Preference | Which option is more convenient | Decide against agreed criteria |

```text
Positions -&gt; Underlying interests -&gt; Evidence -&gt; Options -&gt; Consequences -&gt; Authorised decision
```

Do not translate disagreement into vague wording that lets each side believe it won. A requirement that says "timely processing with appropriate controls" may conceal an unresolved cutoff, approval rule or tolerance. Write the unresolved decision explicitly.

Where possible, create options that preserve mandatory outcomes while varying discretionary features, sequencing or operating model. If no option satisfies all mandates, the conflict belongs in governance.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Stakeholder mandates · SME evidence · Constraints
This:       Conflict classification · Options · Negotiation
Downstream: Decision log · Requirements · Scope · Escalation
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA frames conflict, criteria, options and consequences |
| Approves | Named decision authority selects the option |
| Consumes | All affected stakeholders and delivery teams consume the recorded outcome |
| Independently challenges | Control functions challenge options that weaken controls or exceed tolerances |

### Permanent rule

Consensus is useful but authority is decisive. A material requirement is settled when the authorised owner decides, not when the meeting becomes quiet.

### Never confuse [Consensus] and [Decision]

**Consensus** - participants broadly agree with an outcome.

---

**Decision** - an authorised role commits the organisation to an outcome.

### Permanent trap

Writing compromise language to avoid escalation. Ambiguity moves the conflict downstream, where developers, testers or operators make the decision accidentally.
