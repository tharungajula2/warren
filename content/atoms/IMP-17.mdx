ECL validation should cover more than a final allowance number. A model can produce a plausible aggregate ECL while its segmentation, PD term structure, LGD recovery timing or EAD behaviour is wrong. Validation therefore works from inputs through methodology to outputs.

A practical sequence begins with **conceptual soundness**: does the methodology correspond to the accounting objective, portfolio economics and available data? Next comes implementation testing: are source data, transformations, code, parameter mappings and aggregation correct? Quantitative performance testing then evaluates discrimination, calibration, stability and realised outcomes where relevant. Finally, governance testing checks documentation, change control, limitations, approval and whether users understand the model’s intended boundaries.

**Backtesting** compares model predictions with realised outcomes over appropriately defined periods. A PD backtest may compare forecast defaults with realised defaults; an LGD backtest can compare expected discounted recoveries with actual recovery experience; an EAD backtest can test drawdowns before default. Aggregate ECL-versus-write-off comparison is useful but insufficient because timing and portfolio composition can obscure component errors.

Repeated material overprediction or underprediction is evidence requiring investigation, not automatic proof that a particular calibration change is correct. US interagency guidance explicitly expects periodic validation independent of credit approval and ACL estimation, including conceptual-soundness review, ongoing monitoring and output analysis; it notes that persistent material forecast error may indicate weakness.

### Permanent rule

Validation must test the model against its intended use and underlying risk mechanics, not merely reproduce the developer’s calculation.

### Permanent trap

Declaring a model “validated” because aggregate provision is close to actual write-offs can hide offsetting PD, LGD, EAD or staging errors.

### Never confuse

**Validation** — independent challenge of conceptual soundness, implementation, performance and use.

---
**Backtesting** — outcomes analysis comparing model predictions with realised experience; it is a component of validation, not the whole process.

### Jurisdiction variants

**IN** — Current commercial-bank IRACP requires automated classification controls and periodic system audit; RBI’s ECL Directions from April 1, 2027 add an explicit model inventory, independent validation, pre-implementation validation and post-implementation backtesting framework. [RBI 2025 IRACP; RBI 2026 ECL Directions, paragraphs 119–131]
**UK** — PRA’s 2025 thematic feedback emphasises monitoring and validation of IFRS 9 ECL models and model operating boundaries. [PRA 2025 thematic feedback]
**EU** — EBA/GL/2017/06 provides supervisory guidance on sound credit-risk-management and accounting-ECL practices alongside IFRS 9. [EBA/GL/2017/06]
**US** — Interagency ACL guidance expects periodic independent validation and initial and ongoing model-validation activities, including conceptual soundness, monitoring, benchmarking and model-output analysis. [Interagency ACL Policy Statement]
