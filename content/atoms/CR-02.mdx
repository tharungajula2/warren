A **product** is a standardised commercial form of credit: for example, a term-loan structure, revolving line, overdraft or card structure. It defines broad contractual and operational characteristics but does not by itself identify a particular credit obligation.

A **facility** is the actual contractual availability of credit to a particular obligor or set of obligors. It has terms such as limit, currency, maturity, repayment schedule, pricing, security and covenants. One obligor can have many facilities, and each facility can have different loss severity and exposure behaviour even when the obligor's probability of default is common.

An **account** is an operational or accounting record used to book balances, payments, arrears, interest and other activity. A simple loan may have one facility and one account. More complex structures may have multiple accounts under one facility, while some operational architectures represent a single economic exposure across several ledgers.

This distinction matters throughout credit risk. Probability of default is often principally associated with the obligor or homogeneous retail pool. Loss given default depends heavily on facility-specific collateral, seniority and recovery structure. Exposure at default depends on the particular facility's balance, limit and ability to draw further funds. Delinquency may first be observed on an account, while a regulatory default rule can propagate the consequence to the obligor or other facilities.

Basel's IRB architecture explicitly separates borrower default risk from transaction-specific characteristics, and retail portfolios may, under applicable rules, permit default recognition at an individual facility rather than across every exposure to the obligor.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Product, facility and account describe different levels. Any risk measure must declare the level to which it attaches.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
A delinquent account is sometimes treated as though it were automatically a delinquent obligor, without checking whether the applicable default framework is facility-level or obligor-level.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse>
**Facility** — a specific contractual grant or commitment of credit.

**Account** — an operational record used to book and service activity; it may represent all, part or more than one component of a facility.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Regulatory systems differ in when facility-level status propagates to other facilities, but the underlying object hierarchy is universal.
</Rule>
