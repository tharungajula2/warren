### Core mechanism

| Level | Owns | Typical examples of fields |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Product | Reusable commercial design | Product type, standard terms, target segment |
| Facility | Actual contractual credit availability | Limit, currency, maturity, repayment schedule, covenants, security |
| Account | Operational booking and servicing | Balance, postings, due dates, arrears, interest, fees |
| Obligor | Person responsible for obligations | Rating, default state, aggregate limits |

A simple term loan may be one facility with one account. A revolving facility can have several operational accounts. One obligor can hold facilities with different collateral, seniority and drawdown behaviour even when a common obligor PD is used.

<Flow>
Upstream:   Obligor · Product design · Credit decision
This:       Product → Facility → Account
Downstream: Exposure · Delinquency · LGD · EAD · Collections · Reporting
</Flow>

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Every risk measure must state its level: product, facility, account, obligor or a defined aggregate.
</Rule>

<NeverConfuse a="Facility" b="Account">
**Facility** — the contractual grant or commitment of credit, including its limit and terms.

---
**Account** — the operational record through which balances, payments and arrears are booked; one facility need not equal one account.
</NeverConfuse>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Treating an account-level delinquency flag as automatically equivalent to an obligor-level credit state can misclassify other facilities when the governing default framework applies a different propagation rule.
</Trap>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Regulatory rules differ on when account or facility events propagate to an obligor, but the object hierarchy itself is universal.
</Rule>

<Recall
  q="Which object owns the sanctioned limit and contractual maturity?"
  a="The facility."
/>

<Recall
  q="Which object normally records a missed instalment first?"
  a="The servicing account."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can one obligor have several LGDs?"
  a="Facility-specific collateral, seniority and recovery structures can differ."
/>
