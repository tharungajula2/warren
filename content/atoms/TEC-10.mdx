Dates can also be compared or ranged: e.g. `WHERE SaleDate BETWEEN '2025-01-01' AND '2025-12-31'` for a calendar year.

### Permanent rule

Convert strings to actual `DATE` or `DATETIME` types (using `STR_TO_DATE` or client-side parsing) to leverage date functions. Do not treat dates as plain text.

### Permanent trap

Ignoring the time component can yield unexpected results (e.g. missing same-day records). Always truncate times or use date boundaries.

### Never confuse

**DATE** — stores year-month-day (no time).

---

**DATETIME/TIMESTAMP** — includes time (hours-min-sec). Make sure to use the correct type for dates versus full timestamps.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Date arithmetic and functions follow SQL standards universally.

<Recall
  q="How to get quarterly totals?"
  a="`GROUP BY YEAR(date), DATEPART(quarter, date)` (or a fiscal calendar table)."
