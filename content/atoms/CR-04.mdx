### Core mechanism

| Connection test | Core question | Credit consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Control | Does one person directly or indirectly control another, or are several under common control? | Treat connected exposures as one concentration where the rule requires |
| Economic interdependence | Would financial difficulty at one likely cause funding or repayment difficulty at another? | Aggregate because apparent legal diversification is not economic diversification |
| Mere commercial relationship | Are entities simply customers/suppliers without material dependency? | Investigate; do not aggregate automatically |

Connection for concentration purposes is not the same as default propagation. Two companies can be aggregated for a large-exposure limit yet retain separate ratings and default statuses if the applicable default rules assess them separately.

### Flow

```text
Legal entities
    ↓
Control test OR economic-interdependence test
    ↓
Connected group → Aggregate exposure → Concentration limit / monitoring
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Legal entities · Ownership · Funding links · Revenue dependencies
This:       Test control OR economic interdependence → connected group
Downstream: Aggregate exposure · Large-exposure limits · Concentration monitoring
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Relationship teams and credit analysis identify ownership and dependency evidence |
| Approves | Credit risk / designated governance confirms connected-group treatment |
| Consumes | Limits, portfolio risk, regulatory reporting and capital assessment |
| Challenges | Independent risk, compliance and audit test completeness and consistency |

### Permanent rule

Count common failure mechanisms, not merely legal names.

### Never confuse [Connected counterparties] and [Single obligor]

**Connected counterparties** — separate persons aggregated because their risks are linked by control or economic dependency.

---

**Single obligor** — one legal or natural person responsible for obligations.

### Permanent trap

“Same corporate group” does not automatically mean “same defaulted obligor”, and separate incorporation does not automatically mean independent concentration risk.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI’s Large Exposures Framework applies Indian prudential aggregation and limits to single counterparties and groups of connected counterparties; use the RBI framework in force for the reporting date.

#### UK (PRA)
PRA SS3/25 sets expectations for groups of connected clients using control and economic dependency concepts.

#### EU
The CRR large-exposures framework uses groups of connected clients and tests based on control and economic interconnection.

#### US
US legal lending-limit and aggregation rules use US-specific control/common-enterprise tests; they should not be assumed identical to the Basel connected-counterparty test.
