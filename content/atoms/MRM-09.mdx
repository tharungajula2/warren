### Core mechanism

| Role | Produces | Approves | Consumes | Challenges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Developer | Design, code, tests | No | Sometimes | No |
| Owner / first line | Ownership, monitoring, remediation | Delegated non-validation decisions only | Yes | No |
| Business user | Use evidence, feedback, overrides | Only if separately delegated | Yes | No |
| Approval authority | Decision, conditions | Yes | May | Uses validation input |
| MRM / validation | Validation evidence | No business approval | No routine first-line use | Yes |
| Internal audit | Audit evidence | No | No | Framework effectiveness, not routine validation |

```text
Developer → owner/user → independent validation → approval authority → controlled use
Internal audit ↓ framework assurance
```

Independence is functional: incentives, authority and escalation must permit genuine challenge.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Lifecycle gates · Organisational design
This:       Role separation and accountability
Downstream: Development evidence · Validation · Approval · Audit
```

### Permanent rule

No role title creates independence by itself. Independence requires objective incentives, sufficient expertise, organisational standing and authority to escalate or effect change.

### Never confuse [Independent validation] and [Internal audit]

**Independent validation** - challenges specific model reliability, limitations and use as part of normal MRM.

---

**Internal audit** - independently assesses whether the MRM framework, policies and controls are designed and operating effectively; it does not ordinarily duplicate development or validation.

### Permanent trap

Calling the second line "the model owner". The owner remains accountable for the model; independent challenge must not absorb first-line responsibility merely because it can block or condition use.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Accountability | Independence / status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US | Clear accountability; no prescribed organisation. | Objective effective challenge with independence, standing and influence; conflicts managed. |
| UK | Board/senior-management accountability; appropriate SMF holder(s) own overall MRM. | Independent validation alongside accountable management. |
| IN | Proposed three lines: owners; independent MRM/validation; internal audit, plus Board/RMCB duties. | Draft as at August 2026; not final. |
