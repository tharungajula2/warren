Regulation (EU) 2024/1623, commonly called **CRR3**, implements major elements of the final Basel III reforms in the EU capital framework. The main package began applying on 1 January 2025, including revised treatment across credit, operational and CVA risk and the introduction of the output-floor architecture.

The output floor is intentionally phased. Article 465(1) CRR establishes a transition of 50% in 2025, **55% in 2026**, 60% in 2027, 65% in 2028, 70% in 2029 and **72.5% from 1 January 2030**. This means “EU output floor = 72.5%” is correct only as the fully phased destination, not the current 2026 calibration.

Market risk follows a different timeline. The Commission initially postponed binding FRTB own-funds requirements by one year and subsequently adopted a further postponement to **1 January 2027**. During postponement, pre-FRTB methodologies continue for the binding market-risk own-funds requirement, while the FRTB Standardised Approach remains relevant to the market-risk component of the output-floor calculation.

That distinction prevents a common implementation error: “FRTB postponed” does not mean every FRTB-derived calculation disappears from the EU prudential framework during the postponement.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
A postponed FRTB own-funds commencement does not repeal CRR3 or suspend the output floor.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Applying 72.5% as the EU output-floor multiplier in 2026 overstates the current transitional floor, which is 55%.
</Trap>
