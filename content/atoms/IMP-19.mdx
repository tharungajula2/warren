### Core mechanism

| Dimension | IFRS 9 general model | US CECL |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Initial allowance horizon | 12-month ECL | Lifetime expected loss |
| SICR gateway | Yes | No |
| Stage 1 / 2 / 3 | Yes | No equivalent allowance architecture |
| Measurement methods | Method-neutral | Method-neutral |
| Forward-looking information | Required | Historical experience + current conditions + reasonable and supportable forecasts |
| Beyond supportable forecast | Framework remains probability-weighted and reasonable/supportable | Reversion to historical loss information |
| Purchased impaired assets | POCI | PCD gross-up approach |

### Flow

```text
Origination / acquisition
    ↓
Lifetime expected credit loss recognised
    ↓
Each reporting date
    ↓
Update historical experience
+ current conditions
+ reasonable and supportable forecasts
    ↓
Re-estimate lifetime ACL
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   US GAAP scope · Contractual term · Credit data
This:       ASC 326 CECL
Downstream: ACL · Earnings · US disclosures
```

### Permanent rule

CECL’s absence of IFRS 9 staging does not mean credit deterioration is ignored. Deterioration changes a lifetime estimate that already exists.

### Never confuse [CECL lifetime allowance] and [IFRS 9 lifetime ECL]

**CECL lifetime allowance** — generally recognised from initial recognition for in-scope amortised-cost assets.

---

**IFRS 9 lifetime ECL** — generally begins after SICR, for credit-impaired assets, or where another IFRS 9 requirement specifies lifetime measurement.

### Permanent trap

Creating IFRS 9-style stages inside CECL and using them to decide whether lifetime ECL applies imports a recognition threshold ASC 326 does not contain.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
CECL does not govern Indian statutory bank provisioning. Commercial banks currently use IRACP and move to RBI’s own staged ECL framework from 1 April 2027.

#### UK (UKEB)
UK-adopted IFRS 9 retains the 12-month / lifetime staging architecture.

#### EU (European Commission)
EU-adopted IFRS 9 retains staging.

#### US (FASB)
ASC 326 is the governing US GAAP credit-loss framework. It permits multiple estimation methods and separately addresses available-for-sale debt securities and off-balance-sheet exposures; unconditionally cancellable commitments do not receive an off-balance-sheet CECL allowance.
