### Core mechanism

| Product | Contract says | ALM must estimate | Measures affected |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Non-maturity deposit | withdrawable on demand | stable balance, decay, beta, lag | NII, EVE, LCR/internal stress, FTP |
| Fixed-rate loan with prepayment right | maturity date fixed | scenario-dependent prepayment | EVE, NII, funding tenor, FTP |
| Term deposit with early break | stated maturity | redemption propensity | NII, EVE, liquidity |
| Revolving credit line | undrawn until customer acts | stress drawdown | liquidity, funding, FTP |
| Origination pipeline | expected future booking | fallout, draw timing, final notional | hedge requirement, NII/EVE |

Model governance therefore focuses on assumptions, not merely code. Each material behavioural assumption needs a definition, segmentation, evidence, owner, validation, sensitivity and stress treatment. A model can calculate perfectly and still be wrong because it gives contractual certainty to behaviour that disappears in stress.

### Flow

```text
Contractual position
    ↓
Behavioural assumption set
    ↓
Scenario-specific effective cash flows
    ↓
NII / EVE / liquidity / FTP outputs
    ↓
Validation + stress + management challenge
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   ALM map · probability/variance/tail-risk foundations
This:       Governed transformation from contract to expected behaviour
Downstream: NMD model · prepayment · commitments · stress testing · FTP
```

### Ownership

| Role | Produces / challenges |
| :--- | :--- |
| Treasury / ALM | economic specification and use |
| Model development / analytics | estimation and implementation |
| Business | product behaviour evidence |
| Independent model risk / risk | validation and challenge |
| ALCO / relevant committee | material assumptions and overlays within policy |

### Permanent rule

If a metric depends materially on customer behaviour, the assumption is part of the risk position, not a technical footnote.

### Permanent trap

Calling a behavioural estimate “the maturity” as though it were contractual fact. It is a governed hypothesis that can fail precisely when stress changes customer incentives.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in mechanism. Regulatory frameworks differ in the constraints, reporting and prescribed parameters applied to behavioural models.
