### Core mechanism

| Item | India position as at August 2026 |
| :--- | :--- |
| CET1 minimum | 5.5% of RWA |
| Tier 1 minimum | 7.0% of RWA |
| Total Capital minimum | 9.0% of RWA |
| Capital conservation buffer | 2.5% CET1 |
| Leverage ratio | 4% for D-SIBs; 3.5% for other covered commercial banks |
| Revised banking-book credit-risk approach | Final Standardised Approach; effective 1 Apr 2027 |
| New ECL provisioning framework | Final Directions; effective 1 Apr 2027 |
| ECL transition relief | Optional declining CET1 add-back through FY 2030-31 |

These are separate questions:

```text
Credit exposure
    ↓
RBI Standardised Approach
    ↓
Credit RWA
    ↓
Capital requirement

Same loan
    ↓
RBI ECL Directions
    ↓
Loss allowance
    ↓
Opening / ongoing earnings effect
    ↓
CET1 effect
    ↓
Temporary transition add-back if elected
```

RBI has explicitly selected the Standardised Approach for the revised commercial-bank credit-risk framework. IRB should therefore be taught as international architecture, not as India's new domestic method.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Basel-to-law · SA · ECL transition
This:       India's binding and future capital architecture
Downstream: Indian capital planning · reporting · implementation
```

### Permanent rule

Do not substitute Basel minima, IRB or accounting rules from another jurisdiction for RBI's binding commercial-bank directions.

### Never confuse [RBI credit-risk SA] and [RBI ECL framework]

**RBI credit-risk SA** — prudential methodology for banking-book credit RWA.

---

**RBI ECL framework** — provisioning and income-recognition regime measuring loss allowances.

### Permanent trap

Saying "ECL lowers RWA" confuses the provisioning regime with the risk-weight framework; ECL primarily affects carrying amounts, earnings and CET1, while the SA separately determines RWA.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
**As at August 2026:** capital adequacy Directions dated 28 November 2025 are in force. The SA Directions, RBI/DOR/2026-27/397, and ECL Directions, RBI/DOR/2026-27/398, are final and become effective 1 April 2027. The CET1 transition fractions then step down 4/5, 3/5, 2/5, 1/5 through FY 2030-31 and are nil thereafter.

<Recall
  q="What are India's current CET1, Tier 1 and Total Capital minima?"
  a="5.5%, 7% and 9% of RWA respectively, before applicable buffers and additions."
/>

<Recall
  q="What credit-risk method starts on 1 April 2027 under the final RBI Directions?"
  a="The Standardised Approach, not IRB."
/>

<Recall
  q="Does the ECL CET1 add-back cancel the allowance?"
  a="No; it temporarily changes regulatory capital recognition while ECL remains fully measured."
