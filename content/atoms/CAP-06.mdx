### Core mechanism

```text
Accounting common equity
    ↓
Eligibility tests
    ↓
Prudential filters
    ↓
Deductions and threshold treatments
    ↓
Recognised CET1
    ↓
CET1 / RWA
```

Common Basel adjustments include goodwill and specified intangible assets, certain deferred tax assets, own-credit effects, defined-benefit pension items and investments in the capital of financial institutions. Threshold treatments apply to specified items rather than making every such balance an automatic full deduction.

The common principle is availability. If an asset's value depends on the bank remaining profitable, or if the same capital is counted by two regulated entities, gross accounting equity can overstate the resources genuinely available in stress.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Accounting equity · CET1 eligibility
This:       Prudential filters and deductions
Downstream: Regulatory CET1 · capital ratios · provision shortfall
```

### Formula

```text
Regulatory CET1 = eligible CET1 elements − required deductions ± prudential filters

The exact adjustment set depends on the applicable rulebook.
```

### Permanent rule

A capital ratio uses qualifying regulatory capital after adjustments, not gross book equity.

### Never confuse [Book equity] and [Regulatory CET1]

**Book equity** — equity measured under the applicable accounting framework.

---

**Regulatory CET1** — highest-quality eligible capital after prudential filters, deductions and threshold treatments.

### Permanent trap

Treating goodwill as loss-absorbing capital because it increases accounting assets ignores that its stressed realisable value may vanish exactly when capital is needed.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in the principle. Detailed deductions, thresholds and transitional treatments are jurisdiction-specific implementation choices.
