### Core mechanism

```text
Corporate default occurs
    ↓
Eligible applicant files insolvency application
    ↓
Adjudicating Authority considers admission
    ↓
Admission triggers moratorium and insolvency process
    ↓
Resolution professional and committee of creditors govern the process
    ↓
Resolution plan or liquidation follows
```

| IBC object | Lending consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Admission | Starts the formal collective process |
| Moratorium | Stays specified individual enforcement and recovery actions |
| Committee of Creditors | Financial creditors exercise collective decision rights |
| Resolution plan | Can restructure debt, ownership and recoveries |
| Liquidation | Value is realised under the statutory waterfall if resolution fails |

The legacy pack's voting percentages, default threshold and timetable figures are not carried forward here because they were specifically treated as unverified. The Code and amendments govern those details; this atom preserves the mechanism without guessing numbers.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Failed resolution · Corporate default
This:       Collective insolvency
Downstream: Resolution plan · Liquidation · Recovery allocation
```

### Permanent rule

Once the insolvency proceeding is admitted and the moratorium applies, individual enforcement routes cannot simply continue unaffected.

### Never confuse [IBC filing] and [IBC admission]

**IBC filing** — an application has been submitted.

---

**IBC admission** — the adjudicating authority has admitted the case, triggering the statutory insolvency process and moratorium consequences.

### Permanent trap

Assuming a creditor vote is required before any financial creditor can file an application. Filing rights and later committee voting are separate statutory questions.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Corporate persons
Corporate insolvency runs through the National Company Law Tribunal architecture.

#### Individuals and other debtor categories
Different insolvency/recovery provisions and forums can apply; this atom does not import the corporate process automatically.
