### Core mechanism

```text
Qualifying PSL loan originated
    ↓
Loan remains on originating lender's balance sheet
    ↓
PSL achievement measured against target
    ↓
Surplus achievement may support PSLC sale
    ↓
Deficit bank may buy PSLC achievement without buying the loan
```

| Object | What transfers? | What stays? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Loan sale/assignment | Economic asset and agreed credit risk | Depends on transaction structure |
| PSLC | PSL achievement for the specified certificate category | Underlying loan and borrower credit risk stay with originator |

PSLCs exist in category forms aligned to the RBI framework. They allow a bank that is short in one target or sub-target to buy achievement from another institution that has excess qualifying lending.

Where an institution still has a shortfall after permitted adjustments, the RBI framework can require contribution or placement into specified development or financial-inclusion arrangements. Exact current shortfall mechanics depend on entity and the 2025 Directions as amended.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   PSL target · Eligible exposure
This:       PSL achievement management
Downstream: Compliance reporting · Shortfall treatment
```

### Never confuse [PSLC] and [Loan sale]

**PSLC** — a regulatory certificate transferring PSL achievement.

---

**Loan sale** — a transaction transferring an economic loan asset or interest in it.

### Permanent rule

Buying a PSLC does not make the buyer the creditor of the underlying borrower.

### Permanent trap

Using PSLC purchases as evidence that the buyer has acquired the corresponding credit exposure. The regulatory achievement moves; the loan does not.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Banks eligible for PSLC participation
Certificate use follows the current RBI PSL framework and the category of shortfall.

#### Entities outside the PSLC framework
No certificate treatment should be inferred merely because the entity makes priority-sector-like loans.
