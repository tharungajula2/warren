### Core mechanism

| Jurisdiction | Institution perimeter | Model perimeter | August 2026 status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US | Most relevant above USD 30bn; possible relevance below where model risk is significant | Traditional quantitative and qualifying non-generative, non-agentic AI models | Live supervisory guidance |
| UK | Specified PRA firms with internal model approval | SS1/23 principles across model and risk types in those firms | Live supervisory statement |
| EU | CRR institutions using permitted internal approaches | Internal credit, market and counterparty credit risk models | Live prudential framework |
| IN | Eleven proposed categories of RBI-regulated entities | All models, including third-party and AI/ML | Draft; consultation closed |

Entity → applicable instrument → model population → control obligations

A bank may voluntarily govern more models than the legal minimum. That policy choice does not make the regulatory perimeters identical.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Model definition · Entity type · Regulatory permissions
This:       Applicable MRM perimeter
Downstream: Inventory scope · Tiering · Governance · Validation obligations
```

### Permanent rule

Separate three questions: "Is this a model?", "Is this institution in scope?", and "Does this instrument apply?" A yes to one does not answer the others.

### Permanent trap

Importing one jurisdiction's requirement into another and presenting it as universal. Scope and prescription genuinely differ here.

### Jurisdiction variants

| Jurisdiction | Scope | Status / delta |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| US | SR 26-2/OCC Bulletin 2026-13; most relevant above USD 30bn, potentially below where model risk is significant. | Issued 17 April 2026; replaced 2011 guidance; risk-based, non-prescriptive. |
| UK | Specified PRA firms with internal-model approval; new permission gives 12 months to comply; principles extend beyond approved capital models. | Current SS1/23 effective 23 April 2026. |
| EU | ECB Guide covers approved CRR internal models. | Revised 28 July 2025; supervisory CRR interpretation, not enterprise MRM code. |
