### Core mechanism

| Dimension | Business lineage | Technical lineage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Starts from | Business concept or reporting item | Physical field, table, file, message or API |
| Shows | Source concept, key transformations, aggregation and use | System hops, joins, filters, code logic and target fields |
| Main users | Owners, consumers, risk, finance, audit, supervisors | Engineers, analysts, testers, control teams |
| Main question | "What feeds this number and why?" | "Exactly where and how was this value produced?" |

```text
Customer exposure concept
    ↓
Business rule: aggregate by connected counterparty
    ↓
Risk concentration report
```

```text
LOAN.CPTY_ID + LIMIT.OUTSTANDING
    ↓
ETL join and mapping
    ↓
RISK_EXPOSURE.CONSOLIDATED_AMOUNT
    ↓
Reporting extract field C0170
```

Good lineage links the two views. A business definition without technical traceability cannot prove implementation; technical pipes without business meaning cannot prove that the right concept was implemented.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Golden source · CDE · Business definition
This:       Business and technical lineage
Downstream: Metadata · Mapping · Impact analysis · Root-cause analysis · Testing
```

### Never confuse [Lineage] and [Source-to-target mapping]

**Lineage** - the end-to-end dependency chain across sources, transformations and uses, often spanning several systems and mappings.

---

**Source-to-target mapping** - the implementation specification for a defined source-to-target transformation. It can be an important component of lineage without being the whole lineage.

### Permanent rule

For material risk data, a consumer should be able to move from reported value to business meaning and from that meaning down to the physical source and transformation evidence.

### Permanent trap

Calling a system list "lineage". "Core banking -&gt; warehouse -&gt; report" identifies locations but not fields, transformations, dependencies or governed business meaning.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Lineage mechanics are not jurisdiction-specific; supervisory depth of scrutiny can vary.

<Recall
  q="Why are both business and technical lineage necessary?"
  a="Business lineage proves semantic dependency and intended use; technical lineage proves physical implementation and enables reproduction, troubleshooting and impact analysis."
/>

<Recall
  q="What does technical lineage add to a mapping document?"
  a="It connects mappings across the full chain, including intermediate systems and transformations, so a final reported value can be traced beyond one interface."
