### Core mechanism

| Topic | UK — as at August 2026 | EU — as at August 2026 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main status | Final rules; not yet commenced | CRR3 in application |
| Main date | 1 Jan 2027 | 1 Jan 2025 |
| Output floor in 2026 | Not yet operative | 55% |
| Future floor | 60% in 2027; 65% in 2028; 70% in 2029; 72.5% from 2030 | 60% in 2027; 65% in 2028; 70% in 2029; 72.5% from 2030 |
| Market-risk timing | FRTB internal-model approach from 1 Jan 2028 | 1 Jan 2027 treatment remains tied to the live delegated-act process |
| Smaller-firm proportionality | SDDT simplified regime from 1 Jan 2027 | Separate CRR/CRD proportionality framework |

The UK delay means its output-floor transition starts at the 60% 2027 step rather than replaying the EU's 2025-26 stages. For EU market risk, the Commission adopted a third FRTB Delegated Act on 4 June 2026; **as at August 2026** it remained under European Parliament and Council scrutiny, so the 1 January 2027 treatment must be read with that status attached.

### Permanent rule

Same Basel standard does not mean same current rule: commencement, transition, national choices and proportionality can differ.

### Never confuse [Final rule with future commencement] and [Rule already in force]

**Final rule with future commencement** — final policy whose operative date has not arrived.

---

**Rule already in force** — requirement currently applicable to the institution.

### Permanent trap

Using the EU's 55% 2026 floor for a UK bank, or treating UK Basel 3.1 as already in force in August 2026, mixes implementation calendars.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### UK
**As at August 2026:** PS1/26 is final; main Basel 3.1 commencement is 1 January 2027; FRTB internal-model approach begins 1 January 2028. PS4/26 makes the SDDT capital regime effective 1 January 2027.

#### EU
**As at August 2026:** CRR3 is in application; output floor is 55% in 2026 with future annual steps to 72.5% from 1 January 2030. The third FRTB Delegated Act adopted 4 June 2026 remained under scrutiny; EBA material dated 3 August 2026 describes its application conditionally on entry into force.
