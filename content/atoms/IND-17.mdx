### Core mechanism

| Component | Role in lending |
| :--- | :--- |
| GSTN and other source systems | Originate verified business or public-sector data |
| AA ecosystem | Carries consented financial information |
| ULI | Provides a standardised interface for lenders to access permitted data sources |
| OCEN | Open protocol approach for connecting borrower-facing platforms and lenders |
| Bank/NBFC | Performs underwriting, sanctions credit and owns the loan |

```text
Permitted source data
    ↓
Consent or authorised access
    ↓
Standardised data rail
    ↓
Lender underwriting
    ↓
Credit decision by regulated lender
```

ULI and OCEN do not remove the need for a regulated lender, KFS, underwriting governance or customer consent. They reduce friction in data collection and offer distribution.

The precise 2026 production status and instrument reference for every ULI/OCEN component is not established in this pack as at 31 August 2026. No unverified rollout statistic or mandatory-adoption claim is stated.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   AA consent · Public data
This:       Lending data rails
Downstream: Underwriting · Digital origination · Credit decision
```

### Permanent rule

A data rail can accelerate lending, but it does not itself sanction credit or absorb borrower default risk.

### Never confuse [ULI] and [Lender]

**ULI** — infrastructure for standardised access to permitted lending data.

---

**Lender** — the regulated entity that decides, contracts and owns the credit exposure.

### Jurisdiction variants

No within-India delta in the infrastructure concept; actual participation depends on each entity's technical and regulatory eligibility.
