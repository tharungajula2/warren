An internal loss data collection process ensures the bank tracks each actual loss event.  Banks maintain a database of all operational losses by category (see OPR-02) – including fraud losses, fines, system repair costs, etc.  Typically there is a **loss event threshold**: events below this amount aren’t individually logged to avoid noise.  For example, Basel’s SMA requires data on losses above €20,000. In practice many banks set lower internal thresholds (e.g. $1k) so they also capture frequency patterns.  

The collected data feed multiple uses: management reporting, trend analysis, scenario calibration, and capital. Under SMA, the “Loss Component” is defined as 15× the 10-year average annual losses recorded (gross, before recoveries). Losses must be **gross of insurance** – a common error is logging net values.  Good practice also separates recoveries to analyze control effectiveness.  Note: if an operational failure caused a loan loss, classification can be tricky – banks usually classify by legal cause (so a system error that led to a trading loss still counts as operational loss).

High-quality data is crucial: even one missing large loss can distort capital greatly. Losss should be captured promptly and reconciled (e.g. accounting vs op-risk logs). The internal loss database is typically owned by the 2nd line (Operational Risk) but populated by 1st line or an incident intake system.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Record every material operational loss (gross amount) in the internal loss database. Use the predefined threshold and update it with regulator guidance (e.g. SMA €20k floor).
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Do not undercount losses by using net-of-insurance amounts. The regulatory capital formula (and risk view) assumes gross losses.  Also avoid high thresholds that miss important events – recall a loss just below threshold may lead to serious underestimation of tail risk.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Gross loss" b="Net loss">
**Gross loss** — the total loss amount before any recoveries.  
---  
**Net loss** — the loss after deducting insurance or recoveries. (Banks must record *gross* loss for risk capital.)
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** All Basel-aligned capital frameworks use gross operational losses.  Basel’s SMA specifies €20k min threshold (banks can report more granular).  For reference, UK regulators also expect banks’ internal loss databases to follow Basel rules for materiality.
</Rule>
