### Core mechanism

```text
Business events and positions
    ↓
Source data and reference data
    ↓
Governed definitions, ownership and controls
    ↓
Risk data aggregation
    ↓
Risk reports and regulatory returns
    ↓
Decisions, challenge and supervisory action
```

A risk-data framework therefore controls four linked questions:

| Question | Control objective |
| :--- | :--- |
| What does the datum mean? | Common definition, metadata and glossary |
| Where did it come from? | Authoritative source and lineage |
| Can it be relied on? | Data quality rules, reconciliation and issue management |
| Who is accountable? | Owner, steward, producer, consumer and challenge roles |

Risk measures themselves remain owned elsewhere: PD, LGD, EAD and RWA are credit and capital concepts; ECL staging is impairment; LCR and NSFR are liquidity. DAT owns the enterprise data chain that carries such measures into decisions and reporting.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Business events · Source systems · Reference data
This:       Enterprise risk data discipline
Downstream: Risk aggregation · Management reporting · Regulatory returns · Decisions
```

### Ownership

| Role | Core accountability |
| :--- | :--- |
| Producer | Creates or transforms data |
| Data owner | Accountable for governed meaning and fitness for use |
| Data steward | Operates standards, monitoring and remediation |
| Consumer | Uses data and identifies fitness-for-use failures |
| Challenge | Independently tests whether controls and evidence deserve reliance |

### Permanent rule

A report cannot be more trustworthy than the data, definitions, controls and accountability that produced it.

### Permanent trap

Treating risk data as an IT problem because systems store it. Technology operates the plumbing; business accountability determines meaning, materiality and acceptable quality.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The enterprise mechanics do not change by jurisdiction; local regulators differ mainly in reporting requirements and supervisory enforcement.
