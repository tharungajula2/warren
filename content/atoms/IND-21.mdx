### Core mechanism

For term loans, the corrected delinquency sequence is:

| Status | Days past due |
| :--- | :--- |
| SMA-0 | Up to 30 days |
| SMA-1 | More than 30 days and up to 60 days |
| SMA-2 | More than 60 days and up to 90 days |
| NPA | More than 90 days, subject to the applicable prudential rule |

The account's overdue status is determined from the contractual due date and day-end process under the applicable Directions. Other products, especially revolving facilities such as cash credit or overdraft, can use "out of order" or other product-specific tests rather than a simple instalment count.

```text
Due amount unpaid
    ↓
SMA-0
    ↓
SMA-1
    ↓
SMA-2
    ↓
NPA if the applicable NPA trigger is crossed
```

SMA is a stress-monitoring classification; it is not the same object as Stage 2 under Ind AS 109.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Contractual due date · Payment status
This:       SMA early-stress ladder
Downstream: NPA recognition · Resolution monitoring
```

### Never confuse [SMA-2] and [NPA]

**SMA-2** — the final pre-NPA delinquency bucket for the applicable exposure.

---

**NPA** — prudential non-performing status once the relevant RBI trigger is crossed.

### Permanent rule

SMA categories are not optional watchlist labels; where the framework applies, they follow the prescribed repayment-status logic.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Term loans
Days-past-due buckets drive SMA status.

#### Cash credit and overdraft facilities
Product-specific "out of order" logic applies alongside the broader prudential framework.
