## Overview of Time Value of Money

The core principle of financial mathematics is that a dollar received today is worth more than a dollar received in the future due to its potential earning capacity.

<Formula math="PV = \frac{CF_t}{(1 + r)^t}" />

Where $CF_t$ represents cash flow at time $t$, $r$ represents the discount rate, and $PV$ is the present value.

<Rule label="DISCOUNTING PRINCIPLE">
All future expected obligations must be discounted using a risk-free rate plus credit risk premium to determine current economic value.
</Rule>
