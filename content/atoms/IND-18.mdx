### Core mechanism

```text
Lender reports account and repayment data
    ↓
CIC consolidates borrower credit history
    ↓
Credit Information Report is generated
    ↓
CIC may derive a proprietary score
    ↓
Lender uses report and score as underwriting inputs
```

| Object | What it contains or represents |
| :--- | :--- |
| Credit Information Report (CIR) | Detailed reported credit accounts, balances, enquiries and repayment/default history |
| Bureau score | Model-generated summary signal based on bureau data |
| Lender credit decision | Broader assessment using bureau data plus income, cash flow, collateral, policy and other evidence |

RBI regulates the reporting relationship between credit institutions and CICs, including data quality, update and customer correction expectations. A high score is not a guarantee of repayment; a low or missing score is not by itself a regulatory prohibition on lending.

One-line reminder: model validation belongs to MRM. This atom owns what the bureau object is, not how its proprietary model is validated.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Lender-reported credit data
This:       CIC/CIR/score
Downstream: Underwriting · Pricing · Credit monitoring
```

### Permanent rule

Credit information is an input to a lender's decision, not a substitute for the lender's own credit assessment.

### Never confuse [Credit Information Report] and [Credit score]

**Credit Information Report** — the detailed borrower-level reported credit history.

---

**Credit score** — a model-generated summary indicator derived from bureau information.

### Jurisdiction variants

No material within-India delta in the concept; different CICs may use different proprietary score methodologies.

<Recall
  q="Why can two CICs give different scores for the same borrower?"
  a="They can use different data coverage, timing and proprietary models even when both operate under the same credit-information regulatory framework."
