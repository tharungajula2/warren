## Overview of Loss Given Default

Loss Given Default (LGD) reflects the economic loss severity following a default event.

<Formula math="LGD = 1 - \frac{\text{Recoveries} - \text{Collection Costs}}{\text{EAD}}" />

<Rule label="DOWNTURN LGD">
Regulatory capital requires downturn LGD estimates reflecting severe macroeconomic downturn periods rather than average economic conditions.
</Rule>
