### Core mechanism

Acceptance combines evidence with authority.

| Acceptance dimension | Required question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Authority | Who is authorised to accept the changed business capability? |
| Baseline | Which requirement and scope version is being accepted? |
| Evidence | Which test, reconciliation, readiness and control evidence supports the decision? |
| Exceptions | Which requirements are not fully evidenced or met? |
| Limitations | Which workarounds, defects or temporary controls remain? |
| Conditions | What must happen before or after release? |
| Residual risk | Who owns each known exposure? |

DAT owns UAT coverage and business evidence as a data control. BA does not restate test-case mechanics; BA ensures the acceptance authority knows what the evidence proves, what it does not prove, and which open items are being consciously accepted.

A single business sign-off may be insufficient where different owners hold different mandates. The reporting owner may accept business output while operations accepts process readiness and a risk owner accepts a temporary control. Combine those decisions only if the governance model gives one role authority over all of them.

Conditional acceptance must be actionable: condition, owner, due date and consequence if unmet.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Approved requirements · UAT evidence · Defect decisions · Readiness evidence
This:       Business acceptance · Conditions · Residual limitations
Downstream: Go/no-go · Cutover · BAU ownership
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA assembles the acceptance decision view and links evidence to obligations |
| Approves | Named business acceptance authority accepts within mandate |
| Consumes | Release governance, operations and production owners rely on the acceptance |
| Independently challenges | Risk, compliance or assurance challenge acceptance gaps within their mandates |

### Permanent rule

Acceptance is a decision by an authorised owner, not a test status. Evidence supports acceptance; it does not replace accountability.

### Never confuse [Sign-off] and [Acceptance]

**Sign-off** - approves an earlier baseline, requirement or delivery artefact within a defined mandate.

---

**Acceptance** - authorises use of the delivered change against current evidence and known limitations.

### Permanent trap

Asking business users to "sign UAT" without telling them whether they are confirming test execution, requirement coverage, operational readiness or production acceptance. Different commitments need different authority.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Where an authority requires specific implementation evidence, that evidence becomes an input to acceptance but does not change the ownership discipline.
