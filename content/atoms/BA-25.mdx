### Core mechanism

Cutover governance must distinguish the decision to go, the trigger to stop and the conditions for fallback.

```text
Entry -&gt; Go -&gt; Execute -&gt; Checkpoint -&gt; Continue, stop or fallback -&gt; Stabilisation
```

| State | Condition | Authority or action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Go | Acceptance, readiness, access, data and support criteria met or waived | Go/no-go authority starts transition |
| Continue | Business checkpoint passes | Cutover lead proceeds |
| Stop | Defined trigger breaches tolerance or creates unsafe uncertainty | Stop authority halts sequence |
| Fallback | New state is unsafe and fallback remains viable | Restore coherent business state |
| Complete | Final checkpoints pass and ownership is clear | Declare cutover complete; hand to stabilisation |
| Communicate | State, delay or fallback affects others | Inform users, executives, dependencies and support |

Technology and data owners own detailed migration steps; BA owns the business checkpoints and dependencies they must protect. Fallback covers transactions, reconciliation, procedures, controls and reversals, not merely infrastructure. Agree criteria, authorities and permitted waivers before the go/no-go meeting.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Business acceptance · Parallel-run exit · Operational readiness
This:       Go/no-go · Cutover checkpoints · Stop and fallback authority
Downstream: Production state · Stabilisation · BAU handover
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Technology/data teams produce execution plan; BA integrates business checkpoints and decision conditions |
| Approves | Named go/no-go authority authorises production transition |
| Consumes | Cutover command, operations, support and business owners execute decisions |
| Independently challenges | Risk or control functions challenge unresolved material readiness or fallback gaps |

### Permanent rule

Go-live authority must be named before cutover begins, including who can stop the transition. Ambiguous authority becomes most dangerous when time pressure is highest.

### Never confuse [Go-live] and [Stabilisation]

**Go-live** - the transition point at which the new state becomes production-active.

---

**Stabilisation** - the controlled period after go-live in which production behaviour, incidents and temporary controls are monitored before normal support is declared.

### Permanent trap

Treating fallback as a technical-only plan. If business state, transactions, controls and user procedures cannot be restored coherently, the fallback is not operationally viable.
