### Core mechanism

Simplified asset economics:

Customer yield − FTP charge = business spread before other costs

Simplified deposit economics:

FTP credit − customer deposit rate = business funding spread before other costs

| Bad FTP design | Business sees | Behaviour it encourages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| One flat rate for all asset tenors | long loans appear cheap to fund | excess maturity transformation |
| Same credit for all deposits | unstable deposits look as valuable as sticky funding | volume over quality |
| No contingent charge | undrawn lines appear nearly free | excess commitments |
| Stale curve | yesterday’s funding cost | mispriced new business |
| Inconsistent behavioural assumptions | artificial transfer profit | model arbitrage between treasury and business |

FTP centralises risk **execution**, not risk **economics**. Treasury can recognise natural offsets across businesses while the originating desk still sees the correct incremental cost or benefit in product pricing and performance.

### Flow

```text
Business product decision
    ↓
Funding / contingent liquidity consequence
    ↓
FTP charge or credit
    ↓
Risk-adjusted product economics
    ↓
Origination / deposit / commitment behaviour
    ↓
Future balance-sheet composition
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Funding strategy · behavioural balance sheet
This:       Internal transfer of funding/liquidity economics and incentives
Downstream: Matched-maturity curve · behavioural FTP · ALCO
```

### Permanent rule

The best test of FTP is not whether internal accounts reconcile; it is whether the price makes risk-taking incentives consistent with the bank’s funding and liquidity appetite.

### Never confuse [Risk-originating business] and [Risk-execution owner]

**Risk-originating business** — creates the product decision that consumes or supplies funding/liquidity.

---

**Risk-execution owner** — treasury centrally funds, hedges or offsets the aggregate position.

### Permanent trap

Treating FTP as an after-the-fact profitability allocation. If the transfer price does not influence new-business pricing and volume, it is not performing its control function.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in economic mechanism. Some supervisors impose more explicit expectations on FTP governance for larger firms.
