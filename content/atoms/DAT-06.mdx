### Core mechanism

| Principle | Supervisory function |
| :--- | :--- |
| 12 Review | Supervisors periodically assess compliance with the principles |
| 13 Remedial actions and supervisory measures | Deficiencies require timely remediation; supervisors can use available tools where progress is inadequate |
| 14 Home-host cooperation | Supervisors cooperate across jurisdictions for cross-border banking groups |

```text
Supervisory assessment
    ↓
Deficiency or gap identified
    ↓
Management action plan and accountable remediation
    ↓
Evidence of implementation and sustainability
    ↓
Supervisory follow-up and escalation where needed
```

The 2026 Basel newsletter identifies recurring themes including governance, lineage, cross-border implementation, changing operating environments, emerging technology and compensating controls. It describes implementation as continuous because mergers, new products, technology and risk landscapes keep changing the aggregation problem.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   BCBS 239 governance · Aggregation · Reporting
This:       Supervisory review and implementation record
Downstream: Remediation plans · Escalation · Sustainable control improvement
```

### Permanent rule

BCBS 239 compliance is a sustained capability, not a one-time programme closure date.

### Permanent trap

Treating a compensating control as evidence that the underlying defect is solved. A manual or detective control can contain risk while structural remediation remains open.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### EU (ECB Banking Supervision)
The ECB's May 2024 RDARR Guide clarifies supervisory expectations for significant institutions and is used in targeted reviews and remediation monitoring; it does not replace BCBS 239.

#### UK (PRA)
The PRA applies its supervisory toolkit through the UK prudential framework; this atom does not invent a separate UK version of the fourteen principles.

#### US
US agencies use their own supervisory processes and reporting expectations; the Basel principles remain a benchmark rather than a US form specification.

#### IN (RBI)
RBI's 31 July 2026 Commercial Banks - Supervisory Returns Directions contain explicit governance, architecture, reconciliation and automation requirements for commercial-bank supervisory reporting.
