### Core mechanism

| Principle | Reporting test |
| :--- | :--- |
| 7 Accuracy | Report outputs faithfully represent aggregated risk data and are reconciled or otherwise controlled |
| 8 Comprehensiveness | Material risks, concentrations and relevant forward-looking information are covered at suitable depth |
| 9 Clarity and usefulness | Content is concise enough to support action, while definitions, limits, trends and exceptions are intelligible |
| 10 Frequency | Production frequency matches the volatility of risk and recipient decision needs, including stress |
| 11 Distribution | Reports reach authorised recipients through controlled channels with confidentiality preserved |

```text
Aggregated risk data
    ↓
Select material measures and dimensions
    ↓
Add limits, trends, exceptions and context
    ↓
Review and control report output
    ↓
Distribute at decision-useful frequency
    ↓
Management action and challenge
```

Clarity is not cosmetic simplification. A board report may aggregate heavily, but it must preserve the information needed to identify concentrations, limit breaches and emerging risk. A desk-level report may be more granular because the consumer needs operational detail.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Governed aggregation · Risk appetite and limits · Reporting definitions
This:       Risk reporting practices
Downstream: Board and committee decisions · Escalation · Supervisory evidence
```

### Never confuse [Data timeliness] and [Report frequency]

**Data timeliness** - how quickly current data can be aggregated for the decision need.

---

**Report frequency** - how often a defined report is produced and distributed. A monthly report can depend on data that must still be available rapidly for ad-hoc stress analysis.

### Permanent trap

Equating more pages with comprehensiveness. A report can be large yet fail to expose the risk, exception or concentration that the recipient needs to act on.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The reporting-practice principles are universal; local return formats are covered later.
