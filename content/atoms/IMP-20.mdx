The first boundary is non-negotiable: **Indian scheduled commercial banks are not currently applying Ind AS 109**. RBI’s March 22, 2019 circular deferred implementation of Ind AS for scheduled commercial banks “till further notice”. As of August 28, 2026, commercial banks covered by RBI’s 2025 Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning Directions remain on prudential IRACP rules. Those rules classify problem assets through NPA, substandard, doubtful and loss categories and prescribe provisioning rather than operating an IFRS 9 accounting-stage model.

The second track is **Ind AS-implementing NBFCs and ARCs**. RBI’s March 13, 2020 guidance states that NBFCs covered by Rule 4 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules must prepare financial statements under Ind AS. RBI overlays prudential safeguards: such entities maintain Ind AS impairment allowances while also computing IRACP provisions, with any shortfall addressed through the prescribed impairment reserve mechanism. RBI also reinforces the 30-DPD SICR presumption and governance of ECL models and overlays.

The third track is the **commercial-bank transition to RBI ECL**. RBI’s January 16, 2023 Discussion Paper proposed a three-stage IFRS-9-influenced ECL approach for scheduled commercial banks. That document was a consultation proposal, not operative provisioning law.

RBI subsequently issued final *Commercial Banks – Asset Classification, Provisioning and Income Recognition Directions, 2026* on April 27, 2026. They take effect **April 1, 2027**; until then, the 2025 IRACP Directions continue to govern. The final framework uses Stage 1 12-month ECL, Stage 2 lifetime ECL after SICR and Stage 3 lifetime ECL for credit-impaired assets, while adding RBI-specific prudential floors, transition rules, model-risk requirements and income-recognition provisions. This is an RBI prudential ECL framework—not adoption of Ind AS 109 for banks.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
An accounting standard, a prudential provisioning regime and a proposed regulatory framework are different legal and conceptual objects even when they use similar ECL terminology.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Writing “India uses Ind AS 109 for banks” collapses three different regimes and is factually wrong: scheduled commercial-bank Ind AS implementation was deferred, current bank provisioning remains IRACP through March 31, 2027, and RBI’s April 2026 ECL Directions are a separate prudential framework effective April 1, 2027.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Ind AS 109 for relevant NBFCs" b="RBI bank ECL framework">
**Ind AS 109 for relevant NBFCs** — Indian accounting-standard ECL applied by entities within the applicable Ind AS implementation scope, subject to RBI prudential guidance.

---
**RBI bank ECL framework** — RBI’s separately issued commercial-bank prudential provisioning regime, finalised in April 2026 and effective April 1, 2027; it is not bank adoption of Ind AS 109.
</NeverConfuse>

<Delta j="EU">

[Regulation (EU) 2023/1803; EBA/GL/2017/06]

<Delta j="US">

</Delta>
