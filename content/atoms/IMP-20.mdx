### Core mechanism

| Population / period | Regime | Recognition | Boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Banks through 31 Mar 2027 | RBI IRACP 2025 | Classification + prescribed provisions | Not Ind AS 109 |
| Applicable NBFCs / ARCs | Ind AS + RBI guidance | Ind AS 109 ECL + prudential comparison / impairment reserve | Accounting + prudential measures |
| Banks from 1 Apr 2027 | RBI ECL Directions 2026 | Stage 1 12-month; Stage 2/3 lifetime ECL + floors | RBI, not Ind AS |
| Same banks | Retained NPA rules | NPA classification beside staging | Stage 3 ≠ NPA |

### Flow

```text
31 Mar 2027 IRACP provision
    ↓ compare with
1 Apr 2027 ECL
    ↓
Positive excess → opening earnings adjustment
    ↓
Optional CET1 transition
```

### Formula

```text
Transitional adjustment = max(0, ECL at 1 Apr 2027 - IRACP provisions at 31 Mar 2027)

Maximum CET1 add-back as fraction of transitional adjustment, net of applicable taxes:

2027-28 = 4/5

2028-29 = 3/5

2029-30 = 2/5

2030-31 = 1/5

After 31 Mar 2031 = 0
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   RBI IRACP · Ind AS scope · Closing provisions
This:       Parallel regimes · RBI ECL transition
Downstream: ECL · Prudential floors · Opening earnings · CET1
```

### Permanent rule

RBI bank ECL is not bank adoption of Ind AS 109.

### Never confuse [Loss allowance] and [Capital]

**Loss allowance** — measures expected credit loss and reduces carrying value or creates a provision.

---

**Capital** — loss-absorbing resource.

The CET1 add-back phases capital recognition of the transition; it does not turn ECL into capital.

### Never confuse [Stage 3] and [NPA]

**Stage 3** — credit-impaired ECL state.

---

**NPA** — classification under retained RBI rules.

An exposure may satisfy both; they remain distinct.

### Permanent trap

“Banks adopt Ind AS 109 on 1 April 2027” is wrong. Ind AS remains deferred; RBI’s 2026 Directions create a separate staged-ECL regime with floors and NPA classification.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
IRACP, Ind AS for applicable NBFCs/ARCs, and RBI bank ECL differ by entity and date.

#### UK (UKEB)
UK-adopted IFRS 9 has no equivalent transition.

#### EU (European Commission)
EU-adopted IFRS 9 does not use India’s entity split.

#### US (FASB)
US GAAP uses CECL, not RBI staged ECL.
