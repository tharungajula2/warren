A Credit Support Annex, or CSA, sits alongside an ISDA Master Agreement and defines how collateral supports covered derivatives exposure. The exact legal form differs by governing law and document type, but the economic objective is to reduce unsecured counterparty exposure as market values change.

Variation margin responds to current exposure. If a portfolio moves in one party’s favour, collateral may be transferred by the out-of-the-money party subject to thresholds, minimum transfer amounts, rounding and timing rules. Initial margin, where applicable, is designed to cover potential future exposure during a close-out period and is often subject to segregation requirements under regulatory frameworks.

Eligible collateral can include cash and securities. Non-cash collateral may receive a haircut so that only a discounted value counts. Currency mismatches, concentration limits and wrong-way risk can also affect eligibility or value. Interest or price alignment amounts may be paid on cash collateral according to the agreement.

Collateral mechanics have direct valuation consequences. The rate paid on cash collateral influences the economically appropriate discounting framework for collateralised derivatives. A change in CSA currency or remuneration can therefore change fair value even if the trade’s headline fixed rate and notional do not change.

A margin call is not the same as settlement of the derivative. It is a collateral lifecycle event. Disputes may arise because counterparties value trades differently, apply different market data or disagree on eligible collateral. Operations must track call amount, agreed amount, disputed amount, collateral delivered, value date and substitutions.

Collateral reduces loss given counterparty default but creates liquidity demands. During volatility, a firm can be economically solvent yet face large same-day cash calls. Collateral risk management is therefore both a credit-risk and treasury problem.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
A CSA changes collateralisation of counterparty exposure; it does not rewrite the derivative’s underlying payoff.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Treating posted collateral as unavailable to liquidity risk merely because it reduces credit exposure.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Variation margin" b="Initial margin">
**Variation margin** — collateral responding primarily to current mark-to-market exposure.

---

**Initial margin** — collateral intended primarily to cover potential future exposure during a close-out period.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.**
</Rule>
