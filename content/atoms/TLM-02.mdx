### Core mechanism

For each asset, liability and off-balance-sheet position, ask five different questions:

| Dimension | Question | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Contractual cash flow | When is cash legally due? | starting maturity schedule |
| Behavioural cash flow | When is cash realistically expected? | deposits, prepayment, drawdowns |
| Repricing | When and to what benchmark can price reset? | NII and IRRBB |
| Currency | In what currency must cash be delivered? | FX and currency liquidity |
| Optionality | Who can change amount or timing? | scenario-dependent cash flows |

A five-year fixed-rate loan funded by an overnight deposit creates no accounting imbalance. It does create a funding horizon mismatch and an interest-rate mismatch. If the loan is in USD and the deposit is in INR, it also creates a currency problem even if a derivative temporarily offsets the spot FX position.

### Flow

```text
Position record
    ↓
Contractual cash flows
    ↓
Behavioural adjustments
    ↓
Repricing + currency + optionality map
    ↓
IRRBB · liquidity · funding · FX · FTP measures
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Treasury mandate · accounting balance sheet
This:       Economic decomposition of positions
Downstream: Behavioural assumptions · IRRBB · FX · liquidity ladders · FTP
```

### Permanent rule

Accounting balance does not imply economic matching.

### Never confuse [Maturity mismatch] and [Repricing mismatch]

**Maturity mismatch** — principal or cash returns on different effective horizons.

---

**Repricing mismatch** — interest rates reset at different times or on different bases, even if principal maturities match.

### Never confuse [Repricing event] and [Cash flow]

**Repricing event** — the rate changes while principal may stay outstanding.

---

**Cash flow** — cash actually enters or leaves the bank.

### Permanent trap

Using one “maturity” field for every ALM purpose. Contractual maturity, behavioural life, next repricing date and stressed liquidity horizon answer different questions.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Supervisors prescribe different templates and assumptions, but the economic decomposition is common.

<Recall
  q="Can an accounting-balanced bank have large ALM risk?"
  a="Yes — equality of assets and liabilities says nothing about timing, repricing, currency or optionality."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can one deposit appear in several different time buckets?"
  a="Its legal withdrawal right, behavioural life, rate reset and stressed runoff can all occur on different horizons."
