### Core mechanism

```text
Customer creates consent artefact
    ↓
Financial Information User requests specified data
    ↓
Account Aggregator routes consent request
    ↓
Financial Information Provider releases permitted information
    ↓
AA transmits encrypted information to the FIU
    ↓
Lender uses the information for the consented purpose
```

| Role | Lending relevance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Customer | Controls consent |
| Financial Information Provider (FIP) | Holds source financial information |
| Account Aggregator | Consent and transmission intermediary |
| Financial Information User (FIU) | Receives information for a permitted purpose, such as underwriting |

Consent defines scope, purpose, data type and duration under the applicable framework. The AA is designed to avoid uncontrolled credential sharing and manual statement exchange.

One-line reminder: BCBS 239 and enterprise data governance belong to DAT; this atom owns the Indian consent rail.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Customer financial data · Consent
This:       AA data-sharing rail
Downstream: Underwriting · ULI · Credit decision
```

### Permanent rule

The AA cannot convert customer consent for one defined purpose into unrestricted future access to the customer's financial information.

### Never confuse [Account Aggregator] and [Credit Bureau]

**Account Aggregator** — transmits customer-permissioned financial information for a defined consented purpose.

---

**Credit Bureau** — stores and reports credit-information history under the Credit Information Companies framework.

### Permanent trap

Treating AA consent as a lender's right to scrape any available financial data. The consent artefact is scoped and purpose-bound.

### Jurisdiction variants

No within-India delta in the core AA role; participation obligations differ by whether an entity is acting as FIP, FIU or AA.
