### Core mechanism

```text
Evidence
    ↓
Finding
    ↓
Severity and model-risk impact
    ↓
Remediation / compensating control
    ↓
Owner + due date
    ↓
Validation opinion
    ↓
Closure testing
```

| Finding field | What it must answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Observation | What is wrong or uncertain? |
| Consequence | Which output, use or decision can be affected? |
| Severity | How material is the resulting model risk? |
| Root cause | Data, method, implementation, use or governance? |
| Action | What must change? |
| Interim control | How is risk contained before permanent fix? |
| Owner / due date | Who is accountable and by when? |
| Closure evidence | What proves the issue is actually resolved? |

A practical opinion scale can distinguish:
- Fit for intended use.
- Fit subject to defined limitations, controls or remediation.
- Not fit for intended use.

The labels are institution-specific; the essential point is that the conclusion reflects residual risk, not merely the number of findings.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Conceptual review · Outcomes · Stability · Benchmarking
This:       Findings, severity and validation opinion
Downstream: Approval · Restrictions · Remediation · Issue closure
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Validator | Owns finding and independent closure assessment |
| Model owner | Owns remediation |
| Business user | Implements interim use restrictions |
| Governance body | Oversees overdue or material issues |

### Permanent rule

The model owner fixes the problem; the independent challenger decides whether evidence supports closing the finding.

### Never confuse [Validation opinion] and [Model approval]

**Validation opinion** - independent conclusion on reliability, limitations and conditions.

---

**Model approval** - governance decision to authorise use and accept residual risk after considering validation and business context.

### Permanent trap

Closing findings because the due date arrived or management accepted the risk. Risk acceptance can permit continued use; it does not prove the technical weakness disappeared.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Finding taxonomies differ across institutions, but translating validation weaknesses into accountable remediation and controlled closure is a universal governance mechanism.

<Recall
  q="What turns a technical observation into a useful validation finding?"
  a="It must connect the weakness to model-risk consequence, severity, corrective action, ownership, timing and evidence required for closure."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can a model be &quot;fit with limitations&quot; rather than simply pass or fail?"
  a="Some limitations are acceptable when bounded by use restrictions, monitoring or remediation; validation should expose residual risk rather than force false binary certainty."
/>
