### Core mechanism

Treat each constraint as an observable condition, not an adjective.

| Constraint | Weak wording | Decision-ready wording asks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Availability | "Highly available" | During which business windows must service be available, and what interruption is tolerable? |
| Performance | "Fast" | Which transaction or batch must finish by what operational cutoff? |
| Capacity | "Scalable" | What volume, peak pattern and growth assumption must be supported? |
| Access | "Secure" | Which roles may perform which actions, and who approves access? |
| Auditability | "Fully auditable" | Which actions and decisions must leave retrievable evidence? |
| Recoverability | "Resilient" | What business state must be restorable after failure and within what operating tolerance? |
| Support | "Supported" | Who owns incidents, data questions and business decisions after go-live? |
| Calendar | "Meets close" | Which day-end, month-end, quarter-end or filing cutoff must not be breached? |

Specialist teams may translate these obligations into technical measures. BA owns the business meaning and the owner who can judge whether the constraint is sufficient.

Do not manufacture numerical thresholds. If the business has not decided one, record an open decision rather than writing a convenient number into a requirement.

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA elicits and structures business-facing constraints |
| Approves | Service, process, control or business owner approves the required level |
| Consumes | Architecture, technology, operations and testing translate constraints into implementation and evidence |
| Independently challenges | Information security, operational risk, compliance or resilience functions challenge within mandate |

### Permanent rule

If a quality matters to business acceptance, it is a requirement even when it is "non-functional". Unstated operational expectations are still capable of breaking go-live.

### Never confuse [Functional behaviour] and [Operational constraint]

**Functional behaviour** - what the process or system must do.

---

**Operational constraint** - the conditions under which that behaviour must remain usable, controlled and supportable.

### Permanent trap

Assuming technology will "handle" capacity, logging, recovery or access later. Without business-defined needs, technical teams can implement a reasonable solution that is still wrong for close, control or support.
