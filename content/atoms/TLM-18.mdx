### Core mechanism

For each time bucket:

Liquidity gap_i = cash inflows_i − cash outflows_i

Cumulative liquidity gap_n = sum of Liquidity gap_i through bucket n

| Input | Contractual starting point | Behavioural/stress adjustment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Deposits | legal withdrawal/maturity | runoff profile |
| Loans | scheduled receipts | prepayment, delinquency where relevant |
| Wholesale funding | maturity | rollover assumption |
| Commitments | no funded cash today | drawdown assumption |
| Derivatives | contractual settlements | margin/collateral calls |
| Asset monetisation | not a contractual inflow | haircut + execution timing |

A cumulative deficit says the bank must bridge cash by that horizon. It does not say the bank will fail: available buffers and credible funding actions may cover it.

### Flow

```text
Contractual cash schedule
    ↓
Behavioural assumptions
    ↓
Cash inflow/outflow buckets
    ↓
Discrete gaps
    ↓
Cumulative gap
    ↓
Compare with counterbalancing capacity
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Behavioural model · liquidity inventory
This:       Time profile of cash mismatch
Downstream: LCR · NSFR contrast · survival horizon · CFP
```

### Permanent rule

Liquidity measurement must follow expected cash timing, not simply accounting maturity labels.

### Never confuse [Liquidity gap] and [Repricing gap]

**Liquidity gap** — cash entering minus cash leaving in a period.

---

**Repricing gap** — rate-sensitive assets minus liabilities resetting in a period.

### Permanent trap

Assuming every maturing wholesale liability rolls because it usually does. Rollover is a behavioural funding assumption and should deteriorate in relevant stresses.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI’s liquidity-risk framework requires the Statement of Structural Liquidity and permits behavioural placement of cash flows. It prescribes short-bucket cumulative mismatch tolerance architecture and separate foreign-currency/overseas reporting.

#### UK (PRA)
PRA liquidity supervision supplements LCR with cash-flow mismatch and firm-specific liquidity assessment, including Pillar 2 liquidity tools.

#### EU
No mechanism delta. CRR/EBA reporting and supervisory frameworks prescribe liquidity reporting beyond the simple LCR ratio.

#### US
Regulation YY requires covered bank holding companies to project short- and long-term cash flows, include behavioural assumptions and identify discrete and cumulative mismatches.

  q="What does a cumulative negative liquidity gap mean?"
  a="By that horizon, projected cash outflows exceed inflows and the deficit must be covered by usable liquidity or funding actions."
