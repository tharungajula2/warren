### Core mechanism

Closure tests the original mandate against production reality and prevents unfinished obligations from becoming ownerless.

| Review dimension | Closure test |
| :--- | :--- |
| Required outcome | Original mandate works in operation |
| Requirements | Accepted requirements function in production |
| Benefits | Mandated efficiency, quality, control, service or compliance outcome appears |
| Controls | Changed controls operate and are evidenced |
| Residual defects | Owner, date and escalation exist |
| Temporary controls | Reason, owner and exit plan remain valid |
| Accepted risks | Owners monitor what they accepted |
| Dependencies | Delayed or downstream impacts are addressed |
| Decisions | Material approval and change history are preserved |
| Ownership | No material obligation is orphaned |
| Minor open actions | Safe BAU transfer has owner, due date and escalation route |

```text
Mandate -&gt; Delivered change -&gt; Production outcome -&gt; Review -&gt; Residual actions owned -&gt; Programme closure
```

Benefits come from the mandate, not post-hoc success metrics: regulatory change may primarily deliver compliance; migration may reduce operational risk or remove unsupported infrastructure. Closure can precede minor action completion, but not transfer without durable accountability.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Stable production · Original mandate · Acceptance · Residual actions
This:       Outcome review · Benefits · Control effectiveness · Closure ownership
Downstream: BAU improvement · Future change · Audit and governance evidence
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA assembles trace from mandate to production outcome and residual actions |
| Approves | Sponsor or accountable business owner approves programme closure |
| Consumes | BAU owners, governance and assurance functions consume the final evidence |
| Independently challenges | Risk, compliance, internal audit or other assurance challenge closure where their mandate applies |

### Permanent rule

The programme can close with open work only when every material open obligation has transferred to an accountable owner who has accepted it.

### Never confuse [Project closure] and [Outcome realisation]

**Project closure** - ends the temporary delivery organisation and its governance.

---

**Outcome realisation** - confirms that the required business, control or strategic result exists and continues in production.

### Permanent trap

Archiving the project while temporary controls and deferred actions still depend on programme names or mailing lists. Once the programme disappears, those obligations become orphaned.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Where an external authority requires specific post-implementation evidence, that requirement becomes part of the closure evidence set.

<Recall
  q="What is the central closure test?"
  a="The required outcome operates in production and every material residual obligation has a durable BAU owner."
/>

<Recall
  q="Can closure occur with open actions?"
  a="Yes, when they are understood, authorised, transferred to named owners and governed by dates and escalation."
