### Core mechanism

```text
Facility / exposure
    ↓
Regulatory exposure class
    ↓
On-balance-sheet amount
or off-balance-sheet conversion
    ↓
Eligible CRM
    ↓
Prescribed risk driver / rating where permitted
    ↓
Risk weight
    ↓
Credit RWA
```

Standardised does not mean one weight for every exposure. Sovereigns, banks, corporates, retail, real estate, defaulted exposures and specialised lending can follow different rulebook treatments. External ratings are only one possible input and are used only where the applicable framework permits them.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Exposure objects · RWA arithmetic
This:       Standardised credit-risk engine
Downstream: CRM · IRB comparison · output-floor benchmark
```

### Formula

```text
Credit RWA = post-CRM regulatory exposure amount × prescribed risk weight

For off-balance-sheet items, the regulatory exposure amount can first require a conversion factor.
```

### Permanent rule

The regulator standardises the methodology; the resulting RWA can still be highly risk-sensitive across exposure classes.

### Never confuse [Standardised approach] and [External rating]

**Standardised approach** — supervisor-prescribed capital methodology.

---

**External rating** — one permitted input for specified exposures under specified conditions.

### Permanent trap

Assigning one default weight to every unrated corporate exposure ignores exposure-class rules, due diligence and jurisdictional implementation.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
**As at August 2026:** RBI's final revised Standardised Approach was issued 27 April 2026 and comes into force **1 April 2027**. It is a final rule with future commencement, not a proposal.

#### UK (PRA)
**As at August 2026:** final Basel 3.1 rules are issued and commence **1 January 2027**.

#### EU
**As at August 2026:** CRR3 standardised credit-risk reforms have applied since **1 January 2025**.

#### US
**As at August 2026:** revisions issued on 19 March 2026 are proposals, not implemented rules; the comment period closed 18 June 2026 and the proposals are not finalised.
