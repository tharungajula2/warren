### Core mechanism

RBI transition:

```text
31 Mar 2027 IRACP provisions
            ↓ compare
1 Apr 2027 ECL requirement
            ↓
Excess ECL over old provisions
            ↓
Opening retained earnings reduce
            ↓
Bank may elect regulatory CET1 add-back
            ↓
Add-back fraction declines each year
            ↓
Fully loaded by FY 2031-32
```

| Financial year | Maximum fraction of transitional adjustment added back to CET1 |
| :--- | ---: |
| 2027-28 | 4/5 |
| 2028-29 | 3/5 |
| 2029-30 | 2/5 |
| 2030-31 | 1/5 |
| 2031-32 onward | Nil |

The add-back is net of applicable taxes. RBI states that the recognised amount flows through, as appropriate, to Tier 1 and Total Capital and therefore derived measures, but it is not Tier 2, cannot reduce standardised-approach exposure amounts and cannot reduce the leverage exposure measure.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   IMP-20 India ECL transition · opening retained earnings
This:       Temporary prudential CET1 relief
Downstream: Reported versus fully-loaded capital · leverage ratio · transition disclosures
```

### Formula

```text
Transitional adjustment base = max(0, ECL at 1 Apr 2027 − IRACP provisions at 31 Mar 2027)

CET1 add-back = applicable fraction × transitional adjustment base, net of applicable taxes
```

### Permanent rule

Transition relief changes regulatory capital recognition; it does not reverse, defer or reduce the ECL allowance required by the provisioning framework.

### Never confuse [ECL transition adjustment] and [CET1 add-back]

**ECL transition adjustment** — the opening-equity effect arising from the change in provisioning basis.

---

**CET1 add-back** — temporary prudential recognition of a declining fraction of that effect.

### Permanent trap

Reporting only the transitional CET1 ratio hides the fully-loaded economics. RBI therefore requires disclosure of the capital and leverage impact relative to the no-transition position.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
**As at August 2026:** final rule issued 27 April 2026; effective 1 April 2027. Future phase-out steps run through 31 March 2031. This is a rule with a future commencement date, not a proposal.

<Recall
  q="Does the RBI CET1 add-back reduce the ECL allowance?"
  a="No; the allowance remains recognised, while the add-back temporarily changes prudential capital."
/>

<Recall
  q="What is the maximum add-back fraction in FY 2029-30?"
  a="Two-fifths of the transitional adjustment base, subject to the Directions."
/>
