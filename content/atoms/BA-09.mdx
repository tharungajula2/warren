### Core mechanism

Four categories prevent most scope disputes.

| Category | Meaning | Required discipline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| In scope | Must be delivered in this change | Trace to requirements and acceptance |
| Out of scope | Explicitly excluded | State why and confirm no mandatory outcome depends on it |
| Assumed | Treated as true for current planning | Name owner and resolution date |
| Deferred | Valid need intentionally moved to later delivery | Record authority, consequence and target owner |

Scope should be bounded by business outcomes and affected capabilities, not by documents. "All fields in report X" is not sufficient if the change also alters upstream approval, reconciliation or production support.

Use interfaces as scope tests: what enters the changed process, what leaves it, and which team owns each side? A system can be technically out of scope but operationally dependent on the change. That dependency belongs in impact analysis even if no build is planned there.

```text
Mandate -&gt; Scope boundary -&gt; Requirements baseline
                     |
                     -&gt; Exclusions · Assumptions · Deferrals
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Mandate · Problem framing · Stakeholder decisions
This:       Scope boundary · Exclusions · Assumptions · Deferrals
Downstream: Requirements · Impact analysis · Change control
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA structures scope and records boundary decisions |
| Approves | Sponsor or accountable business owner approves scope baseline |
| Consumes | Delivery, finance, governance, testing and operations use it to control commitments |
| Independently challenges | Risk or compliance challenge exclusions that may undermine mandatory obligations |

### Permanent rule

An exclusion is safe only if its effect on the required outcome is understood. "Out of scope" cannot be used to make a dependency disappear.

### Never confuse [Out of scope] and [Deferred]

**Out of scope** - not part of the committed change.

---

**Deferred** - a recognised need intentionally postponed, with an owner and consequence.

### Permanent trap

Using assumptions as permanent scope shortcuts. An unresolved assumption can invalidate estimates, design and testing; if it matters, it needs an owner and resolution date.
