### Core mechanism

| Instrument | Core question | Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PSI | Has a score or variable distribution shifted between reference and current populations? | Population drift |
| CSI | Has the distribution of a characteristic shifted? | Feature-level drift |
| Benchmark | Does an independent method, external reference or simpler estimate produce materially different results? | Possible bias, misspecification or hidden assumption |
| Challenger | Can an alternative model explain or predict the target materially better or more robustly? | Incumbent may be stale or unnecessarily complex |

Reference population → current population → stability measure → investigate shift

```text
Incumbent model
    ↓
Independent benchmark / challenger
    ↓
Difference
    ↓
Explainable? material? persistent?
    ↓
Retain · Recalibrate · Restrict · Redevelop
```

PSI and CSI are signals, not verdicts. A large distribution shift can arise from genuine business mix changes, economic movement or data defects. Conversely, low PSI does not prove correctness: the population can remain stable while the model is structurally biased or poorly calibrated.

A challenger is evidence, not a replacement. Replacement requires its own development, validation, approval and implementation controls.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Validation work programme · Current population · Incumbent model
This:       Stability and independent comparison
Downstream: Findings · Monitoring · Change / redevelopment decision
```

### Permanent rule

Stability asks "has it moved?", not "is it right?". Benchmarking asks "what does another credible lens say?", not "which model wins one metric?".

### Never confuse [Benchmark] and [Challenger]

**Benchmark** - independent reference used to test reasonableness, which may be simpler, external or non-production.

---

**Challenger** - alternative model deliberately developed to compete with or test the incumbent on relevant performance and robustness dimensions.

### Permanent trap

Using the common PSI rules of thumb as automatic regulatory limits. Bin design, sample size, business context and model purpose affect interpretation; a breach should trigger investigation, not mechanical model failure.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. Stability statistics and independent comparators are validation instruments; there is no jurisdiction-specific mathematics and no universal supervisory PSI threshold to manufacture.

<Recall
  q="Why can a model with very low PSI still be wrong?"
  a="PSI says the population distribution has not moved much relative to the reference. It says nothing about whether the model's original relationships, calibration or assumptions were correct."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why does a better challenger not automatically replace the incumbent?"
  a="The challenger is evidence that merits investigation. Production replacement requires full development evidence, independent validation, approval, implementation controls and transition planning."
/>
