**Pillar 1** contains the prescribed methods for calculating minimum regulatory capital against defined risks. It creates comparability and a common quantitative baseline through rules governing eligible capital, RWA and minimum ratios.

**Pillar 2** exists because no universal formula captures every material risk at every institution. The Basel supervisory review process requires banks to assess capital adequacy relative to their risk profile and strategy and requires supervisors to review that assessment and intervene where necessary. Pillar 2 is therefore not simply “extra Pillar 1”. It addresses risk coverage, governance, concentration, interest-rate risk and other vulnerabilities that may not be fully captured by the minimum formula, subject to the applicable local framework.

**Pillar 3** promotes market discipline through regulatory disclosures. Basel describes it as complementing Pillars 1 and 2 by requiring information that allows market participants to assess regulatory capital, risk exposures and related prudential measures.

The three pillars work together. A technically correct Pillar 1 ratio is not proof that the institution has adequate capital in every economic circumstance. Pillar 2 can identify additional needs. Pillar 3 makes material aspects of that risk and capital position observable to external stakeholders.

The framework is thus formula, judgement and transparency rather than formula alone.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Pillar 1 is deliberately incomplete; Pillar 2 exists because material risk cannot be reduced to one universal minimum-capital formula.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Calling every supervisory capital add-on “Pillar 1” loses the distinction between formulaic minimum requirements and institution-specific supervisory assessment.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Pillar 2" b="Pillar 3">
**Pillar 2** — internal capital assessment plus supervisory review of risks and capital adequacy.

---
**Pillar 3** — disclosure and market discipline; it does not itself mean an additional capital charge.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Terminology and legal implementation vary, but the Basel three-pillar architecture is the common conceptual structure.
</Rule>
