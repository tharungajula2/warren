### Core mechanism

The scarce resource is not meeting time; it is expert attention.

| Before | During | After |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Read policies, existing requirements, reports and process evidence | State the decision or knowledge needed | Send a concise confirmation of conclusions |
| Mark what is already known | Ask highest-risk questions first | Separate confirmed facts from assumptions |
| Prepare scenarios and contradictions | Use concrete examples and exceptions | Assign unanswered questions to named owners |
| Send a short pre-read | Reflect back the answer in business language | Record decisions in the durable log |
| Know what can be answered without the SME | Stop when the required knowledge is obtained | Re-book only for genuinely new expert questions |

A strong question is bounded: "When an account cures after the reporting cutoff, which status should the report use and who approves an override?" A weak question is "Can you explain the process?"

Use scenario walkthroughs when rules are tacit. Ask for normal case, boundary case, failure case and override. If the SME says "it depends", the next question is what it depends on: a rule, judgement, system limitation, approval or convention.

Do not force consensus from one SME where authority belongs elsewhere. Capture expert interpretation, then route the decision to the requirement owner or authorised forum.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   As-is evidence · Open questions · SME knowledge
This:       Focused elicitation · Confirmation · Unresolved points
Downstream: Requirements · Business rules · Decision log
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA prepares questions, captures answers and distinguishes fact from interpretation |
| Approves | Requirement or process owner approves conclusions requiring formal commitment |
| Consumes | Delivery, design and testing teams consume clarified business knowledge |
| Independently challenges | Relevant control functions challenge interpretations within their mandate |

### Permanent rule

Never spend SME time discovering information available in documents you could have read. Use expert time for judgement, exceptions, ambiguity and decisions.

### Never confuse [Expert input] and [Formal approval]

**Expert input** - explains how a rule, process, product or system works.

---

**Formal approval** - commits an authorised owner to the requirement or decision.

### Permanent trap

Leaving the meeting with notes but no distinction between what was confirmed, what was assumed and what remains open. Ambiguous notes become false requirements once circulated.

<Recall
  q="What should a short SME meeting prioritise?"
  a="High-risk questions that existing evidence cannot answer; routine facts should be resolved beforehand."
/>

<Recall
  q="What should follow &quot;it depends&quot;?"
  a="Identify the rule, threshold, scenario, judgement, approval or constraint creating the dependency."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why can an SME answer still need escalation?"
  a="Expertise explains the subject but does not confer authority to choose between business, control or scope options."
