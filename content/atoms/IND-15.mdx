### Core mechanism

| Operating object | Required discipline |
| :--- | :--- |
| Disbursement | Each RE's funded share must be traceable |
| Servicing | Roles, collection channels and borrower communication are contractually defined |
| Cash allocation | Principal, interest and charges are split under the agreed mechanism |
| Reconciliation | Both REs reconcile balances, ageing and collections |
| CIC reporting | Each RE reports its share consistently with the arrangement |
| Stress status | Required SMA/NPA information is exchanged so classification is synchronised where the Directions require it |

One-line reminder: IRACP determines Indian prudential stress status; ECL staging remains an IMP concept.

The 2025 co-lending framework specifically addresses borrower-level information sharing and alignment. That does not mean the two REs necessarily hold identical provisions, because provisioning can depend on each entity's applicable prudential rulebook.

### Permanent rule

Operational servicing may be centralised, but regulatory ownership remains divided according to each RE's retained exposure.

### Never confuse [Classification synchronisation] and [Identical provisioning]

**Classification synchronisation** — aligned recognition of borrower stress or NPA status where required.

---

**Identical provisioning** — the same provision percentage on both books; this does not automatically follow because entity-specific prudential rules can differ.

### Permanent trap

Booking the entire co-lent exposure in one RE and treating the other RE as only a funding provider. That defeats the borrower-level risk-sharing structure.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Bank participant
Applies bank prudential rules to its share.

#### NBFC participant
Applies the applicable NBFC prudential rules to its share, including any layer-based delta.
