The digital lending directions specify that every app or digital lender must provide clear contact details for grievance redressal. This includes an official Nodal Officer name/contact who handles issues. If a borrower complains (overcharging, collection practice, etc.), the lender must address it as per RBI’s standard timelines (e.g. 3-4 weeks resolution). Firms must also publish details of the Banking Ombudsman scheme, since appellants can approach it if unsatisfied. 

Moreover, under RBI’s 2016 Outsourcing guidelines (augmented in 2022 for recovery agents), banks must ensure their fintech partners and any recovery agents follow proper conduct. Customers should not face harassment or 24/7 calls. If needed, the customer’s complaint to the lender can be escalated through RBI’s Integrated Ombudsman Scheme. Tech-savvy customers often get quick redress via email or app, but the requirement is to maintain logs of all complaints and resolutions. 

In effect, RBI’s digital lending rules tighten accountability. A startup cannot avoid RBI oversight by claiming to be a “technology platform” – it must still honor consumer protection rules. Consumers have the same rights (transparency, appeals) as with brick-and-mortar banks when dealing via an app. For example, if a fintech breaches RBI’s guidelines, the bank’s grievance cell must still be answerable. Some fintechs go further (e.g. fintech ombudsmen) but legally the RBI framework covers all.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
All digital lenders must publish and operate a grievance redress system.  Complaints must first go to a designated Nodal Officer. The Banking Ombudsman scheme covers digital loan disputes.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Neglecting offline complaint channels. Assuming “if it’s digital, complaints must be emailed” – RBI requires accessible contact points and escalation. Also, sometimes start-ups think only their tech side is liable, but RBI holds the actual NBFC/bank accountable for the borrower’s redress.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse>
**Lending app support** — the platform or lender’s own customer service/email.  
**Banking Ombudsman** — RBI’s formal consumer forum. After exhausting internal redress, the borrower can approach it about digital loan issues (as with any bank).
</NeverConfuse>

<Delta j="IN">
— RBI specifically requires digital loan grievance offices. Elsewhere, similar tech-lending regulations are nascent; EU has Digital Finance proposals, but no RBI-style scheme yet.
</Delta>
