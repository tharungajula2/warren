### Core mechanism

One-line reminder: Ind AS 109 uses forward-looking ECL and staging; those mechanics belong to IMP.

```text
Ind AS 109 ECL measured
    ↓
RBI prudential requirement measured separately
    ↓
Amounts compared under the applicable RBI framework
    ↓
Any prudential shortfall is handled through the prescribed reserve/floor mechanism
```

| Object | Owner | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ECL allowance | Ind AS 109 | Accounting impairment |
| IRACP classification | RBI | Prudential asset-quality status |
| Prudential minimum | RBI | Regulatory loss buffer |
| Impairment reserve / prudential shortfall treatment | RBI framework for Ind AS entities | Prevents accounting ECL below the prudential floor from weakening regulatory protection |

The exact post-consolidation entity-wise wording and reserve mechanics are UNVERIFIED here as at 31 August 2026 where not separately established. No percentage or formula is inferred.

Commercial banks are in a separate transition: as at 31 August 2026 the current IRACP framework remains in force, with an RBI prudential ECL framework scheduled for 1 April 2027.

### Where it sits

### Permanent rule

Accounting ECL does not replace RBI prudential classification for entities that must comply with both regimes.

### Never confuse [Ind AS 109 staging] and [SMA/NPA]

**Ind AS 109 staging** — accounting impairment status based on credit deterioration and default concepts.

---

**SMA/NPA** — RBI prudential repayment-stress and non-performing classifications.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Ind AS-applicable NBFCs and HFCs
Run accounting ECL and RBI prudential requirements in parallel.

#### Commercial banks
Current IRACP remains in force on 31 August 2026; prudential ECL transition begins 1 April 2027.
