### Core mechanism

| Element | What the test does |
| :--- | :--- |
| Six Basel EVE shocks | parallel up/down · steepener · flattener · short-rate up/down |
| Calibration | recalibrated shock sizes effective **1 January 2026** |
| Currency | apply currency-specific shocks to material currencies |
| Behaviour | stress NMD beta/decay, prepayment, redemption, pipeline and drawdown where scenario-sensitive |
| Internal scenarios | add bank-specific stresses beyond prescribed screens where exposures require |

### Flow

```text
Base positions + assumptions
    ↓
Rate-shape and behavioural stresses
    ↓
ΔNII + ΔEVE
    ↓
Internal limits + supervisory outlier tests
    ↓
Hedge / reprice / fund / reduce exposure / capital response
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   NII/EVE · NMDs · options · commitments
This:       Scenario measurement and limit comparison
Downstream: Hedging · ALCO · jurisdictional reporting
```

### Permanent rule

A supervisory outlier threshold is a supervisory screen; internal risk appetite must be set from the bank’s own vulnerability and capacity.

### Permanent trap

Managing to sit just inside a regulatory threshold while ignoring earnings risk, basis risk, behavioural stress or concentrations not captured by the prescribed screen.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI’s 2025 forthcoming Directions specify ΔEVE and ΔNII measurement and a supervisory outlier architecture including a maximum ΔEVE decline threshold of 15% of Tier 1 capital. **As at August 2026 these requirements are not yet in force**; RBI/DoR/2025-26/173 says the effective date will be communicated separately.

#### UK (PRA)
IRRBB is supervised through the PRA’s Pillar 2 framework and firm-specific assessment. The PRA’s May 2026 revised Pillar 2 methodology is published but becomes effective **1 January 2027**; as at August 2026 that future version is not yet effective.

#### EU
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/856 has applied since **14 May 2024** and specifies supervisory shock scenarios and common assumptions for EVE/NII outlier testing; the EVE architecture includes the 15% of Tier 1 threshold.

#### US
US interagency guidance requires robust IRR measurement, stress and governance but does not impose the EU/Basel supervisory-outlier package as a single universal domestic template for all banks.
