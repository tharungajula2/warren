### Core mechanism

| Stage | Credit state | Allowance | Interest basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Stage 1 | No SICR since initial recognition, subject to applicable expedients | 12-month ECL | Gross carrying amount |
| Stage 2 | SICR, but not credit-impaired | Lifetime ECL | Gross carrying amount |
| Stage 3 | Credit-impaired | Lifetime ECL | Amortised cost for assets that became credit-impaired after origination |

### Flow

```text
Initial recognition
    ↓
Stage 1
12-month ECL
    ↓ SICR
Stage 2
Lifetime ECL
    ↓ credit-impaired
Stage 3
Lifetime ECL

Improvement can reverse migration when the governing criteria are no longer met.
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Origination risk · Current risk · Credit-impairment evidence
This:       Stage 1 · Stage 2 · Stage 3
Downstream: ECL horizon · Interest recognition · Disclosure
```

### Permanent rule

Stage is a credit-risk state that determines measurement treatment. It is not simply a delinquency band.

### Never confuse [Stage 2] and [Stage 3]

**Stage 2** — credit risk has increased significantly since initial recognition, but the asset is not credit-impaired.

---

**Stage 3** — events have occurred that detrimentally affect expected future cash flows.

### Permanent trap

Mapping Stage 1 / 2 / 3 directly to 0–29 / 30–89 / 90+ DPD reverses the framework. Delinquency is evidence and backstop information; it is not the complete definition of the stages.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Commercial banks do not currently use the IFRS 9 stage model. RBI’s framework effective 1 April 2027 introduces Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 for provisioning while retaining separate NPA-classification rules.

#### UK (UKEB)
No delta from UK-adopted IFRS 9.

#### EU (European Commission / EBA)
No accounting delta from EU-adopted IFRS 9; EBA guidance adds supervisory expectations around sound implementation.

#### US (FASB)
CECL has no equivalent Stage 1-to-Stage 2 horizon switch for amortised-cost assets.

<Recall
  q="What changes at Stage 1 → Stage 2?"
  a="The allowance horizon moves from 12-month ECL to lifetime ECL."
/>

<Recall
  q="What distinguishes Stage 3 from Stage 2?"
  a="The asset is credit-impaired."
/>

<Recall
  q="Does CECL use the IFRS 9 three-stage architecture?"
  a="No — CECL generally recognises lifetime expected loss from initial recognition without an SICR horizon switch."
/>
