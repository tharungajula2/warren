### Core mechanism

```text
Individual model risk
    ↓
Common dependencies + correlated assumptions + shared vendors
    ↓
Aggregate model risk
    ↓
Risk appetite / tolerance
    ↓
Accept · Mitigate · Restrict · Diversify · Exit
```

| Aggregation lens | Example question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Common data | How many models depend on the same customer master or market feed? |
| Common methodology | Would one invalid assumption impair several models? |
| Model chains | Does an upstream output feed many downstream decisions? |
| Vendor concentration | Could one provider outage or defect affect many models? |
| Business concentration | Are critical decisions clustered around a small model set? |
| Technology concentration | Do several models share one platform or implementation layer? |

Risk appetite states how much model risk the institution is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives. Tolerance converts that high-level appetite into actionable boundaries, escalation triggers or control expectations.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Inventory · Dependencies · Tiering · Business strategy
This:       Aggregate model risk and appetite
Downstream: Governance priorities · Mitigation · Limits · Reporting
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Model owners | Report model-level risk and dependencies |
| Enterprise MRM | Aggregates risk and identifies concentration |
| Senior management | Operates within approved appetite |
| Board / relevant committee | Oversees appetite and material breaches |

### Permanent rule

Portfolio diversification of financial exposures does not guarantee diversification of model risk. Shared models, data and assumptions can create hidden common-mode failure.

### Never confuse [Model tier] and [Model risk appetite]

**Model tier** - classification of one model used to scale its controls.

---

**Model risk appetite** - enterprise statement of how much model risk the institution is prepared to accept and under what constraints.

### Permanent trap

Adding model risk scores as though they were independent. Aggregation must recognise dependencies and correlated failure, not just arithmetic totals.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. The exact appetite framework is institution-specific, but assessing model risk both individually and in aggregate and governing material exposures is a common MRM principle.
