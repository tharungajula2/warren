### Core mechanism

PSL achievement is measured against the applicable RBI denominator, commonly expressed through Adjusted Net Bank Credit or the prescribed credit-equivalent base.

PSL ratio = qualifying priority-sector credit / applicable PSL denominator

| Entity | Overall architecture as at 31 August 2026 |
| :--- | :--- |
| Domestic commercial banks and qualifying foreign banks | Overall target plus prescribed sub-targets |
| Small Finance Banks | Overall target of 60% under the 2025 Directions, plus applicable sub-targets |
| Regional Rural Banks | Separate overall target of 75%, plus applicable sub-targets |
| Urban cooperative banks and other covered entities | Entity-specific target structure under applicable Directions |
| NBFCs | No general bank-style PSL target merely because they lend; their loans may matter through permitted on-lending or partnership structures |

The 2025 Directions refreshed the PSL framework and were amended on 7 August 2026. A lender must therefore identify its own entity category before applying any target percentage.

This atom owns target applicability only. Whether a particular agriculture, MSME, housing or other exposure qualifies is a separate eligibility question.

### Permanent rule

A loan contributes to PSL only when both conditions hold: the lender is measuring against an applicable PSL obligation and the exposure satisfies a qualifying category rule.

### Permanent trap

Assuming one headline target applies to every bank. SFBs, RRBs, commercial banks and cooperative banks can have different target structures.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Small Finance Banks
Overall PSL target is 60% under the 2025 Directions as at 31 August 2026.

#### Regional Rural Banks
A separate 75% overall target applies under the current framework.

#### Other covered banks
Apply the target and sub-target structure specified for that entity class.
