### Core mechanism

| Step | Prudential effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| NPA trigger reached | Exposure becomes non-performing under the applicable rule |
| Income recognition restriction | Unrealised interest cannot continue to be treated like normal performing income |
| Sub-standard | Initial NPA class under the prudential seasoning framework |
| Doubtful | NPA remains unresolved beyond the prescribed period |
| Loss | Asset is identified as uncollectible or of such little value that full recognition of loss is required |

For many term-loan exposures, the familiar trigger is more than 90 days overdue. Product-specific rules matter: cash credit/overdraft facilities use "out of order" criteria, and certain agricultural or other specialised exposures can follow different tests.

RBI also applies borrower/account-level disciplines intended to prevent selective treatment of one facility while another exposure to the same borrower is non-performing, subject to the applicable Direction.

Exact seasoning periods and special-product rules in the post-28 November 2025 entity-wise Directions are not restated here unless established; any unresolved reference is treated as UNVERIFIED as at 31 August 2026.

### Permanent rule

Once an exposure is NPA, income recognition cannot continue as though contractual accrual automatically equals realised income.

### Never confuse [NPA status] and [Write-off]

**NPA status** — prudential classification of a live credit exposure.

---

**Write-off** — accounting removal of some or all of an exposure; legal recovery rights can continue.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Term lending
More-than-90-days overdue is the core trigger for ordinary term-loan treatment.

#### Revolving and specialised facilities
Apply the product-specific "out of order" or other prescribed test.
