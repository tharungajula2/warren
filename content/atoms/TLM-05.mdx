### Core mechanism

| Question | Banking book | Trading book |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main structural lens here | IRRBB / ALM | trading market risk |
| Typical positions | customer loans, deposits, term funding, structural securities | positions meeting trading-book assignment criteria |
| Accounting category determines it? | No | No |
| Rate movement changes value economically? | Yes | Yes |

A fixed-rate bond does not become economically insensitive because it is placed in the banking book. The classification changes how risk is governed, measured and capitalised; it does not create the underlying sensitivity.

### Flow

```text
Instrument / position
    ↓
Prudential assignment rules
    ↓
Banking book ───────────── Trading book
    ↓                          ↓
IRRBB / structural ALM        market-risk framework
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Treasury mandate · ALM map · capital architecture
This:       Prudential perimeter for structural rate analysis
Downstream: IRRBB sources · structural FX boundary · hedging
```

### Permanent rule

Prudential book classification selects the regulatory framework; it does not determine whether market prices or rates can affect economic value.

### Never confuse [Prudential book classification] and [Accounting classification]

**Prudential book classification** — regulatory assignment for risk and capital purposes.

---

**Accounting classification** — financial-reporting recognition and measurement basis.

### Permanent trap

Using “banking book” as a synonym for “held to maturity”. A banking-book item can be sold, fair-valued or hedged; the concepts answer different questions.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI’s prudential capital framework distinguishes trading and banking treatment, while its 2025 forthcoming IRRBB chapter applies to banking-book positions. The forthcoming IRRBB Directions are **not yet in force as at August 2026**; RBI/DoR/2025-26/173 states that the effective date will be communicated separately.

#### UK (PRA)
No mechanism delta. PRA supervision distinguishes traded market risk from IRRBB/non-trading-book risk; local rule scope and permissions govern assignment.

#### EU
No mechanism delta. CRR/CRD and EBA IRRBB rules distinguish non-trading-book IRRBB from trading market risk.

#### US
No mechanism delta. US capital and supervisory frameworks separately address market risk and structural interest-rate risk; institution-specific scope follows applicable US rules and guidance.
