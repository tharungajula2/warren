### Core mechanism

| Situation | Upgrade logic |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ordinary arrears-driven NPA | Borrower must clear the amounts required by the current prudential rule so the account is no longer overdue in the prescribed manner |
| Multiple facilities to same borrower | Borrower-level treatment can require all relevant arrears to be regularised before upgrade |
| Restructured exposure | Upgrade depends on the post-restructuring performance conditions in the applicable resolution framework |
| Technical write-off | Does not itself cure borrower default or restore standard status |

The legacy pack's generic "two quarters or one year" cure rule is not carried forward. The correct performance period depends on the applicable current framework and exposure type.

RBI's 28 November 2025 consolidation reorganised the prudential directions. Exact post-consolidation upgrade wording for every entity and restructured exposure is UNVERIFIED here as at 31 August 2026; no unsupported period is stated.

### Permanent rule

A payment that reduces arrears is not automatically enough for upgrade; the account must satisfy the full applicable RBI cure condition.

### Never confuse [Cure] and [Restructuring]

**Cure** — borrower performance removes the prudential default condition.

---

**Restructuring** — contractual terms are modified because of borrower financial difficulty or resolution needs; separate upgrade conditions can follow.

### Permanent trap

Upgrading an account immediately because one overdue instalment was paid. The test is whether the account as a whole has been regularised under the applicable rule.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Ordinary bank/NBFC exposures
Apply the current entity-wise cure and borrower-level rules.

#### Restructured exposures
Apply the additional performance conditions in the relevant resolution framework.
