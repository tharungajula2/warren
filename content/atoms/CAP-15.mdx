### Core mechanism

| Protection | First calculation effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Eligible guarantee | Substitute eligible protection-provider treatment on protected portion |
| Eligible financial collateral | Adjust exposure and collateral values, then recognise residual exposure |
| Eligible netting | Replace qualifying gross claims with prescribed net exposure |
| Currency / maturity mismatch | Reduce recognition through prescribed adjustment |

```text
Collateral market value
    ↓
Apply collateral haircut
    ↓
Recognised collateral value
    ↓
Offset against adjusted exposure
    ↓
Residual exposure
    ↓
Risk weight
```

A haircut is not a predicted collateral loss. It is a prudential valuation adjustment for volatility, holding period, currency mismatch or related risk. A guarantee is therefore not "collateral with a zero haircut"; it follows a different risk-transfer mechanism.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   CRM eligibility
This:       CRM calculation mechanics
Downstream: Post-CRM exposure · credit RWA · capital ratio
```

### Formula

```text
Recognised collateral value = market value × (1 − applicable haircut), before any additional prescribed mismatch adjustment

Residual exposure = adjusted exposure − recognised collateral value, floored as required by the applicable method
```

### Permanent rule

Use the prudential mechanism that matches the legal protection; do not mix substitution and collateral-value adjustment.

### Never confuse [Haircut] and [Risk weight]

**Haircut** — adjustment to recognised exposure or collateral value.

---

**Risk weight** — factor applied after exposure measurement to determine RWA.

### Permanent trap

Using the guarantor's risk weight for pledged securities simply because both are "security" applies the wrong CRM method.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in the conceptual mechanics. Supervisory haircut tables, eligible instruments and national options differ.

<Recall
  q="What changes first under guarantee substitution?"
  a="The credit-risk treatment of the protected amount shifts to the eligible protection provider."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why haircut collateral before netting it against exposure?"
  a="Its value can move before the bank can close out or realise the protection."
