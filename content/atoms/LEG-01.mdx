## Enforceability Conditions

Netting agreements must be supported by written, independent legal opinions in all relevant jurisdictions to qualify for capital reduction.

<Rule label="WRITTEN LEGAL OPINION">
Legal opinions proving close-out netting enforceability in insolvency must be updated annually per counterparty jurisdiction.
</Rule>
