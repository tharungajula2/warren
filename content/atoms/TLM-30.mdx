### Core mechanism

| Dimension | IN — RBI | UK — PRA | EU — CRR/EBA | US — agencies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Liquidity | local LCR + SLR/MSF/FALLCR; NSFR | PRA LCR/NSFR + ILAAP | CRR LCR/NSFR + ILAAP/SREP | tailored LCR/NSFR + Regulation YY |
| IRRBB | forthcoming; **not effective Aug 2026** | Pillar 2 | EBA GL + 2024 RTS + CSRBB | interagency guidance |

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Full treasury/ALM control loop
This:       Dated local implementation
Downstream: Entity policy · reporting · supervision
```

### Permanent rule

Never import another jurisdiction’s acronym, threshold or effective date merely because the mechanism is common.

### Permanent trap

Relabelling a foreign policy with the local regulator. Scope, calibration, terminology and transition status can differ.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
LCR changes are in force from **1 April 2026**; the SLR HQLA carve-out remains **2% MSF + 16% FALLCR** as at August 2026. NSFR applies since **1 October 2021**. RBI’s 28 November 2025 Forthcoming Instructions say IRRBB’s effective date will be communicated separately: **not implemented as at August 2026**.

#### UK (PRA)
SS24/15 has applied since **20 January 2026**. July 2026 liquidity amendments are **proposals**, with consultation closing **11 September 2026** and proposed implementation **1 January 2027**.

#### EU
EBA/GL/2022/14 applies from **31 December 2023**. Delegated Regulations (EU) 2024/856 and 2024/857 apply from **14 May 2024**. LCR/NSFR remain within the CRR framework.

#### US
The 2019 category-tailoring framework remains the LCR scope architecture as at August 2026; NSFR applies since **1 July 2021**. Regulation YY supplies liquidity governance for covered firms.
