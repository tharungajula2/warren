### Core mechanism

```text
Loan terms determined
    ↓
KFS prepared in prescribed form
    ↓
APR and material charges disclosed
    ↓
Borrower receives and acknowledges terms
    ↓
Loan agreement and servicing must remain consistent with disclosed terms
```

| Item | Conduct purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Interest rate | Shows the contractual borrowing rate |
| APR | Captures the all-in annualised cost using the prescribed methodology |
| Fees and charges | Prevents undisclosed price components |
| Penal charges | Separates a contractual penalty from disguised compounding of interest |
| Repayment schedule | Makes timing and total repayment obligations legible |
| Grievance contact | Gives the borrower a route to challenge conduct or servicing |

KFS requirements apply across specified retail and MSME lending contexts and are also built into digital-lending controls. This atom owns the disclosure object itself; a digital channel does not create a separate economics standard.

RBI reorganised responsible-business-conduct instructions in 2025. The exact cross-entity post-consolidation reference set is UNVERIFIED here as at 31 August 2026, so no unestablished coverage threshold is stated.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Product pricing · Sanction terms
This:       KFS and all-in disclosure
Downstream: Digital lending · Customer consent · Servicing conduct
```

### Permanent rule

A fee that forms part of the borrower's cost cannot be made compliant by hiding it outside the stated interest rate.

### Never confuse [Interest rate] and [APR]

**Interest rate** — the contractual rate charged on the loan balance.

---

**APR** — the prescribed annualised measure of total borrowing cost, including covered charges.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Banks and covered NBFCs
KFS and fair-practice requirements apply through the relevant RBI conduct Directions.

#### Digital lending
The same disclosure principle is reinforced through digital-lending-specific delivery and consent requirements.
