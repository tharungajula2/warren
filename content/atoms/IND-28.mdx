### Core mechanism

```text
Secured credit defaults and statutory conditions are met
    ↓
Creditor issues the required demand notice
    ↓
If default is not cured, permitted enforcement measures may follow
    ↓
Borrower may challenge qualifying measures before DRT
    ↓
Security is realised or dispute is adjudicated
```

| Route | Core object |
| :--- | :--- |
| SARFAESI | Enforcement of qualifying security interest by an eligible secured creditor |
| DRT | Specialised recovery/adjudication forum under the debt-recovery statute |
| DRAT | Appellate forum for DRT decisions |
| ARC involvement | Acquisition/reconstruction and enforcement of stressed financial assets under the statutory framework |

SARFAESI does not turn an unsecured personal loan into a secured claim. Eligibility, excluded security types and monetary/forum thresholds are statutory and can change; no legacy threshold is repeated here unless established.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   NPA/default · Security interest
This:       Secured enforcement / tribunal route
Downstream: Asset sale · Recovery · IBC interaction
```

### Permanent rule

SARFAESI power depends on a qualifying secured interest and statutory eligibility; default alone is not enough.

### Never confuse [SARFAESI] and [DRT]

**SARFAESI** — statutory secured-creditor enforcement mechanism.

---

**DRT** — tribunal that hears recovery applications and challenges within its jurisdiction.

### Permanent trap

Using SARFAESI as shorthand for all Indian debt collection. Unsecured claims and other recovery routes sit outside that secured-enforcement mechanism.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Eligible secured creditors
May use SARFAESI where statutory conditions are met.

#### Unsecured or otherwise excluded claims
Must rely on other contractual, tribunal, insolvency or court routes as applicable.
