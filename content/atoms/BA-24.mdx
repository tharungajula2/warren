### Core mechanism

Readiness is a set of owned operating conditions.

| Readiness area | Evidence question |
| :--- | :--- |
| People | Are the required roles staffed and aware of new responsibilities? |
| Procedures | Are operating and exception procedures current and usable? |
| Access | Do users and support teams have approved entitlements? |
| Controls | Are new or changed controls assigned, executable and evidenced? |
| Reconciliation | Can outputs be checked and breaks owned? |
| Support | Is incident, query and escalation ownership live? |
| Training | Can affected users perform the changed process? |
| Communications | Do dependent teams know what changes and when? |
| Calendar | Has the release been assessed against close, filing and peak processing? |
| Contingency | Can the business operate if a key component fails? |

```text
Technical readiness + Business readiness + Control readiness + Support readiness -&gt; Go-live candidate
```

Month-end matters because operational tolerance changes. A credit migration that is safe on a quiet Tuesday may be unsafe before statement production, provisioning, finance close or a reporting cutoff. The BA does not impose a universal blackout calendar; the BA identifies which business events make the chosen date risky and who may accept that risk.

Readiness should distinguish "complete" from "waived". A missing procedure signed off as a controlled temporary condition is not the same state as a completed procedure.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Acceptance · Defect decisions · Parallel evidence · Operating constraints
This:       People · Process · Control · Support readiness
Downstream: Go/no-go · Cutover · Stabilisation
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA coordinates readiness evidence; each operational owner produces domain evidence |
| Approves | Business and service owners approve readiness within their areas |
| Consumes | Go/no-go authority uses the integrated readiness view |
| Independently challenges | Operational risk, compliance or control functions challenge material gaps |

### Permanent rule

"System ready" and "business ready" are different states. Production transition requires both, plus explicit ownership of any waived readiness condition.

### Never confuse [Readiness gap] and [Defect]

**Readiness gap** - an operating prerequisite such as training, access, procedure or support is incomplete.

---

**Defect** - delivered behaviour fails an agreed requirement or expected result.

### Permanent trap

Treating training completion percentages as proof of readiness. Attendance does not prove that critical roles can execute new decisions, controls and exceptions.

No delta.
