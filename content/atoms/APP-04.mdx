## Returns + Portfolio Risk

Log return:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle r_t=\ln\left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-1}}\right)" />

Portfolio expected return:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle E(R_p)=w^\top \mu" />

Portfolio variance:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle \sigma_p^2=w^\top \Sigma w" />

Portfolio volatility:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle \sigma_p=\sqrt{w^\top \Sigma w}" />

### Key insight

Portfolio risk does **not** equal:

```text
weighted average of individual risks
```

because:

> **Covariance determines diversification benefit.**

## Monte Carlo

**10,000 random portfolios**

Each:

<Flow>
random positive weights
→ normalise to sum 1
→ long-only
→ fully invested
</Flow>

Score used:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle \frac{Portfolio\ Return}{Portfolio\ Volatility}" />

### Important

This is **Sharpe-ratio-style**, because the notebook does not explicitly subtract a risk-free rate.
