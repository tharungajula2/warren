### Core mechanism



### Formula

```text
FV = P × (1 + r)^n
FV = future value
P = present amount
r = rate per compounding period
n = number of consistent periods

PV = FV / (1 + r)^n
PV = present value obtained by reversing the same compounding assumptions

For a nominal annual rate compounded m times per year:

FV = P × (1 + r_nom / m)^(m × n)
r_nom = nominal annual rate
m = compounding periods per year
n = number of years

The rate convention, cash-flow dates, and period count must describe the same time basis.
```

### Flow

```text
Cash-flow dates fixed
    ↓
Rate convention identified
    ↓
Convert to consistent period basis
    ↓
Compound forward / discount backward
    ↓
Aggregate comparable values
```

### Permanent rule

Never apply a rate until its unit of time and compounding convention are consistent with the cash-flow timeline.

### Never confuse [Nominal annual rate] and [Effective annual rate]

**Nominal annual rate** — an annualised quote whose realised one-year growth depends on the stated intra-year compounding convention.

---

**Effective annual rate** — the actual one-year growth rate after the convention is reflected.

### Permanent trap

Multiplying or dividing an annual rate by 12 without checking what the quoted rate means. Some conversions are simple only under a stated convention; others require compounding logic.
