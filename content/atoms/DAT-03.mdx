### Core mechanism

| Principle | Required capability | Evidence in practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 Governance | Senior management and the board oversee risk data aggregation and reporting within the risk-management framework | Policy, scope, accountability, resources, independent review, issue escalation |
| 2 Data architecture and IT infrastructure | Architecture supports accurate, complete and timely aggregation in normal and stressed conditions | Documented architecture, integrated taxonomies, controlled interfaces, resilience, change impact assessment |

```text
Business strategy or structural change
    ↓
Assess impact on data scope, architecture and reporting
    ↓
Assign accountable decisions and resources
    ↓
Integrate sources, definitions and controls
    ↓
Validate normal and stress capability
```

A merger, new product or system replacement is therefore a risk-data event, not just a technology event. If the change creates a new source, mapping, identifier or legal-entity boundary, the reporting chain must absorb it without losing traceability.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   BCBS 239 architecture · Bank structure · Business and technology change
This:       Governance and data architecture
Downstream: Authoritative sources · Lineage · Aggregation capability · Reporting resilience
```

### Ownership

| Role | Governance consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Board and senior management | Set direction, approve framework, demand remediation |
| Business data owners | Account for meaning and fitness of governed data |
| IT and data engineering | Build and operate architecture to approved requirements |
| Independent challenge | Tests design, implementation and evidence |

### Permanent rule

Architecture choices cannot be allowed to make material risk unknowable at the level where management or supervisors need it.

### Permanent trap

Treating a successful nightly load as proof of architecture adequacy. Principle 2 is about the end-to-end ability to aggregate under changing and stressed conditions, not batch completion alone.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The principles are universal; local supervisors may impose different remediation mechanisms or additional expectations.
