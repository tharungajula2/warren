### Core mechanism

Requirements become usable when each statement has one obligation, an owner and a clear condition of satisfaction.

| Element | Requirement question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Actor or process | Who or what needs the capability? |
| Required behaviour | What must happen? |
| Trigger | When does it apply? |
| Business rule | Under what condition or logic? |
| Boundary | What cases are included or excluded? |
| Constraint | What operational limit must hold? |
| Owner | Who confirms the meaning? |
| Acceptance condition | What evidence will show the need is met? |

A lending migration requirement might say the servicing process must preserve the approved repayment schedule and account status through migration. The detailed source-to-target mapping is a data control owned elsewhere; BA carries only the business obligation and the impact of any mapping decision.

A good rule is deterministic where the business is deterministic: "If condition X holds at cutoff, classify as Y." If judgement is intended, say who exercises it, what evidence they use and what approval applies. Do not convert real judgement into fake algorithmic precision.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   As-is process · SME knowledge · Scope
This:       Business requirements · Business rules
Downstream: Sign-off · Design challenge · Traceability · Acceptance
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA drafts and structures requirement statements and business rules |
| Approves | Requirement owner confirms business meaning |
| Consumes | Architecture, technology, data, testing and operations consume the approved obligation |
| Independently challenges | Control functions challenge requirements affecting their mandate |

### Permanent rule

A requirement states what must be true for the business; design states how the solution will make it true. Precision does not require the BA to take design ownership.

### Never confuse [Business requirement] and [Functional specification]

**Business requirement** - states the required business outcome, behaviour, rule or constraint.

---

**Functional specification** - states how solution components will implement that requirement.

### Permanent trap

Hiding an unresolved decision inside passive language such as "appropriate", "where required" or "as necessary". If the condition matters to implementation or acceptance, define the rule or name the authority that decides it.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta.

<Recall
  q="How can a requirement be precise without becoming design?"
  a="Define actor, trigger, behaviour, rule, boundary, constraint and acceptance condition while leaving implementation choices to specialists."
/>

<Recall
  q="How should genuine judgement appear?"
  a="Name who exercises it, the evidence or conditions considered and any approval required."
