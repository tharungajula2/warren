### Core mechanism

| Mitigant | What must be true | Main failure mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Collateral asset | Identifiable, valued, controllable and realisable | Value falls, asset disappears, sale is delayed |
| Security interest | Validly created and perfected under applicable law | Priority or enforceability fails |
| Guarantee | Legally effective claim on a capable guarantor | Guarantor lacks capacity or defaults with borrower |
| Covenant / structural protection | Trigger and remedy are clear and monitorable | Breach occurs too late or remedy is weak |

**Eligible value is not appraisal value.** A lending or risk value normally considers valuation uncertainty, volatility, liquidation time, prior claims, currency mismatch, concentration and policy/regulatory haircuts.

### Formula

```text
`Adjusted collateral value = Market / appraised value × (1 − haircut)`

`Haircut` — reduction for uncertainty, volatility, liquidation and other prescribed risks.

Haircut ↑ → recognised collateral value ↓ → unsecured exposure / loss severity pressure ↑.

This is a teaching relationship; regulatory frameworks prescribe their own eligible collateral, valuation and haircut rules.
```

### Flow

```text
Collateral / guarantee identified
    ↓
Due diligence → Legal creation / perfection → Valuation → Haircut
    ↓
Monitoring
    ↓
Default / enforcement need
    ↓
Realisation / guarantor payment → Net recovery
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Approved structure · Asset/guarantor due diligence · Legal documentation
This:       Create → Perfect → Value → Haircut → Monitor → Enforce if needed
Downstream: Lending terms · Limit usage · Recovery strategy · LGD evidence
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Credit, legal, valuation and collateral-management teams establish evidence |
| Approves | Credit authority accepts the mitigant within policy |
| Consumes | Operations, monitoring, workout, capital and LGD estimation |
| Challenges | Legal, independent risk, valuation review and audit test perfection and values |

### Permanent rule

A mitigant is worth only what can be legally enforced and economically realised when the borrower is under stress.

### Never confuse [Collateral value] and [Repayment capacity]

**Collateral value** — secondary recovery value available if repayment fails.

---

**Repayment capacity** — the borrower’s ability to meet contractual payments from the primary repayment source.

### Never confuse [Guarantee] and [Collateral]

**Guarantee** — a contractual promise by another person to perform or pay if the obligor does not.

---

**Collateral** — an asset or property interest available to secure recovery.

### Permanent trap

Counting a guarantee at face value without testing legal effectiveness, coverage and guarantor credit quality merely moves the credit risk to an unmeasured second obligor.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta in the economics.** Perfection, priority, insolvency treatment, valuation standards and regulatory recognition are jurisdiction-specific; production treatment must follow local law and the applicable prudential framework.
