**Specialised lending** describes exposures where repayment is closely tied to the cash flows generated by a financed asset or undertaking and where the lender has substantial control or security over those assets under the regulatory definition. Such transactions can behave differently from ordinary diversified corporate lending.

Under Basel’s **supervisory slotting approach**, the bank evaluates prescribed qualitative and quantitative characteristics and places an eligible exposure into a supervisory category. The assigned “slot” then determines the applicable risk weight and expected-loss treatment under the Basel framework.

The intuition is classification before calibration. Rather than saying “the internal PD is x and LGD is y”, slotting asks whether the transaction’s financial strength, political and legal environment, transaction structure, collateral or other specified characteristics place it in a stronger or weaker supervisory bucket.

Slotting is therefore not an external credit rating. The classification is produced through the prudential specialised-lending framework. Nor is it simply a standardised corporate risk weight; it sits within the Basel IRB architecture for specialised lending where the relevant conditions apply.

**Perishable — India’s covered commercial-bank banking-book credit regime becomes the revised Standardised Approach on 1 April 2027, so Basel IRB slotting should not be presented as the Indian domestic capital method.**

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Slotting maps the whole specialised-lending risk profile into a supervisory category; it is not merely another name for a borrower rating.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Applying ordinary corporate PD/LGD logic automatically to every project-style exposure ignores the specialised-lending classification and approach rules.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Slotting category" b="External rating">
**Slotting category** — prudential category assigned to qualifying specialised lending from prescribed risk characteristics.

---
**External rating** — assessment produced by an eligible external credit assessment institution and used only where the applicable rules permit.
</NeverConfuse>
