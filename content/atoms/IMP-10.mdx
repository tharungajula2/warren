### Core mechanism

| Ordinary asset | POCI asset |
| :--- | :--- |
| Not credit-impaired at initial recognition | Credit-impaired at initial recognition |
| Ordinary EIR | Credit-adjusted EIR |
| Stage migration can occur after origination | Initial impairment is embedded in starting economics |
| Allowance reflects required ECL under staging | Subsequent cumulative change in lifetime ECL is recognised |

### Flow

```text
Credit-impaired at initial recognition?
    ├─ No → ordinary IFRS 9 staging
    └─ Yes
         ↓
       POCI
         ↓
Expected credit losses embedded in initial expected cash flows
         ↓
Credit-adjusted EIR
         ↓
Recognise subsequent changes in lifetime ECL
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Acquisition / origination · Credit impairment · Purchase economics
This:       POCI treatment
Downstream: Credit-adjusted EIR · Subsequent ECL change
```

### Permanent rule

POCI starts from an impaired credit state; it is not an ordinary new asset that happens to be placed directly into Stage 3.

### Never confuse [POCI] and [Asset that later becomes Stage 3]

**POCI** — credit-impaired when first recognised by the reporting entity.

---

**Later Stage 3 asset** — not credit-impaired initially, but becomes credit-impaired after recognition.

### Permanent trap

Adding a conventional Day-1 Stage 3 allowance on top of pricing that already reflects expected credit impairment can double-count the initial credit loss under IFRS 9.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
Current commercial-bank IRACP has no Ind AS 109 POCI model. RBI’s bank ECL framework from 1 April 2027 separately identifies POCI-type treatment. Applicable Ind AS NBFCs follow Ind AS requirements.

#### UK (UKEB)
No delta from IFRS 9 POCI accounting.

#### EU (European Commission)
No delta from IFRS 9 POCI accounting.

#### US (FASB)
ASC 326 uses purchased credit deteriorated, or PCD, accounting. The acquisition-date allowance is added to purchase price to establish the initial amortised-cost basis: a different architecture from IFRS 9 POCI.
