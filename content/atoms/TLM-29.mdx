### Core mechanism

Positions + assumptions → integrated measures → limits/appetite → ALCO decision → execution → remeasurement → escalation.

### Ownership

| Function | Produces | Approves / decides | Consumes | Challenges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Treasury / ALM | NII/EVE, funding, liquidity, FTP analysis | delegated execution | ALCO decisions | — |
| ALCO | integrated balance-sheet view | funding/hedging/pricing actions within mandate | risk and finance analysis | assumptions through committee debate |
| Business lines | forecasts and product behaviour | customer decisions within limits | FTP and limits | economics/feasibility |
| Finance | earnings/accounting/capital effects | finance policies | treasury forecasts | reconciliation |
| Independent risk | risk measures/limit monitoring as designed | risk limits within delegation | ALM data | models, assumptions, breaches |
| Board / board risk committee | appetite-level reporting | risk appetite and key policies | escalation | senior-management effectiveness |

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   All treasury/ALM measures · FND risk appetite
This:       Governance converting measurement into action
Downstream: Jurisdiction-specific requirements · recurring balance-sheet cycle
```

### Permanent rule

No material ALM action should be approved on one favourable metric when it worsens another material constraint on the same balance sheet.

### Never confuse [First-line treasury control] and [Independent risk challenge]

**First-line treasury control** — manages the bank’s structural positions within authority.

---

**Independent risk challenge** — sets/challenges risk standards and assesses whether first-line measurement and actions remain within appetite; it does not become the dealing owner.

### Permanent trap

Running ALCO as a dashboard meeting. A committee that reviews ratios without making or escalating balance-sheet decisions is observing risk, not governing it.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta in core governance logic. Legal committee mandates, board responsibilities and supervisory documentation differ.

<Recall
  q="What makes ALCO a control rather than a reporting forum?"
  a="It converts integrated measures into accountable pricing, funding, hedging, limit or balance-sheet actions and remeasures the result."
/>

<Recall
  q="Why must an action be remeasured across metrics?"
  a="One transaction can improve liquidity while hurting earnings, EVE, capital, currency concentration or funding cost."
