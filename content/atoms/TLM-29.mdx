The common core makes cross-border work look deceptively uniform. LCR asks about 30-day stressed liquidity; NSFR asks about structural stable funding; IRRBB measures banking-book exposure from adverse interest-rate movements. Basel provides the international architecture, but each jurisdiction determines legal scope, local calibration and supervisory process.

India implements Basel-style LCR and NSFR but overlays LCR with the statutory SLR and MSF/FALLCR mechanism. NSFR has applied to covered commercial banks since 1 October 2021. RBI’s November 2025 consolidated “forthcoming” Directions place the new IRRBB framework in Pillar 2/ICAAP, require ΔEVE and ΔNII, prescribe stress testing and use the 15% Tier 1 EVE outlier threshold; as of the current Directions, the implementation date is still to be communicated.

The UK’s PRA framework explicitly uses ILAAP as a formal supervisory construct. Current SS24/15, effective 20 January 2026, sets out the PRA’s approach to supervising liquidity and funding risks.

In the EU, LCR and NSFR sit in CRR Part Six, while EBA/GL/2022/14 addresses IRRBB and **credit spread risk in the banking book (CSRBB)**, with associated technical standards for supervisory outlier tests and standardised approaches.

The US adopts LCR and NSFR for covered large organisations through a tailoring framework rather than imposing identical requirements on every bank. US liquidity supervision also uses explicit liquidity-risk management and CFP requirements rather than adopting ILAAP as the universal regulatory label.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Basel comparability does not eliminate jurisdiction-specific legal scope, calibration or supervisory vocabulary.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Copying an EU or UK policy into India or the US by changing the regulator name while leaving ILAAP terminology, scope assumptions and local liquidity mechanics unchanged.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Basel standard" b="Local rule">
**Basel standard** — international prudential architecture produced by the Basel Committee.
---
**Local rule** — the legally applicable national or regional implementation, which may tailor scope, calibration and supervisory process.
</NeverConfuse>

— LCR includes the SLR/MSF/FALLCR interaction; NSFR has applied since 1 October 2021; RBI’s November 2025 IRRBB Directions place the forthcoming regime in Pillar 2/ICAAP but state that implementation date will be communicated separately. [RBI/2020-21/95; RBI/2022-23/25; RBI 2025 Directions]
</Delta>

<Delta j="UK">

<Delta j="EU">
— CRR Part Six contains LCR/NSFR requirements; EBA/GL/2022/14 addresses IRRBB and CSRBB with related technical standards. [CRR; EBA/GL/2022/14]
</Delta>

<Delta j="US">
— LCR/NSFR requirements are tailored by firm risk category rather than uniform across banks; liquidity-risk management and contingency funding are formal supervisory constructs without a universal ILAAP label. [OCC Bulletin 2019-52; Federal Reserve NSFR final rule; 12 CFR 252.34]
</Delta>
