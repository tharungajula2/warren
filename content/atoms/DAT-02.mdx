### Core mechanism

| Group | Principles | What the group owns |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Governance and infrastructure | 1-2 | Governance; data architecture and IT infrastructure |
| Risk data aggregation capabilities | 3-6 | Accuracy and integrity; completeness; timeliness; adaptability |
| Risk reporting practices | 7-11 | Accuracy; comprehensiveness; clarity and usefulness; frequency; distribution |
| Supervisory review, tools and cooperation | 12-14 | Review; remedial action and supervisory measures; home-host cooperation |

```text
Governance and infrastructure
    ↓
Risk data aggregation capabilities
    ↓
Risk reporting practices
    ↓
Supervisory assessment and remediation
```

The standard was issued after the financial crisis exposed banks that could not identify group-wide exposures and concentrations fully, quickly and accurately. It initially targeted global systemically important banks, while supervisors and banks have since used its principles more broadly as a benchmark for risk-data governance.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Financial-crisis aggregation failures · Enterprise risk management
This:       BCBS 239 fourteen-principle framework
Downstream: Governance · Data architecture · Aggregation · Reporting · Supervision
```

### Never confuse [BCBS 239] and [Basel capital rules]

**BCBS 239** - sets principles for risk data aggregation and internal risk reporting capabilities.

---

**Basel capital rules** - determine prudential capital requirements and related calculations; DAT does not re-teach those mechanics.

### Permanent rule

The fourteen principles are not fourteen independent checklist items. Weakness in governance or architecture propagates into aggregation and then into reporting.

### Permanent trap

Reducing BCBS 239 to a data-quality programme. Data quality is essential, but the standard also owns governance, architecture, adaptability, report usefulness, distribution and supervisory remediation.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The BCBS principles themselves do not change by jurisdiction; their legal and supervisory implementation can.
