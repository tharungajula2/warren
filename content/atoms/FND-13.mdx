### Core mechanism



### Formula

```text
P = sum of [CF_t / (1 + i)^t] across all t
P = specified initial carrying amount
CF_t = relevant cash flow at time t
i = effective interest rate per period
t = cash-flow timing in consistent periods

Solve for i.

The cash-flow definition matters: changing which fees, premiums, discounts, or transaction costs belong in CF_t can change the solved rate.
```

### Flow

```text
Define initial amount
    ↓
Define included future cash flows
    ↓
Place each on timeline
    ↓
Solve rate making PV = initial amount
    ↓
Effective interest rate
```

### Permanent rule

EIR has meaning only with a specified cash-flow definition. Change the included cash flows or timing and the solved rate can change.

### Never confuse [Coupon rate] and [Effective interest rate]

**Coupon rate** — contractual rate or formula used to determine stated interest payments.

---

**Effective interest rate** — the rate solving the relevant present-value equation.

### Never confuse [Effective interest rate] and [Current market yield]

**Effective interest rate** — rate defined by the specified initial carrying amount and cash flows.

---

**Current market yield** — return implied by today's market price and expectations; it can move after origination.

### Permanent trap

Using the contractual coupon as EIR without checking price, fees, transaction costs, premiums, discounts, or payment timing.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The rate-solving mathematics is universal. Accounting frameworks determine the exact cash-flow set and how a resulting EIR is subsequently used.
