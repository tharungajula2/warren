### Core mechanism

| Layer | Owns |
| :--- | :--- |
| Supervisory-return directions | Applicability, governance, filing requirements, timelines and portals |
| ADF | Controlled, increasingly automated sourcing from bank systems into RBI reporting |
| CIMS | RBI online platform for return submission, data dissemination and related processes |
| SDMX element-based programme | Phase-wise move toward standardised element-level collection |

```text
Governed bank source data
    ↓
ADF-oriented data flow
    ↓
Return logic, validation and reconciliation
    ↓
CIMS or other designated RBI portal
    ↓
Supervisory return
```

The regulatory baseline changed in 2026. The **Master Direction - Reserve Bank of India (Filing of Supervisory Returns) Directions - 2024**, RBI/DoS.DSG/2023-24/110 and DoS.DSG.No.10/33.01.001/2023-24, dated 27 February 2024, consolidated then-applicable instructions across several supervised-entity classes. For commercial banks, **Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Supervisory Returns) Directions, 2026**, RBI/DoS/2026-27/415 and DoS.CO.DSG.9/33.01.001/2026-27, was issued 31 July 2026 and took immediate effect. It addresses governance, data architecture, reconciliation, source records, automation and online filing.

ADF predates CIMS. RBI's 27 September 2011 FAQ describes correct and consistent data through automated flow; CIMS is a later platform layer.

As at August 2026, SDMX element-based reporting is **not complete**. RBI's Annual Report 2024-25 records application development, select-return testing and a pilot, with phase-wise rollout as the next step.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Indian bank data governance · Regulatory production cycle
This:       RBI returns · ADF · CIMS · evolving SDMX programme
Downstream: Return mappings · CIMS submission · Data-quality remediation
```

### Never confuse [ADF] and [CIMS]

**ADF** - the discipline of controlled, increasingly automated flow from bank systems into regulatory reporting.

---

**CIMS** - an RBI platform for return submission, dissemination and related purposes. Portal use alone does not prove the upstream flow is automated or well governed.

### Permanent rule

Portal submission is the end of the reporting chain, not evidence that upstream data architecture, reconciliation and automation are adequate.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
As at August 2026, commercial banks are governed by the 31 July 2026 Commercial Banks - Supervisory Returns Directions. The 2024 Master Direction is the predecessor consolidation; SDMX element-based reporting remains a phase-wise programme, not a completed universal replacement of return-based reporting.
