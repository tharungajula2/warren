To ensure banks capture all operational losses, the Basel framework defines seven event-type categories.  Every loss should be classified under one of them:
- **Internal Fraud** (e.g. employee theft, unauthorized trading)
- **External Fraud** (e.g. robbery, cyberattacks by outsiders)
- **Employment Practices &amp; Workplace Safety** (e.g. discrimination, health/safety breaches)
- **Clients, Products &amp; Business Practices** (e.g. product mis-selling, fiduciary breaches)
- **Damage to Physical Assets** (fires, natural disasters)
- **Execution, Delivery &amp; Process Management** (errors in transaction processing, failed execution)
- **Business Disruption &amp; System Failures** (IT outages, power loss, pandemic-related closures).

Banks use these categories to tag and aggregate losses so they can report to supervisors.  For example, a trading loss due to a systems bug might be a “process management” event; a hurricane damage to a data center is “physical asset”; a lawsuit from a customer suit is “clients/products”.  This taxonomy helps risk managers ensure no area is overlooked.

<Flow>
[Risk Category] -&gt; [Event Type Example]
+-------------------------+
| Internal Fraud          | Employee embezzles funds
| External Fraud          | Hackers steal customer data
| Employment Practices    | A discrimination lawsuit
| Clients/Business Prac.  | Mis-selling products to clients
| Physical Assets         | Fire destroys a branch
| Execution &amp; Process     | Trade execution error
| Disruption &amp; Systems    | Data center outage
+-------------------------+
</Flow>

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Classify all operational losses under one of Basel’s seven categories.  This is mandatory for risk data consistency.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Don’t neglect the “clients/products” and “employment practices” categories because they sound vague.  For instance, a suit from a client over bad advice goes under “clients/products”, not another category.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Internal Fraud" b="External Fraud">
**Internal Fraud** — loss due to dishonest acts by staff (theft, unauthorized trading).
---
**External Fraud** — similar fraud by outsiders (ATM robbery, hacking by non-employees).
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** All Basel-aligned regimes use these same seven event types for operational risk reporting.
</Rule>
