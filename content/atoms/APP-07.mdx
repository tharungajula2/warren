## Effective Number of Stocks

Diversification measure:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle ENS= \frac{1} {\sum_i w_i^2}" />

Examples:

Equal 100-stock portfolio:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle ENS=100" />

Highly concentrated portfolio:

<Formula math="\Large \displaystyle ENS \ll Number\ of\ holdings" />

Therefore:

> Stock count alone does not measure diversification.
