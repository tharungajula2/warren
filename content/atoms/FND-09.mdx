### Core mechanism

| Route | Current-period effect | Where accumulation appears |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Profit or loss | Included in current profit | Retained earnings after closing and distributions |
| OCI under an applicable IFRS/US GAAP requirement | Outside current profit or loss | Accumulated within equity, subject to the framework |
| Direct reserve/equity route under another framework | Depends on governing rule | Relevant reserve within equity |

Some OCI items are later **reclassified** (“recycled”) into profit or loss when the relevant standard requires it; others are not. The instrument and governing rule determine the treatment.

India requires a naming discipline: the RBI **AFS-Reserve** used for commercial-bank investment valuation is a prescribed reserve mechanism. It should not automatically be relabelled “IFRS 9 OCI”.

### Flow

```text
Recognised gain / loss
    ↓
Applicable framework determines route
    ├─→ Profit or loss
    ├─→ OCI
    └─→ Other prescribed reserve / equity route
                 ↓
           Equity changes
```

### Permanent rule

Management preference cannot create OCI eligibility. Presentation follows the applicable accounting or regulatory framework.

### Never confuse [OCI] and [Any reserve outside P&L]

**OCI** — a defined financial-reporting category under frameworks that use it.

---

**Other reserve** — a separately prescribed equity route that may have different recognition and recycling mechanics.

### Permanent trap

Calling every valuation movement outside profit or loss “OCI”. That collapses distinct accounting and regulatory mechanisms into one label.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI's 2025 commercial-bank investment directions prescribe an **AFS-Reserve** for qualifying AFS valuation gains and losses rather than routing those movements through profit or loss. Debt-instrument amounts are transferred to profit or loss on sale or maturity; designated equity treatment differs.

#### UK
UK-adopted international accounting standards use the IFRS architecture of profit or loss and OCI for entities applying that framework.

#### EU
EU-adopted IFRS uses the same architecture for accounts within scope.

#### US
US GAAP contains comprehensive-income and accumulated-OCI requirements, but the qualifying items and reclassification rules come from US GAAP, not IFRS.
