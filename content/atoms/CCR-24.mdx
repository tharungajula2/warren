In detail, EU CRR2 already implemented SA-CCR (from Jan 2023) and amended CVA rules. CRR3 (Basel 3.1) further updates CVA capital (transition Jan 2025) and keeps SA-CCR as is. The UK follows the same schedule: the PRA’s policy says Basel 3.1 reforms (CVA and margining) will be in force by mid-2025. Both jurisdictions require qualifying CCP (QCCP) treatment for exposures (EU and UK lists). They also follow the same definitions of EE/EPE, WWR, etc, as Basel.

### Permanent rule

Use CRR or UK Prudential Regulation Authority rules for final figures in these jurisdictions. Generally, EU/UK text mirrors Basel with local choices (e.g. sovereign CVA exemptions in EU).

### Permanent trap

Forgetting conversion factors or treatment differences. For instance, EU CVA rules used to exempt certain sovereigns; UK may diverge post-Brexit.

### Never confuse

**CRR2/3 (EU)** — European regulations implementing Basel (CRR3 is latest, full Basel 3.1).
---
**UK PRA rules** — UK’s onshore regs (PRA policy statements for Basel 3.1).

### Jurisdiction variants

**UK** — PRA announced Basel 3.1 implementation by July 2025, including CCR and CVA changes (reducing SA-CCR alpha to 1.0 for non-financials, transitional treatment).
**EU** — CRR3 (Basel 3.1) adopted with application date Jan 1, 2025; CRR2 already moved banks to SA-CCR in 2023 and updated CVA.
