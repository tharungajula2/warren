### Core mechanism

| Principle | Question it answers | Failure example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 Accuracy and integrity | Are values produced reliably, with controlled manual intervention and appropriate reconciliation? | Unexplained spreadsheet adjustments change exposure totals |
| 4 Completeness | Are all material risks, entities, business lines and relevant dimensions captured? | A subsidiary or product is outside aggregation scope |
| 5 Timeliness | Is information available quickly enough for the risk and decision being managed? | Stress exposure arrives after the decision window |
| 6 Adaptability | Can the bank answer new or ad-hoc risk questions without rebuilding the data estate? | A supervisor asks for sector-by-country exposure and the bank cannot produce it promptly |

```text
Authoritative source populations
    ↓
Controlled transformations and reconciliation
    ↓
Complete material scope
    ↓
Refresh at decision-useful speed
    ↓
Flexible aggregation by required dimensions
```

Timeliness is contextual: a slowly changing structural metric and a fast-moving trading exposure can require different refresh speeds. Adaptability is likewise not limitless flexibility; the point is that common risk dimensions and detailed data are governed well enough to support credible new views.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Governance · Architecture · Source data · Definitions
This:       Accuracy · Completeness · Timeliness · Adaptability
Downstream: Internal risk reports · Stress response · Supervisory requests
```

### Never confuse [Completeness] and [Accuracy]

**Completeness** - the required material population and dimensions are present.

---

**Accuracy** - the values and transformations correctly represent the governed source and intended measure. A complete dataset can still be wrong.

### Permanent rule

Aggregation quality is fit-for-purpose across all four capabilities; excellence in one dimension does not cancel failure in another.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** The aggregation principles do not change by jurisdiction.
