### Core mechanism

| Layer | What moves it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Interest income | Asset balances · contractual/effective yields · repricing · collection |
| Interest expense | Deposit and borrowing balances · funding mix · repricing |
| Net interest income | Interest income minus interest expense |
| Pre-provision operating result | NII plus non-interest income minus operating costs |
| Profit | Further accounting gains/losses · credit-loss charges · tax and other items |

A bank can therefore grow NII while profit falls if expenses or credit-loss charges rise faster. Equally, a bank can report strong profit despite weak NII if non-interest income is unusually high.

### Formula

```text
NII = Interest income - Interest expense
NII = net interest income, a currency amount
Interest income = income recognised on interest-earning assets
Interest expense = cost recognised on interest-bearing funding

NIM = Net interest income / Average interest-earning assets
NIM = net interest margin, a ratio
Average interest-earning assets = stated denominator over the measurement period

Reporting conventions may annualise NII before calculating NIM; the denominator and period must therefore be read with the ratio.
```

### Flow

```text
Interest-earning assets → Interest income
Funding liabilities     → Interest expense
             ↓
       Net interest income
             ↓
+ Non-interest income
- Operating costs
- Credit-loss / other charges
             ↓
          Profit
```

### Permanent rule

Profit is broader than spread income. A bank's earnings must be read through both the quantity of business and the prices, costs, losses, and timing attached to it.

### Never confuse [Net interest income] and [Net interest margin]

**Net interest income** — an amount of income.

---

**Net interest margin** — a ratio scaling NII to average earning assets.

### Permanent trap

Calling the difference between a headline loan rate and a headline deposit rate “the bank's NIM”. Balances differ, funding is broader than deposits, instruments reprice at different times, and NIM has an asset denominator.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Presentation conventions differ, but the economic distinction between interest income, interest expense, NII, and a margin ratio is universal.
