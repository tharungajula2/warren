### Core mechanism

| Side | Meaning | More weight generally goes to |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ASF | stability value of capital and liabilities | capital, longer-term liabilities, more stable deposits |
| RSF | stable funding required by assets/OBS | longer-dated, less-liquid or encumbered assets |

ASF and RSF are prudential weights, not a one-year cash-flow ladder: legal maturity alone does not determine either side.

### Flow

```text
Liabilities + capital
    ↓ ASF factors
Available Stable Funding

Assets + specified OBS exposures
    ↓ RSF factors
Required Stable Funding

ASF / RSF
    ↓
Structural funding resilience
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   LCR · liquidity ladder
This:       One-year stable-funding structure
Downstream: Internal stress · funding strategy · FTP
```

### Formula

```text
NSFR = ASF / RSF

ASF ↑ → NSFR ↑.

RSF ↑ → NSFR ↓, all else equal.
```

### Permanent rule

LCR and NSFR solve different horizons: immediate stress coverage versus structural funding stability.

### Never confuse [NSFR] and [One-year liquidity gap]

**NSFR** — weighted stable funding available relative to weighted stable funding required.

---

**One-year liquidity gap** — projected cash inflows minus outflows over time; it is a cash-flow construct.

### Permanent trap

Calling NSFR a “one-year LCR”. It does not compare a liquid buffer with one-year net cash outflows.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI NSFR requirements for covered commercial banks have been in force since **1 October 2021**.

#### UK (PRA)
UK firms are subject to the applicable PRA stable-funding requirements within the UK prudential framework. Current liquidity rules are in force as at August 2026; separate 2026 PRA proposals concerning parts of the liquidity framework have a proposed 1 January 2027 implementation date and are not treated here as current rules.

#### EU
CRR Part Six contains the binding NSFR architecture; current consolidated CRR remains the applicable EU legal framework as at August 2026.

#### US
The interagency NSFR final rule became effective **1 July 2021** and is tailored by firm category, with full and reduced calibrations aligned broadly to the LCR tailoring architecture.
