At the **account level**, exposure commonly begins with the recorded amount currently outstanding. That is an accounting or operational quantity, not necessarily the maximum amount that could be lost.

At the **facility level**, exposure includes the credit position attributable to the contractual facility. A funded term loan may be dominated by the outstanding principal. A revolving line also has an undrawn commitment that could be drawn before default. Guarantees and other non-funded commitments may create contingent exposures even though no loan balance has yet appeared.

At the **obligor level**, exposure is an aggregation across relevant facilities to the same obligor. This is the level required for questions such as: "How much could be lost if this legal person fails?" The answer can differ materially from any single account balance.

At a **connected-group level**, exposures to legally separate obligors may be aggregated because control or economic interdependence makes their risks insufficiently independent. That issue is treated separately in CR-04.

At the **portfolio level**, exposure is aggregated across obligors and groups. The resulting total is useful for portfolio composition but cannot by itself reveal concentration or dependence. A portfolio of many equal independent exposures has a different tail-loss profile from the same total exposure concentrated in a few correlated names.

Regulatory definitions reinforce the need to specify measurement purpose. RBI's commercial-bank prudential directions define exposure broadly to include funded and non-funded exposures. Basel and US IRB frameworks separately define exposure at default as a risk parameter intended to capture the amount exposed when default occurs rather than merely today's balance.

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Every exposure number requires both a measurement basis and an aggregation level.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Using current drawn balance as "exposure" for a revolving or committed facility understates risk when additional drawings can occur before default.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse>
**Outstanding balance** — the amount currently recorded as drawn or owed.

**Exposure** — the credit amount relevant to a specified risk question; it may include contingent or future drawings and may be aggregated above account level.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** Detailed regulatory exposure-value rules differ, but exposure always requires an explicit measurement basis and aggregation level.
</Rule>

<Recall
  q="Why is an undrawn line relevant to exposure?"
  a="Because some of it may be drawn before the obligor defaults."
/>

<Recall
  q="Does portfolio exposure alone measure portfolio risk?"
  a="No. Concentration, default likelihood, severity and dependence also matter."
/>
