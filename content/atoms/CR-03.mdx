## The Expected Loss Formula

Expected Loss represents the baseline economic cost of lending operations. Unlike unexpected loss, expected loss is budgeted for through loan loss provisions.

<Formula math="EL = PD \times LGD \times EAD" />

<Rule label="EXPECTED LOSS PROVISIONING">
Institutions must establish reserves equal to Expected Loss for performing portfolio exposures under accounting standards.
</Rule>
