### Core mechanism

| Level | Question answered | What may be included |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Account | What is booked here now? | Outstanding principal, accrued items |
| Facility | What credit position belongs to this contract? | Drawn amount plus relevant undrawn or contingent exposure |
| Obligor | How much depends on this legal person? | Relevant facilities to the same obligor |
| Connected group | How much depends on one linked economic risk? | Exposures to connected counterparties |
| Portfolio | How much exposure exists across the book? | All included obligors/groups under the chosen perimeter |

Funded and non-funded positions can both create credit exposure. A commitment or guarantee may have little or no current loan balance while still creating future or contingent loss potential.

### Flow

```text
Account exposure → Facility exposure → Obligor exposure → Connected-group exposure → Portfolio exposure
    ↓
Limits · Concentration · EAD / EL / capital views
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Accounts · Limits · Commitments · Transactions
This:       Account → Facility → Obligor → Connected group → Portfolio
Downstream: Limits · EAD · Concentration · Expected loss · Capital
```

### Permanent rule

Every exposure number requires both a **measurement basis** and an **aggregation level**.

### Never confuse [Outstanding balance] and [Exposure]

**Outstanding balance** — the amount currently booked as drawn or owed.

---

**Exposure** — the amount relevant to a stated credit-risk question; it can include future drawings, contingent claims or aggregation above account level.

### Permanent trap

Setting exposure equal to current drawn balance for revolving or committed facilities can omit the amount that may be drawn or become due before default.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
The RBI Commercial Banks IRACP Directions define exposure to include funded and non-funded exposures, including underwriting and similar commitments.

#### UK (PRA)
Detailed regulatory exposure values depend on the applicable prudential approach; the conceptual distinction between booked balance and risk exposure does not change.

#### EU
CRR exposure-value rules vary by exposure type and credit-risk approach; the aggregation principle is unchanged.

#### US
US regulatory capital rules define exposure measures by asset and approach; current balance is not a universal substitute for exposure at default.
