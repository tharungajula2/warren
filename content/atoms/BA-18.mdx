### Core mechanism

Protect the mandatory outcome first; then choose among delivery levers without hiding their cost.

```text
Fixed date + Pressure -&gt; Reassess work -&gt; Protect mandatory outcome -&gt; Choose credible lever -&gt; Escalate residual risk
```

| Lever | Valid use | Boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| De-scope | Discretionary, separable work | Mandatory obligations stay mandatory |
| Re-sequence | Stages preserve the required end-state | Incomplete dependency is not a harmless phase |
| Add resources | Work is parallelisable and capacity arrives in time | Onboarding and coordination can consume the gain |
| Simplify | Simpler design still meets requirement and controls | Lower quality is not simplification |
| Temporary control | Exposure is understood, time-bounded and owned | Workaround is not the target state |
| Escalate | No credible plan meets date and obligation | Red status exposes the conflict; it does not solve it |
| Decision pack | Authority must choose | Show requirement, outcome, control, dependency, evidence gap, owner and decision date |

This makes consequences comparable and prevents "protect the date" from becoming permission to conceal unfinished mandatory work.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Fixed mandate · Current scope · Delivery status · Dependencies
This:       Scope-capacity-risk trade-off
Downstream: Change decisions · Test focus · Readiness · Residual-risk acceptance
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA frames scope and consequence options; project management supplies delivery capacity and schedule evidence |
| Approves | Sponsor and relevant business or risk authorities approve trade-offs within mandate |
| Consumes | Delivery, testing and operations execute the revised priorities |
| Independently challenges | Control functions challenge weakened controls, unsupported deferrals or residual risk |

### Permanent rule

A fixed deadline does not make all other constraints disappear. It forces earlier, clearer decisions about what is protected, what is deferred and who owns the resulting risk.

### Never confuse [Priority] and [Mandatory scope]

**Priority** - the order in which work should receive attention.

---

**Mandatory scope** - the minimum outcome that must be delivered for the governing obligation to be satisfied.

### Permanent trap

Adding people as the default recovery plan. Late capacity helps only where work can be separated, knowledge transfer is feasible and coordination cost does not consume the gain.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. The actual date or externally required evidence can vary by authority; the delivery discipline does not.
