## Capital Ratios and Conservation Buffer

Institutions must maintain minimum CET1 capital of 4.5%, Tier 1 capital of 6.0%, and Total Capital of 8.0% of RWA <Source id="1" />.

<Rule label="CAPITAL CONSERVATION BUFFER">
An additional 2.5% CET1 Capital Conservation Buffer applies, raising effective minimum CET1 requirement to 7.0%.
</Rule>
