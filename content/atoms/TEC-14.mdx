one_screen: >-
  In a data warehouse or risk data mart, tables are designed as *fact* or
  *dimension*. A **fact table** contains measures (numeric data) and foreign
  keys to dimensions. A **dimension table** contains descriptive attributes
  (e.g. borrower name, product category) and a primary key. Dimension tables
  (one side) are small and static (e.g. Time dimension, Product dimension). Fact
  tables (many side) are large and growing, storing transactions or snapshots
  (e.g. exposures, losses) with keys to link each record to its dimensions.

For example, a *Loan_Fact* table might have columns `(LoanKey, CustomerKey, ProductKey, Balance, InterestRate)`, where `CustomerKey` points to a *Customer_Dim* table with `(CustomerKey, Name, Segment, RiskRating)`, and `ProductKey` points to a *Product_Dim* table. This star schema lets analysts slice facts by any dimension attribute.

<Flow>
    +-------------+
    |  Loan_Fact  |
    +-------------+
    | CustomerKey |--&gt; +-----------+
    | ProductKey  |--&gt; |Customer_Dim|
    | Balance     |    +-----------+
    +-------------+      |   ...   |
                        +-----------+
</Flow>

<Rule label="PERMANENT RULE">
Fact tables should contain only keys (foreign keys) and measure columns. All descriptive attributes belong in dimensions, not the fact.
</Rule>

<Trap label="PERMANENT TRAP">
Placing text or descriptive fields in the fact table (denormalization) is discouraged. It bloats the fact and complicates queries.
</Trap>

<NeverConfuse a="Fact Table" b="Dimension Table">
**Fact Table** — contains numeric measurements and foreign keys linking to dimensions.
---
**Dimension Table** — contains attributes (descriptions) and a primary key.
</NeverConfuse>

<Rule label="NO DELTA">
**No delta.** The star schema concept of fact vs dimension is standard in DW design.
</Rule>

<Recall
  q="What determines the grain of a fact table?"
  a="The combination of all its foreign keys (e.g. one row per loan per month)."
/>

<Recall
  q="Where do borrower names go?"
  a="In a dimension table (e.g. `Customer_Dim`), not in the loan fact table."
/>
