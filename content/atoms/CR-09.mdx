### Core mechanism

| Evidence | Question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Repayment capacity deterioration | Can expected cash flow still service the obligation in full? |
| Distressed restructuring | Would the borrower fail or suffer material difficulty without a concession? |
| Credit-related write-down / sale loss | Does the bank’s own action evidence material deterioration? |
| Insolvency / bankruptcy event | Does legal distress indicate repayment will be avoided or delayed? |
| Security dependence | Is repayment only credible through enforcement or realisation of collateral? |

A watch-list flag, downgrade or adverse news item is an **input**, not automatically default. Documented triggers and evidence standards prevent UTP from drifting across teams or cycles.

### Flow

```text
Adverse evidence
    ↓
Assess sustainable full payment without extraordinary recovery action
    ↓
Full payment likely → No UTP → Continue monitoring
Full payment unlikely → UTP → Default
```

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Underwriting baseline · Monitoring · Adverse events · Financial information
This:       Evidence → Assess full payment without extraordinary action → UTP yes/no
Downstream: Default state · Collections/workout · PD labels · Provisioning/capital consequences
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | Relationship/credit monitoring identifies deterioration and evidence |
| Approves | Credit risk or delegated default governance determines UTP status |
| Consumes | Collections, restructuring, modelling, finance and regulatory reporting |
| Challenges | Independent risk, model validation and audit test consistency |

### Permanent rule

Assess UTP on the ability and likelihood to pay the credit obligation in full, not on how much the bank expects to recover after failure.

### Never confuse [Unlikely to pay] and [High LGD]

**Unlikely to pay** — a default-likelihood conclusion about full contractual payment.

---

**High LGD** — a severity conclusion about the size of loss conditional on default.

### Permanent trap

Calling a fully secured exposure “not defaulted” because collateral should cover the debt confuses borrower repayment with recovery from security.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN (RBI)
RBI commercial-bank NPA classification is its own prudential framework. Do not import a Basel IRB UTP label into RBI NPA reporting without an explicit mapping to the RBI rules in force.

#### UK (PRA)
The current UK framework includes unlikeliness-to-pay indicators. PRA SS3/24 provides the future Basel 3.1 expectations from 1 January 2027.

#### EU
UTP is an independent limb of CRR Article 178. The May 2026 EBA amendments apply from 19 October 2026 and should be treated as future-effective until then.

#### US
Advanced Approaches wholesale default expressly includes a determination that the obligor is unlikely to pay in full without recourse to actions such as realising collateral.
