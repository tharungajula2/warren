### Core mechanism

| Category family | Classification question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Agriculture | Is the borrower/activity within the permitted farm, allied, infrastructure or ancillary definitions? |
| MSME | Does the enterprise meet the applicable MSME classification and is the credit eligible under PSL rules? |
| Export credit | Is the exposure within the qualifying export-credit perimeter? |
| Education | Does the loan satisfy the permitted education purpose and current conditions? |
| Housing | Does the loan fit the qualifying housing purpose and applicable limits? |
| Social infrastructure | Is the financed activity within the defined eligible infrastructure class? |
| Renewable energy | Does the project or borrower fall within the prescribed renewable-energy category? |
| Weaker sections | Does the borrower or exposure meet one of the specifically designated weaker-section conditions? |

Classification is exposure-specific. A borrower can have one loan that qualifies and another that does not. The current Directions also distinguish direct lending, permitted on-lending and other structures, so the economic purpose alone is not enough.

Every numerical eligibility threshold in the legacy pack is treated as unverified unless established from the current 2025 Directions as amended. This atom therefore teaches the classification logic without repeating stale rupee caps.

### Permanent rule

PSL status follows the current RBI definition of the exposure, not the lender's internal product name or marketing label.

### Permanent trap

Assuming "MSME borrower" automatically means every loan to that borrower is PSL. Purpose, product structure and current classification conditions still matter.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### Commercial banks, SFBs and RRBs
The same category definitions can feed different entity-specific target and sub-target structures.

#### Cooperative banks
Apply the category definitions together with the cooperative-bank PSL framework applicable to that entity.
