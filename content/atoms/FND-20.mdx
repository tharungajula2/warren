### Core mechanism

| Layer | Question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Capacity | What is the maximum risk the institution can bear before binding constraints or viability are threatened? |
| Appetite | What risk is it willing to assume in pursuing objectives? |
| Limits | What measurable or enforceable boundaries convert appetite into decisions? |
| Risk profile | What risk is actually being taken now? |
| Escalation | What happens when profile approaches or breaches limits? |

Capacity should bound appetite. Appetite should drive limits. Limits should influence decisions. Monitoring should compare the resulting profile with the approved boundaries.

### Flow

```text
Risk capacity
    ↓
Risk appetite
    ↓
Risk limits
    ↓
Business decisions / exposures
    ↓
Actual risk profile
    ↓
Monitor → Escalate → Remediate / decide
```

### Ownership

| Role | Produces / owns |
| :--- | :--- |
| Governing body / board | Approves appetite and oversees alignment with strategy and capacity |
| Senior management | Implements appetite and allocates limits |
| Business management | Operates within limits and owns resulting risk |
| Risk function | Framework, aggregation, monitoring, challenge, escalation |
| Internal audit | Independent assurance over governance and controls |

### Permanent rule

Appetite must sit within capacity, and appetite that cannot be translated into decisions, limits, or escalation is not operational.

### Never confuse [Risk capacity] and [Risk appetite]

**Risk capacity** — maximum bearable risk subject to binding constraints.

---

**Risk appetite** — chosen risk-taking within that capacity.

### Never confuse [Risk appetite] and [Risk profile]

**Risk appetite** — desired boundary.

---

**Risk profile** — actual risk being run.

### Permanent trap

Raising a limit after a breach solely to make the breach disappear. A limit change is itself a governed risk decision and must be justified as such.

### Jurisdiction variants

**No delta.** Supervisory expectations differ in detail, but the capacity–appetite–limits–profile architecture is cross-jurisdictional.
