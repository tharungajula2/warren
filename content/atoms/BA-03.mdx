### Core mechanism

Map people by mandate, not title, visibility or meeting attendance.

| Role | Owns or supplies |
| :--- | :--- |
| Need owner | Required business outcome |
| SME | Process, rule or system knowledge; no automatic approval right |
| Requirement owner | Meaning of the business obligation |
| Decision authority | Choice between legitimate competing interests |
| Approver | Formal approval within a defined mandate |
| Acceptance authority | Fitness for business use and disclosed limitations |
| Go/no-go authority | Permission to start production transition |
| Residual-risk owner | Acceptance and monitoring of known post-release exposure |
| Independent challenger | Challenge to risk or control weakness without first-line ownership |

One person may hold several roles, but name each role separately because authority changes with the decision. Low attendance can coexist with veto authority; high visibility can coexist with none. Where policy owner and operator disagree, preserve both inputs until policy, practice and authority are reconciled. A reporting head may own need, requirement and acceptance without becoming architect or test owner.

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Produces | BA drafts the role and decision-right map |
| Approves | Sponsor or programme governance confirms decision authorities |
| Consumes | All workstreams use it to route questions, approvals and escalations |
| Independently challenges | Control functions challenge omissions where their mandate is affected |

### Permanent rule

Presence in a meeting is not authority. Authority comes from the role's organisational mandate and must be explicit before a material decision is treated as settled.

### Never confuse [SME] and [Decision authority]

**SME** - provides deep knowledge about a process, rule, system or control.

---

**Decision authority** - has the mandate to choose when options or stakeholder positions conflict.

### Permanent trap

Writing "business to confirm" as an owner. "Business" is a population, not an accountable person or role; unresolved collective ownership becomes delayed sign-off and disputed decisions.
