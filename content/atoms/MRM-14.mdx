### Core mechanism

| Evidence area | Developer question |
| :--- | :--- |
| Data | Are quality, selection and transformations tested? |
| Logic | Does implementation match the specification? |
| Sensitivity | Do outputs move plausibly when important inputs or assumptions move? |
| Robustness | Does performance depend excessively on one sample or choice? |
| Generalisation | Does evidence hold outside the fitting sample where appropriate? |
| Alternatives | Were reasonable competing assumptions or methods considered? |
| Edge cases | What happens under unusual, missing or extreme inputs? |
| Limitations | What known weakness remains after development? |

```text
Build
    ↓
Test
    ↓
Diagnose failure
    ↓
Revise or document limitation
    ↓
Freeze candidate version
    ↓
Submit evidence for independent validation
```

Out-of-sample and out-of-time tests may be useful, but this atom does not teach their statistics. The point is governance: development evidence must be relevant to the model's stated purpose and difficult enough to reveal failure.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Purpose · Data · Method · Implementation
This:       First-line developmental evidence
Downstream: Independent validation · Findings · Approval
```

### Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Developer | Designs, executes and documents testing |
| Model owner | Confirms evidence pack is complete |
| Business user | Provides practical challenge and unusual-use cases |
| Validation | Reviews and independently extends the testing where needed |

### Permanent rule

Developer testing is necessary evidence, but it is not independent validation. The person proving the model was built carefully is not the final challenger of that proof.

### Never confuse [Developer testing] and [Independent validation]

**Developer testing** - first-line testing used to design, debug and evidence the candidate model.

---

**Independent validation** - objective challenge of the model and the sufficiency of the developer's evidence, supplemented by independent analysis where warranted.

### Permanent trap

Removing failed tests from the final pack. A documented failure and reasoned remediation is evidence of control; a polished record that hides development weaknesses deprives validators of the most informative evidence.

### Jurisdiction variants

No delta. The 2026 US guidance expressly recognises model testing as a core development activity and scales rigor to complexity and materiality; the underlying first-line versus independent-challenge distinction is universal.
