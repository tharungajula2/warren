### Core mechanism

```text
Management target / headroom
    ↓ loss
Regulatory buffer entered
    ↓
Distribution constraints begin or intensify
    ↓ further loss
Buffer depleted
    ↓
Capital requirements approached
    ↓
Supervisory intervention escalates
```

Management headroom is voluntary capital held above the regulatory stack to protect against forecast error, volatility, strategy execution and market expectations. It is not itself a regulatory buffer.

Under the Basel conservation framework, the deeper capital falls into the conservation-buffer range, the greater the proportion of earnings that must be retained. EU and ordinary UK terminology commonly uses maximum distributable amount mechanics for specified distributions when the combined buffer is not met. The US uses a different stress-capital-buffer structure.

### Where it sits

```text
Upstream:   Buffer stack · risk appetite
This:       Buffer usability and distribution consequences
Downstream: Capital actions · recovery planning · stress management
```

### Permanent rule

Entering a buffer is less severe than breaching a minimum, but it is not the same as having unrestricted excess capital.

### Never confuse [Management headroom] and [Regulatory buffer]

**Management headroom** — internally chosen margin above the regulatory stack.

---

**Regulatory buffer** — rule-based CET1 cushion with defined prudential consequences when used.

### Never confuse [MDA] and [Capital stock]

**MDA** — maximum amount permitted for specified distributions under the applicable regime.

---

**Capital stock** — amount of qualifying regulatory capital held by the bank.

### Permanent trap

Treating the buffer as unusable because distributions are constrained defeats its purpose; treating it as free surplus ignores the conservation mechanism.

### Jurisdiction variants

#### IN
RBI applies capital-conservation restrictions as CET1 moves through prescribed buffer ranges.

#### UK / EU
MDA-style restrictions apply under their respective ordinary combined-buffer frameworks; exact calculations are jurisdictional.

#### US
Stress capital buffer rules produce a different large-bank distribution and capital architecture.
